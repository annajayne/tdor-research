> Life Imprisonment for Three in Transgender Murder Case
> Apr 22 2026 12:04 PM | Updated on Apr 22 2026 12:50 PM
> 
> Transgender Murder Case
> Konaseema District: On Tuesday, the Court of the 10th Additional District Judge in Rajamahendravaram sentenced three individuals to life imprisonment in connection with the murder of a transgender person—a case that had caused a sensation three years ago at Jonnada on National Highway 216A. Alamuru police briefed the media about the case at the Amalapuram SP office.
> 
> Marumatla Anand (alias Anandi), a transgender person hailing from Upparagudem in Tadepalligudem Mandal, West Godavari District, was residing with fellow transgender individuals in Dowleswaram at the time.
>
> On the night of May 12, 2023, Anandi was brutally murdered in an irrigation canal beside the Jonnada highway. Following a complaint lodged by Dhanala Mandakini—another transgender person from Dowleswaram—the then Sub-Inspector (SI) S. Siva Prasad registered a case regarding this sensational murder. Ravulapalem Rural Circle Inspector (CI) N. Rajani Kumar conducted the investigation, while Kothapeta Deputy Superintendent of Police (DSP) K.V. Ramana supervised the proceedings.
>
> Following the conviction of the accused—Ketha Venkata Bharat Sudhir alias Pedda (Dhavaleswaram), Velu Kalyan alias Peda Kali (Bommmuru), and Singampalli Karthikeya alias Simba (Dhavaleswaram), all hailing from East Godavari district—Judge S. Uma Sunanda sentenced them to life imprisonment and imposed a fine of ₹15,000 each.
>
> Public Prosecutors K. Radhakrishna Raju and D. Vanibhai presented arguments on behalf of the victims. Eluru Range IG G.V.V. Ashok Kumar and SP Rahul Meena commended the officers who cracked the case: Kothapeta DSP Sunkara Murali Mohan, Ravulapalem Rural CI Ch. Vidyasagar, and Alamuru SI G. Naresh.
