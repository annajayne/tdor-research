> Honoring the life of Kayonna Ankeny, a Black trans woman, shot to death in Georgia three years ago.
> June 29, 2026 by Sue
> 
> In February 2023. the body of a Black trans woman was discovered in an abandoned building in Augusta, Georgia. While she was identified, her life and death did not come to our attention until now. 
> 
> Her name was Kayonna Ankeny.
> 
> The Crime
> On Sunday, February 5, 2023, Richmond County deputies were called to vacant building on the 3400 block of Meadowbrook Drive about a deceased person. The victim was reportedly shot at least one time.
> 
> The abandoned building is boarded up at the end of the Meadowgrove subdivision on Langdon Drive. Faded signs on the build at the time referenced a laundromat and dry cleaners, according to the Augusta Press.
> 
> Reports indicate that she had been in the building several days before police discovered her body.
> 
> As of today, there is no information on a suspect in the case.
> 
> Honoring Kayonna
> Kayonna was born and raised in Augusta, Georgia. Kayonna was described as “a fun loving kind person that loved life … a good person.”
> 
> Kayonna’s socials were filled with comedic reels, images of the environment, and the sort of “inside joke” you share with close friends. She made many fun videos with her nieces and nephews, filled with laughter.
> 
> Tragically, Kayonna is the third of four adult siblings to die a premature death.  Thirteen years ago, her older brother was murdered, and recently her sister died from lupus.
> 
> Kayonna’s cousin shared “no matter what we went through. I could still call her for advice, a ride, a lone pick-up, or drop-off.”
> 
> Friends organized a vigil a few days after she was found. Family announced plans for a Celebration of Life at a future date.
