> They revealed where the wake will be held for the trans woman from Salta who brought grief to the Lerma Valley.
> A transgender person died after being detained at the Rosario de Lerma police station. The courts are investigating the circumstances of the incident.
> They revealed where the wake will be held for the trans woman from Salta who brought grief to the Lerma Valley.
> 
> Rosario de Lerma is in shock after the death of Daniela Fernanda Arias, a transgender person who had been detained Wednesday night at the local police station, was confirmed. Police sources consulted by Que Pasa Salta on condition of anonymity assured that the incident has already prompted an administrative and judicial investigation
> 
> According to the preliminary version of the Police, Daniela had been detained for a misdemeanor after an intervention in Plaza Evita , where - according to the official account - she was consuming alcohol along with three other people, in a space where children usually play.
> 
> When the officers tried to identify them, the group allegedly reacted violently and Daniela refused to provide her information, which is why she was taken to the police station to complete the identification procedure contemplated by the Contravention Code.
> 
> Before being placed in the cell, she was examined by a forensic doctor, who confirmed a high level of alcohol intoxication . Once inside, and according to the reported account, Daniela allegedly attempted suicide with an article of clothing. Police officers reportedly noticed the situation and performed CPR while calling for medical assistance.
> 
> An ambulance arrived a few minutes later and took her to the local hospital, where she ultimately died despite resuscitation attempts. Official sources indicated that she had been detained for less than an hour and was about to be released.
> 
> The case was turned over to the prosecutor's office, which ordered multiple measures: review of internal records, analysis of security camera footage, interviews with on-duty personnel, and forensic examinations. The autopsy results are pending and are key to accurately determining the cause of death.
> 
> The wake for Daniela Fernanda Arias is being held in the municipal hall of the local cemetery, Sagrado Corazón . The burial will take place this Friday, November 28th, at 9:30 a.m.




> Rosario de Lerma: The death of a trans woman after being detained at a police station is under investigation
> The Femicide Unit is investigating the death of a 34-year-old trans woman that occurred after she was detained in a police station in Rosario de Lerma.
> 
> By Critical Voices Editorial Staff
> Thursday, November 27, 2025
> 
> SALTA (Voces Críticas Newsroom) The Femicide Prosecutor's Office has opened an investigation into the death of a 34-year-old transgender woman, which occurred last night at the Rosario de Lerma hospital. The victim had been detained minutes earlier at a local police station, and prosecutors are seeking to determine whether the custody protocol was followed.
> 
> The young woman's death occurred after a police operation in Plaza Evita, where officers intervened after observing four people consuming alcoholic beverages. Three left the scene without incident, while the transgender woman was detained for refusing to identify herself. After a routine medical examination, she was taken to the Second Police Station to complete the identification process.
> 
> According to the prosecutors' preliminary report, when police officers went to the cell to inform her that she would be released, the woman allegedly attempted suicide using an article of clothing. At that moment, the emergency protocol was activated, and resuscitation efforts were performed while an ambulance was requested, amidst a tense atmosphere due to the severity of her condition.
> 
> The victim arrived at the Rosario de Lerma hospital around 9:48 p.m., where the medical team tried to stabilize her; however, minutes later her death was confirmed, which opened an urgent investigation to reconstruct each step of the procedure.
> 
> The UFEM (Special Prosecutor's Office for Violence Against Women) has already taken steps to determine whether the arrest, medical examination, and cell supervision were carried out in accordance with current regulations. They will also review security camera footage, statements, and internal protocols to establish responsibility and fully clarify what happened. Reported by  Voces Críticas.
