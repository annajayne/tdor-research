> Death Details:
> Thiago Elar, an Italian TikTok creator with a community of around 140,000-150,000 followers, passed away on 21 July 2025 at the age of 27. He was originally from Bergamo and lived in Osio Sotto. Thiago was openly transgender and had used social media to share his lived experience with severe eating disorders and mental health struggles while hospitalized in psychiatric care. His final posts included reflections on his condition and calls for understanding and help from his audience. The cause of death was reported as natural and related to complications from his prolonged health condition
