> SOLIDARITY: Family asks for help to bring back the body of a woman from Três Pontas who died in Europe.
> Put Positive Team- January 1, 2026160
> 
> The family of Myrella Montini, a transgender woman originally from Três Pontas, is experiencing profound grief and facing significant difficulties in repatriating her body after her death abroad. Myrella had lived in Santander, Spain, for approximately 10 years, where she built her life far from Brazil.
> 
> In December, during a trip to Denmark, Myrella fell ill during one of the excursions and suffered a massive heart attack. She was hospitalized, but unfortunately did not survive and passed away on December 19th.
> 
> Myrella was the daughter of Zezé Delcídio, a plumber, and Nazaré, and sister of Maquisuel (plumber), Maurão (plumber), and Márcia Oliveira, a well-known family in Três Pontas. In addition to the pain of the loss, the family is now facing an extremely delicate financial and bureaucratic situation.
> 
> According to the family, it is estimated that it will take between 15 and 30 days for the body to be brought back to Brazil. The costs of international transport are high, and the family does not have the financial means to cover the expenses.
> 
> Although Myrella has assets and financial resources, the family does not have access to these funds at this time, as they are dependent on legal procedures abroad. Even so, the family is being charged for the costs of the repatriation, which further increases their suffering during this period of mourning.
> 
> Given the situation, the family is appealing for help and solidarity, seeking support to bring Myrella's body back to Brazil and ensure a dignified burial in her hometown, allowing her to rest close to her loved ones.
