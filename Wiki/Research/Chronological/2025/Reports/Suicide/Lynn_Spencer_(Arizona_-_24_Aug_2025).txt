> Lynn Spencer. Age 21. August 24th 2025. Arizona USA. Suicide.
>
> Lynn was a caring and loving person, and having her gone has made everyday without her felt as if something is always missing now. Lynn lived in Arizona with her grandparents who she was not open to, but often voiced their disapproval of the trans community. Often, Lynn spent time with her best friend Ryan and their related groups. She was active in online communities and forum pages, and left an impact on everyone that she met. She had dreams of moving away with her friends, traveling by motor bike to different locations and settling in a house full of cats one day. I wish to have her simply be remembered, and to be known as Lynn, as a final gift to give her.
