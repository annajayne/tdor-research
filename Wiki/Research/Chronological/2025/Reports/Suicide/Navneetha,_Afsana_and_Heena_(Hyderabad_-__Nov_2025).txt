> Hyderabad: Third Transgender person dies after self-immolation protest in Borabanda
> A third transgender person, Navneetha, died at Gandhi Hospital after suffering severe burns during a protest in Borabanda last week. Eight people had set themselves on fire demanding action against Monalisa, who was later arrested. Two others died earlier
> By Telangana Today
> Published Date - 25 November 2025, 03:30 PM
> 
> Hyderabad: A transgender person who had allegedly self-immolated during a protest at Borabanda on Monday last week has succumbed while undergoing treatment at Gandhi Hospital on Tuesday morning. With this, the toll in the suicide case rose to three.
> 
> The transgender person identified as Navneetha (24), a resident of Borabanda, along with a group of transgender persons, held a protest at Borabanda bus stand demanding action against Monalisa, a transgender person who is a leader of another group.
> 
> During the protest, a group of transgender people had doused themselves with petrol and set themselves ablaze. Eight of them had suffered burns and were shifted to Gandhi Hospital for treatment.
> 
> Earlier, two transgender persons, Afsana and Heena, who were admitted to the hospital, died on November 20 and 23.
> 
> Following a complaint, the Borabanda police had earlier arrested a transgender Monalisa last Wednesday and remanded her in judicial custody.
