> Road tragedy in Bucaramanga: trans woman died after being hit by a motorcycle
> Those close to the victim are demanding a thorough investigation into the accident. See what is known so far.
> Giselle Camila Ramos was run over by a motorcycle at the intersection of Diagonal 15 and Calle 53 in Bucaramanga.
> 
> Giselle Camila Ramos, a 33-year-old trans woman, died in a tragic traffic accident after being hit by a motorcycle in Bucaramanga.
> 
> The incident, which his relatives are asking to be thoroughly investigated, occurred around 10:00 p.m. on Monday, December 29, at the intersection of Diagonal 15 and Calle 53, when the victim was crossing the road and was hit by a motorcycle with license plate DYJ-17G, driven by Jonathan Alexander Peralta Pérez, a Venezuelan national.
> 
> As a result of the impact, Giselle Camila was left lying on the road, while the motorcycle ended up several meters from the point of collision. Despite the rapid arrival of emergency services, the woman died due to the severity of her injuries.
> 
> Mariela Morales, also of Venezuelan nationality, was riding as a passenger in the vehicle. She was injured and taken to a medical center.
> 
> People close to the victim indicated that Giselle had already suffered a traffic accident weeks earlier, a situation that has generated doubts and concern among her family, so they are requesting that the case be thoroughly investigated.
> 
> The deceased worked as a webcam model, used to travel around the area near the accident site, and was appreciated by those who knew her within the LGBTIQ+ community for her charisma and joy.
> 
> Organizations and spokespeople for the diverse population called on the authorities to treat the case with a differential approach and respect.
> 
> “The first thing we wanted to find out is if there is a larger situation. When it involves a trans person, these types of incidents generate a double violence: first, because they are not recognized as such, and second, because of the morbid fascination surrounding how the events occurred, which ends in a second revictimization,” Diego Ruíz, director of the Conpazes Corporation, told Vanguardia .
> 
> Units from the Bucaramanga Transit Authority carried out the technical inspection of the body and are continuing with the investigations to clarify the circumstances of the accident and determine responsibilities.
> 
> With this case, the number of people who have lost their lives in road accidents this year in Bucaramanga has reached 82, and more than 150 in the entire metropolitan area, according to traffic authorities.
