> A transgender woman, known as "Soraya Montenegro," 34, who fell from the balcony of her apartment on October 4th in the Chapada neighborhood, died in the hospital in the early hours of Wednesday (22). 
> 
> According to the IML report, Soraya suffered severe head trauma and did not survive the injury. The victim's fall occurred in Beco Eduardo, during a drinking session between her, a friend, and another woman, according to a friend of Soraya's. 


> The trans woman identified as Soraya Kendall, has been hospitalized in serious condition since the early hours of this Saturday, 4, when she fell from the balcony of the apartment where she lives, on the 1st floor of the building located on Rua Eduardo Ribeiro, Chapada neighborhood, South-Central Zone of Manaus.
> 
> The police are investigating the case and there is already information that Kendall was attending a party where she gathered a few friends, including a young man who is his fellow countryman in the municipality of Tabatinga, in the interior of Amazonas.
> 
> One of the people who was in the apartment, a friend of Soraya, said that the fall happened around 3 am, but the fellow countryman had already left, along with other people.
