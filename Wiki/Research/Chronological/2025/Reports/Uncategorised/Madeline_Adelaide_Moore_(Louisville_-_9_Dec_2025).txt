> Madeline was a good friend of mine online. we talked often of table top role playing games, fantasy adventures, music, etc etc. the impact she had on me and multiple of my friends lives could never be understated. she was a star in our eyes. one of the most intelligent, kind, compassionate, and all around amazing people i have ever had the pleasure of speaking to. i will never forget her.




> Madeline was the magician for my daughter's third birthday party. She loved kids, and made magic for the little kids with her tricks, bubbles, and jokes. We had so much fun, and Jocelyn and I were so happy for our kids to be besties. She was a bright creative spark who will be missed and loved forever.


> Madeline Adelaide Moore left this world on December 9th, 2025, in Louisville, KY. She was 27 years old. Madeline was born in Louisville on August 4th, 1998.
> 
> She is survived by her sibling, Jonah Karman, her mother Jocelyn, her uncles Kevin and Colin, her father Michael Karman, her grandparents Gerald and Beverly, as well as her half-siblings Tasha Karman and Zachary Karman and her nephew Levi Karman and her step-mother Jennifer Karman.
> 
> Madeline is also mourned by a variety of loving friends : Gaming groups, young queers, online communities, and co-workers from Louisville Free Public Library.
> 
> In her time here, Madeline was a creative force, found meaning and delight in art, writing, world building, queer community, culture and history, video games, and tabletop games. She was a talented Game Master, an obsessive and dazzling narrative artist, and a dedicated, sweet friend. She brought joy to those around her with her brilliance, wit, sassiness, and empathy.
> 
> A memorial service for Mads will be held on Sunday, December 21st at 3:00 p.m. at First Unitarian Church at 809 S. 4th St. in Louisville.


> LOUISVILLE, Ky. (WDRB) -- The Louisville Metro Police Department's Missing Person Unit is looking for a 27-year-old woman last seen in Old Louisville earlier this week. 
> 
> According to a news release from LMPD, Madeline Moore — a biological male who identifies as female — was last seen Dec. 8 when she left her home in the 1100 block of South 1st Street on foot. Moore is white, 6 feet, 1 inch tall and weighs approximately 140 pounds. She has brown hair and brown eyes. 
> 
> When last seen, Moore was wearing a gray cardigan and pajama pants. She hasn't communicated with family members, who believe she could be in need of medical attention.
