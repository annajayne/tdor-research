> Nora Jane Jenneman passed away on November 21, 2025, at age 25.
> 
> Born February 9, 2000, in Grand Rapids, Michigan, Nora was such a loving person: kind, beautiful, easy, with a passion for nature, art, sports and a terrific sense of humor. She was incredibly affectionate, good-natured and also developed a fierce competitiveness as an athlete.
> 
> She was an exceptional ice hockey player and gave us many thrills watching her play over the years. Living in Salt Lake City the past few years, Nora began to ski more frequently and quickly found a new passion as a member of the Westminster University Freeski team. She loved her teammates and spending time in the mountains training. She was so happy to find a home and family at Westminster where she was pursuing a career with a purpose to care for, guide and support others, which she had already begun to excel in as part of her daily life.
> 
> Throughout her life, Nora appreciated many different types of music and art. Her drawings are intricate, unique, thought-provoking and sometimes very intense. But always meaningful art.
> 
> Nora was brave in every way. Not only as a fearless athlete, but even more so in how she never missed an opportunity to stand up for herself or her beliefs, even when the consequences of doing so were certain.
> 
> Her unique style and creative mind attracted people from all walks of life. She impacted so many people and taught us an immeasurable amount about acceptance, love, behavioral skills and self-awareness. Nora filled our hearts with so much, and we pray that we will continue to feel her positive energy and spirit throughout our lives, and that she will continue to feel our everlasting love for her.
> 
> Nora is survived by her parents Robert and Stephanie Jenneman - Grand Rapids, Michigan and her brother Joe Jenneman, Nashville, TN along with many cousins, aunts, uncles, grandparents and very loving friends she made on her short but memorable and meaningful journey here.
> 
> Visitation and Service will be at the Metcalf & Jonkhoff Funeral Home in Grand Rapids, Michigan on Saturday, January 31, 2026. The Visitation will begin at 1:00pm with a Service beginning at 3:00pm. All are welcome to join.
> 
> In lieu of flowers, please consider making a donation to the scholarship fund established in Nora's honor: https://givebutter.com/A5G68C.





> Nora Jenneman Scholarship Fund
> Introducing the Nora Jenneman Scholarship Fund
> We are honored to announce the creation of the Nora Jenneman Scholarship Fund, established in loving memory of Nora Jenneman, one of our deeply cherished Life Coaches whose compassion, authenticity, and unwavering commitment to others left an indelible mark on our community.
> 
> Nora had a rare gift: she met people exactly where they were and helped them believe that a life worth living was not only possible, but within reach. She brought empathy, courage, and fierce advocacy into every space she entered—especially for those who too often feel unseen or misunderstood.
> 
> In that same spirit, the Nora Jenneman Scholarship Fund has been created to support our transgender patients, helping remove financial barriers to care and ensuring access to affirming, life-saving treatment. Every donation directly supports individuals on their journey toward safety, healing, and hope.
> 
> This fund is more than a scholarship—it is a continuation of Nora’s legacy. It reflects her belief in dignity, inclusion, and the transformative power of feeling truly accepted. Through this fund, Nora’s impact will live on in the lives she would have championed most.
> 
> We invite you to honor Nora’s memory by contributing to the Nora Jenneman Scholarship Fund. Together, we can help ensure that every patient - especially those navigating the challenges of being transgender—has the opportunity to find a life worth living.
