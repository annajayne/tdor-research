> HER NAME WAS SHAROL VICTORIA
>
> Justice, memory and solidarity for our trans sister
>
> Good afternoon to all, each and every one.
>
> With deep pain and dismay, we inform the community of unfortunate news that grips our lives and the trans community in Bogota.
> 
> On Sunday, December 7, 2025, in the morning hours, our colleague SHAROL VICTORIA, trans woman, was run over in the Las Ferias sector (Bogota).
> 
> The vehicle involved allegedly fled, leaving it without assistance.
> 
> Currently, her body is in forensic medicine.
> 
> A close sister performed the reconnaissance to avoid being buried as NN, a minimum act of dignity in the face of a reality that should not be repeated.
>
> Sharol Victoria's family was already contacted in Venezuela, but unfortunately she does not have the necessary financial resources to travel to Colombia and claim her body.
