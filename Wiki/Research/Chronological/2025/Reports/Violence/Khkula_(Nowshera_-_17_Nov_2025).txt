> Transgender Person and Companion Shot Dead in Nowshera
> Officials said investigation into the double murder is underway, with mobile phone data of the victims and other evidence being collected to trace the perpetrators.
> By TNN - 17 Nov, 2025
>
>  A transgender person and his companion were shot dead in the suburban area of Benazir Garhi in Pabbi, Nowshera. According to police, both victims were found riddled with bullets.
> 
> Police said the slain transgender person, Salman alias Khkula, had been taken away the previous night by Khayal Muhammad. 
> 
> Khkula’s father, Noor Rehman, told police that his son had left home accompanied by the now-deceased Khayal Muhammad.
> 
> Officials said investigation into the double murder is underway, with mobile phone data of the victims and other evidence being collected to trace the perpetrators.
> 
> The bodies have been shifted to Mian Rashid Memorial Hospital in Pabbi for post-mortem, while Rescue 1122 has completed its operation.
