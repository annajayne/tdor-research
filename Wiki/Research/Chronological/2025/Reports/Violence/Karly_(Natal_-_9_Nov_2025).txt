> It is with immense sadness and regret that we inform you of the passing of our beloved Karly, who lived in the Nazaré neighborhood near the Judith Bezerra de Melo school. 
>
> We have received information that her wake and burial took place yesterday (November 9th). 
>
> Karly was assisted by our food basket distribution program during the COVID-19 pandemic. She was a hairdresser, a follower of Candomblé, and over 60. She helped many transgender teenagers and young adults by answering their questions and finding work in her salon. 
>
> Here is our tribute to this trans woman who did so much for her community!
