> Cruel end for warrior
> 
> Condolences are pouring in for Hallee Laban’s family after her alleged brutal death.
> 
> THE Middelpos community in the Northern Cape is struggling to recover from the death of one of the town’s beloved LGBTQIA activists and councillors known as Hallee Laban.
> 
> So much so that it has been a struggle to get people talking about Laban’s alleged “cruel” end.
> 
> According to rumours currently doing the rounds, Laban was attacked with an iron pole on Friday, September 5.
> 
> She was then admitted to hospital and was discharged on Saturday. During the course of Saturday while she lay sleeping, the same suspect who attacked her allegedly set her Wendy house on fire.
> 
> Laban was allegedly burned so badly that her family is waiting for DNA results to establish her identity and the forensic examination to be completed before they can make funeral arrangements.
> 
> Not only was Laban a councillor in the region, she was also the chairperson of the policing forum, a community campaigner and advocating for working with the youth to upskill them.
> 
> Laban also made history by becoming the first LGBTQIA councillor in the Hantam region.
> 
> Lucinda Evans, a well-known campaigner, expressed her dismay at the incident.
> 
> “It is shocking that on the night Hallee was so indecently assaulted, there were no safehouses for her, for someone who stood for everything that is right and just.
> 
> “There are no spaces for survivors of gender-based violence in the Northern Cape. They should just go back home because there is a lack of emergency safehouses.”
> 
> Evans says the questions that need to be asked of the police are why a J88 form was not completed after Hallee’s assault and why an arrest of the suspect did not follow.
> 
> “There needs to be urgent intervention regarding GBV intervention in the Northern Cape. The issue of GBV in the province is inhumane.”
> 
> Evans says Hallee is determined to represent every survivor of GBV for whom there has never been an emergency safehouse as a shelter.
> 
> Bertie Saal, ANC spokesperson in the Namakwa region, says they regret the sudden death of councillor Gerrit “Hallee” Laban.
> 
> Laban was officially sworn in in January this year and was responsible for GBV on the council. Saal describes Laban as a sparkling person who was always willing to roll up her sleeves and work.
> 
> “Her involvement and dedication as chairperson of the GPF speaks volumes, and her seat as chairperson is now vacant. Comrade Laban was a full-fledged activist for her people and fulfilled various community roles, including as the local library assistant.”
> 
> Saal says Laban will be remembered for her humor and how open she was as a person. Also for the fact that she made history in the municipality as the first LGBTQIA councilor.
> 
> Regarding the allegations about how Laban met her end, Saal says they cannot say much about it until it is confirmed by the police investigation.
> 
> He says, however, that the ANC is opposed to all forms of GBV and does not support violence against the LGBTQIA community in any form.
> 
> “We now intend to launch a GBV program so that we can combat the evil and also create awareness about acceptance especially in smaller towns. The program will be in memory of Hallee.”
> 
> Gita Marshant November, an activist at Gender Dynamix and LGBTQIA campaigner, expressed her sadness over the incident on Facebook.
> 
> “Society has never been kind to trans bodies, but to lose yourself at the hands of such abhorrent hatred is more than heartbreaking, it makes you furious. Your light was stolen far too soon.”
> 
> Captain Ivan Magerman, a police spokesperson, confirmed that an inquest had been opened following the discovery of a 36-year-old man (Laban) in a house where a fire had broken out. The incident took place on 6 September at around 18:40.
> 
> “It is alleged that the house in which the deceased was found caught fire. The person subsequently died at the scene in Agter Street, Middelpos. The cause of the fire is part of the investigation.”




> The ANC in Namakwa yesterday paid last respect to the late  Councillor Gerrit “Hallee” Laban. A dignified celebration of his life was witnessed by a massive congegration, attended by the Regional Chairman Mervin Cloete, Deputy Chairperson Alinda Beukes, Secretary JF Basson, Deputy Secretary Angelita Engelbrecht. 
> 
> The community of Middelpos as well as Calvinia and Sutherland came in their numbers to lay to rest a comrade who was one of a kind. Provincial Executive Member Dimakotso Koloi conveyed a message on behalf of the Provincial Chairperson Zamani Saul and the entire PEC. ANCYL Regional Chairman Loranne Saal joined the service and was accompanied by REC Member Eden from Karoo - Hoogland.
> 
> A sombre send off dignified by community members, political leaders, friends and family. A strong message from the Regional Chairperson Mervin Cloete to protect and stand in solidarity with the LGBTQ+ community. We guard against judgment of our brothers and sisters. Nothing but justice for comrade Hallee Laban conclude Cdr Koloi . 
>
