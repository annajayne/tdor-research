> A seamstress was murdered on Tuesday night (23/12), around 23:30, in Sítio Baiacos, rural area of Brejo da Madre de Deus, in Agreste de Pernambuco. The crime took place inside a home. The victim was identified as 41-year-old Cicero Pedro da Silva, a seamstress, known by the nickname "Peu", who was found dead at the scene.
> 
> According to police information, the victim was consuming alcoholic beverages in the company of others when, motivated by jealousy, he allegedly assaulted a woman with a brick. In reaction, a man who was at the scene stabbed Cicero.
> 
> After the crime, the suspects fled before police arrived. Diligence was carried out, but no one was arrested until the end of the incident. The case will be investigated by the Civil Police.
> Arrest of the Suspects
> 
> Two young people were arrested by the Military Police on suspicion of murdering Cicero Pedro da Silva, 41 years old, on Wednesday (24/12), in the rural area of Brejo da Madre de Deus, in Agreste de Pernambuco. The crime was committed by fish knife blows.
> 
> The suspects, Wanderson Moreira da Costa, 19 years old, and Henrique da 
> Silva Moura, 20, were located by the Military Police in a garage in the Center of Santa Cruz do Capibaribe, following reports. They confessed to the crime and claimed the motive was jealousy.
> 
> According to PM, after trying to run away and hide in a bush area, the couple surrendered without resistance. Both were taken to the Police Station, where the case was registered and is still under investigation by the Civil Police.
>

> A murder was reported in Sítio Bandeiras, in the rural area of Brejo da Madre de Deus, after a team from the 24th Military Police Battalion was called via the Operations Center (COPOM). Upon arriving at the indicated location, the police confirmed the death of a transgender woman identified as Cícero Pedro da Silva, 41 years old, better known as “Peu”.
> 
> According to information gathered by the police from witnesses, the victim was drinking alcohol with a group when he became involved in an argument motivated by jealousy. During the altercation, "Peu" allegedly assaulted a woman, hitting her on the head with a brick. In response to the assault, a man present at the scene stabbed her multiple times.
> 
> The victim succumbed to their injuries and died at the scene before receiving medical assistance. The suspects involved in the fight and subsequent homicide fled the scene immediately after the crime. Military personnel searched the area, but so far none of those involved have been located or arrested. The case remains under investigation by the Civil Police Station.
