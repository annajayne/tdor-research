====== Jenifer Lopez Perez Amaya (Matagalpa - 24 Dec 2025) ======

> Jennifer, a 32-year-old trans woman, was stabbed while near a gas station located at the highway.



> Justice has not yet arrived, almost 5 months after the murder of Jennifer Pérez , the trans woman killed in the municipality of El Tuma-La Dalia, in the department of Matagalpa , in the central zone of Nicaragua.
> 
> The trans woman surnamed Pérez Amaya, 32 years old, was murdered in circumstances still unknown on the night of December 23, 2025, in La Dalia, when she was near a gas station located at the exit of the road towards Waslala.
> 
> The body of La China, as she was affectionately known, had stab wounds and was found in the early hours of December 24 by people who passed through that area.
> 
> The incident caused consternation among the residents of La Dalia, especially in the San Martín Number 2 neighborhood, where the victim lived.
> 
> At first, the villagers didn't know who it was, but as the minutes passed they confirmed that it was "La China" .
> 
> On social media, friends and residents of the municipality mourned the murder of La China, whom they described as a sociable, hardworking person who  “had no problems with anyone.”
> 
> To date, the police have not provided any information about Jennifer's murder, but locals confirmed to Intertextual that the main suspect in the crime is a man identified as Miguel Dávila Tórrez .
> 
> In trying to confirm that version, Intertextual found that the Matagalpa prosecutor's office filed an accusation against Dávila on May 13, 2026, although he is still a fugitive from justice.



> Jenifer López, a 32-year-old transgender woman from the municipality of Tuma-La Dalia, in the department of Matagalpa, located 173 km from Managua, was found dead, face up, near a gas station on the road to Waslala, on December 24, 2025. According to information gathered by the Nicaraguan LGBTIQ+ Observatory, her death was caused by a knife attack. To date, the National Police have not publicly revealed the possible motive for the crime, nor have any suspects been arrested.
