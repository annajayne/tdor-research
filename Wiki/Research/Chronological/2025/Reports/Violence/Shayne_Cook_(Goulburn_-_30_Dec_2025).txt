> A quiet NSW town is grappling with the alleged murder of a woman who police claim was stabbed to death by her ex-girlfriend and the ex’s new boyfriend.

> Sharn Dee Cook, 48, was found with multiple stab wounds inside a home on Joshua St in Goulburn about 7am on January 3.

> 
> Cook’s ex-girlfriend, 40-year-old Anne-Marie Streek, and Streek’s new boyfriend, 44-year-old Stephen Henry Evans, were arrested at a nearby home about 8.30am last Thursday.
> 
> Both have since been charged with one count of domestic violence-related murder.
> 
> A friend of Cook’s, who asked to remain anonymous, told 7NEWS.com.au the small town was heartbroken.
> 
> She said Cook and Streek — also known as Anne-Marie Ringuet — had been together for almost a decade, with social media photos showing their earliest photos together dating back to October 2016.
> 
> After first getting to know each other online, the friend said Cook moved from Queensland to Goulburn so they could be together.
> 
> The friend claimed Cook had “saved” Streek from a previous “terrible” relationship.

> Streek’s Facebook page shows photos of the pair together from 2017 to 2023, before she later changed her profile picture to an intimate image with a man.

> Two of Cook’s Facebook accounts were both filled with photos of Streek.
> 
> One older account lists her as “engaged”, with an introduction stating: “I’m in a beautiful relationship with the most beautiful woman”.

> On Cook’s TikTok, she shared photos of the couple alongside her dogs, describing them as her “little family”.
> 
> Cook’s friend said she treated her dogs like her children.

> “She loved her dogs. She was really so good with them. She called them the boys like they were actually her children,” she said.

> The dogs were also featured on Streek’s social media.
