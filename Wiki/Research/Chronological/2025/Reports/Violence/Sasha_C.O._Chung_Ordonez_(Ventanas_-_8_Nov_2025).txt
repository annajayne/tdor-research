> The Silueta X Association, a key member of the Ecuadorian LGBT Federation, has voiced profound grief and indignation following the murder of Sasha C.O. Chung Ordóñez, a 25-year-old trans woman and beloved local stylist. Chung was fatally shot on the night of Saturday, November 8, 2025, in the Bellavista sector of Ventanas, Los Ríos province. The organization is demanding authorities investigate the crime as a potential Transfeminicide or hate crime.
> 
> Sasha, known for her joy, charisma, and authenticity, has been added to the painful list of victims of escalating violence that particularly targets the Trans population in Ecuador. The crime is especially alarming given the severe increase in insecurity affecting the Los Ríos province.
> 
> Diane Rodríguez, President of the Silueta X Association, issued a strong statement following the tragedy:
> 
> “The murder of Sasha Chung Ordóñez is a brutal reminder of the vulnerability facing Trans people in Ecuador. Sasha was a light in her community, and her violent passing wounds us deeply. We demand that Los Ríos authorities launch an immediate and exhaustive investigation to determine if this attack was directed against her due to her gender identity, which would constitute a Transfeminicide or hate crime. Impunity only perpetuates violence. Sasha’s life, and that of any Trans person, is non-negotiable! We demand real justice and urgent protective measures.”


> Asociación Silueta X,, a member of Federación Ecuatoriana de Organizaciones LGBT+ and the National Trans Revolution Platform - CoaliTrans, deeply regrets the murder of Sasha CO Chung Ordóñez , 25, affectionately known as Sasha , a stylist and trans woman, who was gunned down on the night of Saturday, November 8, in the Bellavista sector of the Ventanas canton, Los Ríos province. Sasha, known for her joy, charisma, and authenticity, joins the tragic list of victims of the violence plaguing the country and, in particular, the trans population. This crime occurs within a context of growing insecurity that severely affects the province of Los Ríos.
> 
> Diane Rodríguez, President of the Silueta X Association, expresses her outrage and sorrow at this latest act of violence: "The murder of Sasha Chung Ordóñez is a brutal reminder of the vulnerability faced by trans people in Ecuador. Sasha was a light in her community, and her violent death wounds us deeply. We demand that the authorities of Los Ríos conduct an immediate and thorough investigation to determine whether this attack was directed against her because of her gender identity, constituting a transfemicide or a hate crime. Impunity perpetuates violence. Sasha's life, and the life of any trans person, is not negotiable! We demand real justice and urgent protection measures."
