> A 42-year-old trans woman was found dead inside her home in the early hours of last Wednesday (3), in Guarujá , on the coast of São Paulo.
> 
> According to information from the Public Security Secretariat (SSP) of the State of São Paulo,
> military police officers were patrolling when they were informed by locals about the presence of a body in the backyard of a residence located on Avenida Gino Fabris, in the Sítio Grande neighborhood.
> 
> The police entered the property and found the body with a stab wound to the left ear. Inside the house, there was a large amount of blood, as well as a bloodstained cloth in the sink. No object used against the victim was found.
> 
> According to the police report, neighbors stated that they did not hear any noises or witness any unusual activity during the night.
>

> Transgender woman found dead at home in Guarujá, SP.0
> By Itanhaém News on December 6, 2025BREAKING NEWS
> A 42-year-old transgender woman was found dead in the backyard of her home in Guarujá, a coastal city in São Paulo state. The case, which raises suspicions about the cause of death, was registered at the Guarujá Police Station.
> 
> According to the police report, the victim's body had a wound on the left ear, possibly caused by a sharp object. The weapon was not found at the scene. No arrests have been made in connection with the case so far.
> 
> The incident occurred on Wednesday (3), around 6 am. Military police officers, on routine patrol in the Sítio Grande neighborhood, were alerted by passersby about the presence of a body inside a property. Faced with the situation, the police officers broke down the access gate and found the victim already lifeless, lying in the backyard of the house.
> 
> Inside the residence, police officers found a large amount of blood splattered throughout the living room, bedroom, and especially on a cloth found in the kitchen. The São Paulo State Public Security Secretariat (SSP-SP) reported that no object that could have been used to attack the victim was found at the scene.
> 
> Neighbors of the victim reported to authorities that they did not hear any noises, screams, or any other unusual activity that could indicate a crime.
> 
> During the initial analysis of the crime scene, the police officers who responded to the incident were unable to determine if there had been a physical struggle, due to the general disarray inside the house. According to the officers, the apparent disorganization of the place suggested that the victim may have had hoarding tendencies.
> 
> The incident was registered at the police station as a suspicious death and discovery of a body. The SSP-SP (São Paulo State Public Security Secretariat) reported that "investigations are underway to clarify all the facts related to the case." Authorities continue to investigate to determine the circumstances of the death and identify possible culprits.
