> The 11 suspects arrested this Friday morning (5) during Operation Factional Dictatorship CPX have been identified . They are being investigated for the kidnapping, torture and death of José Walleffe dos Santos Lins from Alagoas , as well as the kidnapping and torture of his wife, Ariane da Silva Cerqueira, and the kidnapping of the couple's two-year-old son. Some of the suspects are also being investigated for the disappearance of Gabriely, a neighbor and friend of the family.
> 
> The operation executed 11 temporary arrest warrants and another 11 search and seizure warrants. According to the Specialized Homicide and Personal Protection Police Station (DHPP), the investigation uncovered crimes of torture, homicide, and concealment of a corpse committed in August in Várzea Grande .
> 
> According to investigator Rogério Ribeiro, all the suspects have links to criminal factions and participated in different stages of the crimes of kidnapping, torture, and homicide.
> 
> Of the 11 suspects, three are fugitives, six have been arrested, and one died in a confrontation with police. The identity of one of the suspects has been kept confidential . The Civil Police did not disclose the reason.
> 
> The operation is part of the investigations into the kidnapping, torture, and death of José Walleffe dos Santos Lins, a 28-year-old from Alagoas . His body was found on August 20th, buried in the Isabel Campos Residential Neighborhood in Várzea Grande, after being missing for 11 days.
> 
> According to the police, the body showed signs of violence and injuries consistent with a knife or sharp object .
> 
> In August of this year, José Walleffe, his partner, Ariane da Silva Cerqueira, 27, and their 1-year-old son were kidnapped. A few days later, mother and child were found. The three are originally from Maceió (AL) and had been in Mato Grosso for a short time.
> 
> At the time, Ariane and her son were taken to a hospital in the capital. The child received medical attention and was physically well. Ariane, however, had bruises all over her body and face, as well as a fracture in her right arm, and needed to remain hospitalized for surgery.
> 
> Investigations led by Detective Nilson Faria indicated that José was killed as a result of a dispute between criminal factions , and the participation of several members of the group in the crime was identified.
> 
> Furthermore, the couple's neighbor and friend, Gabriely Silva de Jesus, a 21-year-old transgender woman, disappeared on August 14th . The investigator on the case believes that some of the suspects have a connection to the victim's disappearance.
> 
> According to police, Gabriely's family reported that she had only recently moved into the condominium, as had Ariane and José, with whom she was a neighbor and friend and maintained daily contact. She allegedly witnessed and attempted to intervene in their kidnapping, which was carried out by a criminal faction.
> 
> Days later, Gabriely stopped responding to messages and did not contact her family again. The case is being investigated by the Civil Police.



> Friday, December 5 , 2025, 5:25 PM - A | A
> 
> Transgender woman killed after alerting family about young man's execution in Várzea Grande.
> The victim's body has not yet been located; the case exposes criminal dominance in the region.
> 
> A trans woman, whose name was not released, was murdered by members of a criminal faction in Várzea Grande after informing the mother of José Wallef dos Santos Lins that he had been captured by the group. The information was confirmed by the Civil Police during Operation Factional Dictatorship CPX, launched this Friday (5) in the metropolitan region.
> 
> According to Detective Nilson Farias of the Homicide Division, the young woman "disappeared" shortly after informing her family about the kidnapping. "She told José Wallef's mother that he had been captured by the gang. Not satisfied with that, they went and made this trans woman disappear. We killed her, and her body has not yet been located," stated the detective.
> 
> The murder of José Wallef was also committed with extreme violence. Before killing him, the criminals kidnapped him, his wife, and their two-year-old son. Of the ten people involved, one of the suspects, identified as Bruno César Amorim Santos, pointed out as the group's leader, died in a confrontation with the police. Six others were arrested, and three remain at large.
> 
> According to the Civil Police, the case reveals how the community's residents lived under the rules imposed by the faction.
> 
> "They were living in a parallel state, with their own bloody rules. People didn't even have the right to express themselves," Farias emphasized.
