> Morgana Clemente Ferreira Silva, 19, who had been missing after leaving a snack bar, was found dead in the Poço Sota region, in the city of Goiás, in the northwest of the state. An 18-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of femicide. 



> The family of Morgana Clemente Ferreira, a 19-year-old found dead after disappearing in Cidade de Goiás last October, has again requested the arrest of the accused. According to lawyer Brasineide Clemente, the victim's aunt and assistant prosecutor in the case, there is concern about the defendant remaining free and reports of possible intimidation of witnesses.
> 
> The case is in the procedural instruction phase and awaits a statement from the Public Prosecutor's Office regarding a request for a new preventive detention order submitted by the prosecution. According to Brasineide, the accused was arrested shortly after the investigations pointed to his involvement in the crime, but was granted provisional release in February of this year.
> 
> “Right after he indicated the location and was identified by the investigations, he was arrested. But the defense filed a request and obtained his release in February. Since then, he has been responding to the proceedings while free,” Brasineide stated in an interview with Jornal Opção. The lawyer said that the request for a new arrest warrant was filed after the accused left the city of Goiás without formally informing them of the change of address during part of the proceedings.
> 
> “He is no longer living in the city. From what we have been informed, he is living in Novo Brasil. This information was not initially in the case file and was only presented after the arrest warrant was issued,” she stated. According to Brasineide, another factor that motivated the request was the suspicion of threats involving witnesses in the case.
> 
> “The victim’s mother received reports about possible threats. This was also brought to the attention of the court. We understand that there are elements that justify a new evaluation of the preventive detention,” she said. The lawyer also reported that the accused did not appear at one of the hearings in the case, a situation that led to his being declared in default.
> 
> Later, he participated in another procedural act via videoconference. “He did not appear at the first hearing and was declared in default. Then, at the following hearing, he participated virtually. Now we are awaiting the Public Prosecutor's Office's position on the arrest warrant request,” he explained.
> 
> Relationship and disappearance
> Brasineide also recalled the events of the early morning hours when Morgana disappeared. According to her, family and friends believe that the young woman had a relationship with the accused, although she had not officially revealed his identity.
> 
> "Morgana mentioned that she was dating someone, but said she didn't want to reveal who it was yet. She said she would tell her family soon," she reported.
> 
> On the night of her disappearance, Morgana attended a gathering with friends. According to her lawyer, the accused was part of the same group and arrived at the gathering in the early hours of the morning.
> 
> “They spent part of the night with friends. Then, when the group started to disperse, the two stayed together. That was the last time she was seen,” he stated.
> 
> The family began searching for the young woman after she failed to return home and did not respond to messages. According to Brasineide, all of her friends reported that Morgana had last been seen in the company of the accused.
> 
> "When we contacted the people who were in the group, they all said the same thing: that the last person who was with Morgana was him," she said.
> 
> The body was located.
> During the investigation, the Civil Police identified inconsistencies in the statements given by the accused. According to the lawyer, he initially claimed to have left Morgana at a place known as Poço da Sota.
> 
> "He said he had left Morgana there and that he didn't know what had happened. But the investigations continued," he stated.
> 
> Later, the young woman's body was found in another part of the area. Police arrested the suspect and charged him with femicide. Jornal Opção was unable to reach the accused's defense attorney for comment.

> Morgana Clemente Ferreira, 19, was found dead after leaving a snack bar and disappearing in Goiás , in the northwest of the state. According to police, the suspect took her to a place to shower and drink. Morgana's body was found near the location indicated by the suspect.
> 
> "He allegedly went with her to a place called 'Poço da Sota'. The young man went to the location to show the teams where he had consumed alcoholic beverages with the victim," said Detective Gustavo.
> 
> The suspect's identity has not been revealed, and therefore g1 was unable to contact his defense. The Civil Police did not provide further information because the investigations are under seal.
> 
> Investigations revealed that the 18-year-old was the last person to be with Morgana. While the suspect was showing police the location where he was last seen with her, the body was found, according to the police chief.
> 
> According to the police, Morgana disappeared on Sunday (12) and her body was found on Tuesday (14) with a puncture wound in the abdomen. According to the forensic examination, which has not yet been completed, the object used was probably a knife.
> 
> Detective Gustavo Cabral stated that the suspect showed no reaction upon seeing the young woman's body and that the evidence gathered during the investigation points to his involvement in the crime. The investigation will continue, and if the suspicion is confirmed, he will face charges of femicide.
> 
> According to the police, the suspect was arrested on Tuesday (14) and must answer for the crime of femicide.
> 
> The inquiry was forwarded to the Specialized Police Station for Women's Assistance (Deam) in the city of Goiás for further investigation.
