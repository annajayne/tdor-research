> Trans woman who disappeared after going out for a sex work appointment is found dead in Mato Grosso.
> Betina worked in the region and had left on Monday (1) to carry out a program and, since then, had been missing.
> 
> Missing since Monday (1), the body of the trans woman identified as Betina, 33 years old, was found in a wooded area near Fama University, in Nova Mutum (242 km from Cuiabá). According to the report, the victim was already in an advanced state of decomposition and was found by residents of the region. 
> 
> The Military Police and the Official Expertise and Technical Identification (Politec) were at the scene to carry out standard procedures. Family members arrived to identify the body and confirmed that it was Betina.
> 
> According to reports, Betina worked in the area and had left on Monday to perform a service, and had been missing since then. During the search, teams also found the victim's motorcycle, which was near the body.
> 
> The case is being investigated by the Civil Police, who are looking into the circumstances and motivation behind the homicide.


> Content/ODOC – The body of the trans woman was found in an advanced state of decomposition on Wednesday afternoon (3), in a wooded area of ??Nova Mutum (264 km from Cuiabá). The victim, identified only as Betina, had been missing since Monday (1).
> 
> The Military Police were called by residents who spotted a body on the side of a rural road, near Unifama University. Upon arriving at the scene, officers located a white Honda Biz motorcycle and Betina's belongings near where the body was found.
> 
> Due to the length of time she had been missing, the body was already decomposing. Documents bearing the young woman's registered name were found in the area, and later the family identified the body, confirming that it was Betina.
> 
> The victim had left home to perform a sexual act and had not been in contact with her family since.
> 
> The Official Forensic and Technical Identification Service (Politec) conducted its work at the scene and sent the body to the Institute of Forensic Medicine (IML). So far, no signs of violence or the cause of death have been released.
