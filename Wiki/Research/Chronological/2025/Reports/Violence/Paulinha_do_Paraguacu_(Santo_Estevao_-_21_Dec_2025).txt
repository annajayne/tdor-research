> A trans woman was found dead on Sunday morning (21), on Estrada da Boiadeira, in the rural area of Santo Estêvão, a municipality located 41 kilometers from Feira de Santana.
> 
> According to the Civil Police, the victim was identified as Paulinha do Paraguaçu. The body was found on the roadside median, in a seated position, leaning against a fence. The woman's hands and mouth were tied, and she had gunshot wounds to the head, arms, chest, and leg.
> 
> According to the police, Paulinha was wearing a red dress and black clogs. At the crime scene, teams from the Department of Technical Police (DPT) collected ammunition casings scattered along the street.
> 
> At this time, there is no information regarding the perpetrator or the motive for the homicide. The case will be investigated by the Territorial Police Station (DT) of Santo Estêvão.
