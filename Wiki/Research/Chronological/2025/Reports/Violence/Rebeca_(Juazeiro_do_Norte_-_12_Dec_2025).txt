> A 34-year-old trans woman, identified as Rebeca, was shot dead on Friday morning (12) in the João Cabral neighborhood, in Juazeiro do Norte. Preliminary information indicates that she was hit by a shot in the back during the early hours of the morning. 
> 
> Residents called Samu, but Rebeca was already dead when the team arrived at the scene. The Forensic Expertise carried out the initial procedures, analyzed the crime scene and removed the body. 
> 
> The Military Police tried to access images from cameras installed in the region, but the available equipment did not store recordings, only transmitted images in real time.
