> The second person was a trans woman from Peru, whose name is not reported in any news article I could find. All we know is that she was a sex worker (specifically a prostitute) from Peru and that she was trans; she was either 27 or 28 year old (sources differ). She was robbed and murdered by two men who had previously robbed and assaulted a number of prostitutes and sex workers (some of which also trans). She was killed in her own apartment in the city of Alessandria on October 9, 2025.




> A robbery gone wrong has resulted in the death of a 28-year-old Peruvian transgender woman. She was killed by two young men, aged 22 and 25. The Carabinieri had already arrested them immediately after the incident, but for other crimes, even before discovering the murder. Now, Nabil Fakhreddine and Othmane Makboul have been remanded in custody, where they were already being held for robbery and snatching, and for murder. The Carabinieri of Alessandria carried out the arrest warrant, while reconstructing what happened on October 9th in a home in the Cristo neighborhood. That morning, a body was found lying on the bedroom floor after the apartment owner called the police , as the tenant had not answered the phone for days. 
> 
> Connections immediately emerged with another incident involving two twenty-year-olds in a series of robberies and snatch thefts committed within a very short period of time, between September and October, in Tortona and Alessandria. The last confirmed incident occurred on the morning of October 8th.   
> 
> The investigation led to the two homeless foreigners, aged 20 and 25, through testimonies, reports, and complaints filed by the victims. Then, analysis of the phone number of one of the men revealed a cell phone connection consistent with the date and time of death on October 9th. Surveillance cameras did the rest.



> Murder on Via De Amicis in Alessandria: The perpetrators identified. A robbery gone wrong.
> The two killers of the Peruvian transsexual had already been arrested for robbery
> December 22, 2025
> 
> The Carabinieri had already arrested them for a series of violent robberies, all involving prostitutes. Now they are also charged with the murder of a 28-year-old Peruvian transsexual, found dead in an apartment on Via De Amicis in Alessandria on October 9th. The two alleged killers are two homeless foreigners, aged 22 and 25, caught by wiretaps placed in a waiting room and a cell in the Alessandria prison. The 22-year-old also confessed to prosecutor Gualtiero Battisti, accusing his friend of being the perpetrator of the crime.
> 
> Which could therefore be the result of a robbery gone wrong. The events date back to just over two months ago: it was 11:30 a.m. on October 9th, when a patrol from the Alessandria Cristo Carabinieri station responded to Via De Amicis, where the owner of an apartment reported that a tenant hadn't answered her phone for days.
> 
> The officers found the door unlocked and, using the owner's keys, entered and found a body lying on the bedroom floor. As confirmed by the coroner, who arrived shortly thereafter along with the forensic team and the prosecutor, the death had occurred approximately 24 hours earlier. Carabinieri from the Alessandria Operations Unit interviewed possible witnesses, from the property owner to neighbors, only to discover that the victim was a 28-year-old Peruvian transgender man. However, they immediately connected the case to the arrest, which had occurred shortly before, of two twenty-year-olds accused of a series of robberies and snatching thefts. According to the prosecution, the two traveled by bicycle and primarily targeted elderly people and prostitutes between Tortona and Alessandria. Between September and October, they contacted the victims to arrange paid sex, before robbing them.
> 
> The last confirmed incident occurred on the morning of October 8th, at 8:40 a.m. on Corso Romita, involving the attack on a transgender man involved in prostitution. The investigators' hypothesis regarding the murder is supported by similarities to robberies, as well as the victim's characteristics and injuries. The geolocation of the suspects' phones also confirmed this: the phone number of one of the robbers connected to a cell tower at a time consistent with the victim's death. The two had contacted the cell tower to arrange a meeting on October 9th. Furthermore, video surveillance footage from the area allowed them to reconstruct the suspects' route from the building where they lived to the victim's residence, where they arrived on two bicycles (which were later seized from the same building).
> 
> Meanwhile, the images also show that the two were wearing some of the clothing already seized during their previous arrest for robbery, except for a gray sweatshirt worn by the 22-year-old and found in prison. Having established this, the prosecutor ordered wiretaps in a waiting room and a prison cell, which revealed statements and admissions from the two men regarding what happened on Via De Amicis. The younger man then confessed when the Carabinieri visited the prison to collect biological material for DNA testing.
