> Dolly Dobi died on May 5, 2025 by murder in Fort-de-France (Martinique), born on August 13, 1977 in Castries, Saint Lucia (Caribbean).



> On Monday, May 5th, a violent murder—that of Dolly, a homeless woman—might have gone unnoticed had the LGBTQIA+ community of Martinique not alerted the public. Yesterday, the transgender woman was buried in Sainte-Thérèse, surrounded by her entire family who came specifically from Saint Lucia to accompany her to her final resting place.
> 
> Pâquerette Dalu was determined to accompany Dolly, a homeless woman from Saint Lucia, who was brutally murdered in the street earlier this month. The emotion is palpable in the voice of this loyal friend who never disowned the woman known as Dolly. Pâquerette knew the entire Dobi family when they were still living on the English-speaking island. She explains: "Kurt arrived at the age of 11. He immediately took to Martinique, where he went to school. Until he was 18, he had no problems. None at all! He was loved by everyone, he was such a kind boy. And polite, and well-mannered. The problems started with crack cocaine. Kurt, who would become Dolly, took great care with his appearance. Unfortunately, someone told him that to avoid gaining weight, he should take crack cocaine. That's how Dolly gradually fell into drug addiction." Kurt Dobi, known as Dolly, was a lesbian and a trans woman. Dolly didn't find this situation easy to live with, even though her family never disowned her. Daisy continues: "As long as her mother was alive, Dolly took refuge with her. She always welcomed her child with open arms. She would start by giving her a bath, then feed her, and try to find ways to get her off the streets. But Dolly never stayed very long. Hardly more than a week. After that, she would go back to the streets. This drove her mother to despair, because a mother suffers when poverty befalls her children!"
