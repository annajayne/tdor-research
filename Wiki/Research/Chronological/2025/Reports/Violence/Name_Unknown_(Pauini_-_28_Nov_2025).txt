> Man arrested on suspicion of killing transgender partner after argument in Amazonas.
> During questioning, the man confessed that the argument was due to jealousy. The suspect grabbed a gun and shot the 27-year-old victim in the abdomen.
> By g1 AM
> 01/12/2025 11:12 AM  Updated2 weeks ago
> 
> The Civil Police of Amazonas (PC-AM) arrested a 35-year-old man suspected of killing his partner, a 27-year-old transgender woman. The crime occurred in the rural area of Pauini, in the interior of Amazonas. According to the police, the crime was motivated by jealousy.
> 
> According to Detective Renan Boina de Barbe, from the 63rd Interactive Police Station (DIP) in Pauini, the team began the search after receiving a report about the victim's death.
> 
> "During the on-site investigation, it was determined that the perpetrator fired shots at his partner. She was taken to the hospital, but did not survive her injuries," said the police chief.
> 
> The suspect was identified and taken to the police station on Friday (28). During questioning, he confessed that, during an argument fueled by jealousy, he grabbed a gun and shot the victim in the abdomen.
> 
> "Based on the facts, a request for the perpetrator's preventive detention was submitted to the court, considering the seriousness of the crime and the strong social outcry. The court order was issued," stated the police chief.
> The man was arrested in the Ouro Verde neighborhood, in the city center. He will be charged with femicide and will remain at the disposal of the Justice system.

>

> 
> The victim was taken to the hospital, but did not survive the injuries caused by a gunshot wound.
