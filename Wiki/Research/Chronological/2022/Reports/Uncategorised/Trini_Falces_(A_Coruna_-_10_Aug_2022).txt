> Trans activist Trini Falcés , a leading figure in LGBTQ+ rights and recipient of the Marcela and Elisa Award in 2019, passed away this week in A Coruña , as announced this afternoon by her family. Falcés's life was fraught with obstacles, and her story is intertwined with the history of the LGBTQ+ movement. She was imprisoned during the Franco regime, at just seventeen years old, under the Law of Vagrants and Delinquents . "She lived through repression, prison, police abuse, and the stigma of a society that didn't understand (and sometimes still doesn't understand) what lies beyond supposed normality," her closest circle recalled in a letter.
> 
> In 1953, in the civil registry of A Coruña, he managed to change his name ; and years later, already established in Barcelona temporarily after being searched and captured simply for the fact of being, she led the first Pride march in 1977 . "You come face to face with the body to claim our rights when sex and gender dissidence was still a crime. In our memory is diverse and colorful, we do not know who started this problem, however, we have to learn to disabuse it," the letter concludes.
