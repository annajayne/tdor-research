> Daniela, the trans woman run over by a truck in Santander
> Q'hubo Editorial Staff
> November 5, 2024
> Daniela will be remembered for her kind words and enormous heart. It was also learned that she resided in Cúcuta.
> 
> At kilometer 67 of the road that leads from Bucaramanga to Pamplona,  ​​in the sector known as Cuesta Boba, jurisdiction of the municipality of Tona, a traffic accident occurred that claimed the life of Daniela, a transgender woman.
> 
> She was riding as a passenger on a Suzuki GN motorcycle, registration number CCR-35H, which was driven by Ronny Leonel Ramírez Ceballos. 
> 
> Around 5:00 pm this Sunday, November 3rd, there was fog and rain on the aforementioned road . The motorcyclist, on a curve with the pavement slippery, lost control of his vehicle and fell.
> 
> Ramírez Ceballos was unharmed.  Daniela, however, was unlucky; after falling into the opposite lane, she was run over by a Chevrolet truck with license plate SXV-065.
> 
> Paramedics in an ambulance arrived at the scene and tried to help the woman, but the serious injuries left her dead at the scene.
> 
> The Santander Police Traffic and Transportation Division conducted the technical inspection of the body and prepared the accident report. An investigation will be carried out to determine the exact cause of the traffic accident. 
> 
> The victim was a resident of Cúcuta, Norte de Santander. Her body remains at the Morgue of Legal Medicine and Forensic Sciences in Bucaramanga awaiting release to her family.
> 
> The Asotransnor Foundation, which works to promote the rights and inclusion of transgender people in the capital of Norte de Santander,  stated that “Daniela will be remembered for her kind words and enormous heart. We deeply regret your loss and will miss you profoundly. May your soul rest in peace forever.”
