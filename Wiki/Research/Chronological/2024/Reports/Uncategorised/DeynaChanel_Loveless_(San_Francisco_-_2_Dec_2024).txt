> We honor and celebrate the love and life of DeynaChanel Loveless, who passed away on December 28, 2024. Known affectionately as Coco, DeynaChanel was a Black trans woman cherished by her community and a passionate advocate for trans housing in San Francisco. She served as a consulting partner for the City’s Department of Homelessness and Supportive Housing, contributing to the strategic planning process for the Home by the Bay initiative through her impactful community engagement work. DeynaChanel was also an active member of the TGIJP community, employed by Larkin Street Youth Services, was a certified HIV and homelessness advocate, and a bright light in the community. DeynaChanel would have turned 60 on February 3rd 2025. 
>  
> Community members will gather on January 17th 2025 to celebrate the life of DeynaChanel Loveless from 2:30 - 3PM near Gate B of the SF Ferry Building. Refreshments will be provided, comfortable shoes are encouraged. All community members are welcome to join.

> Coco will be missed and cherished by all of the many lives she has touched, and her advocacy and legacy will live on in all of us. Rest in power, DeynaChanel Loveless.
