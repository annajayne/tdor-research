> A trans woman named Michelle Cornwell died by suicide in a Florida prison on January 27, 2024 at 31 years old. She was a poet, artist, and advocate who published her work in the newsletter of the Alyssa Rodriguez Center for Gender Justice. In one letter, she wrote, "They took my hair, makeup, etc.; they can take everything. They can use any restraint or restriction that legislation has approved in correction. But, one thing they cannot do is take away my voice."



> Trey A. Cornwell, "Michelle", 31, of Brooksville, Florida passed away on January 27, 2024, in Milton, Florida. Trey “Michelle” was born in Gainesville, Florida on March 14, 1992. He was the thirdborn of eight children.
> 
> As a child Trey enjoyed playing sports. He played soccer, baseball, football, basketball and was even on his high school swim team. He was also very smart. He made honor roll or principals list more than all of his other brothers combined. He was very close with his brothers and cousins growing up. They would play outside from sun up to sun down then come inside and play video games, board games and Pokémon cards until bedtime.
> 
> As an adult Trey got more interested in art. He loved to draw pictures and write poetry. He also loved music. He enjoyed to write songs, sing, and played the guitar. One thing many people may not know about him is how much he enjoyed cooking. He would cook meals for the entire family. His favorite meal to cook was beef stroganoff.
> 
> Trey was always a funny, friendly and caring person. He could always take a joke. If you gave him one you better be ready to take one though. He always seemed to bring more fun and life into the room. He was also very outgoing and could talk to anyone. He would make friends with them too and he could be friends with anyone. He never judged people before he got to know them either.
> 
> Trey could find the positive in any situation. He was a great person and an amazing friend, brother and son.
> 
> Predeceased by Grandparents: Lillian and Dominic Laviano; Roy and Linda Cornwell: Great Grandparents: LeRoy and Naomi Conley and Charles Reeves; Cousins: Tyler Allyn Cantrell and Christopher Conley.
