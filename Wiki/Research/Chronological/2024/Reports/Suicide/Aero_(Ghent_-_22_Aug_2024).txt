> The parents of a teenager in transition who took their own life last summer have filed a complaint against the Kortrijk hospital AZ Groeninge, according to HLN, confirmed by the parents' lawyer.
> 
> According to the parents, the death could have been prevented if the hospital had paid more attention to psychological support. "This is a very serious matter, and AZ Groeninge has treated it far too lightly," said attorney Walter Van Steenbrugge. 
> 
> The teenager had long wanted to change his gender and had told his parents he wanted to become a boy. "Ghent University Hospital has an excellent reputation in this area, but transitioning at that hospital involves a lengthy two-year process involving numerous psychological examinations," says Van Steenbrugge. "However, as soon as he turned eighteen, he made an appointment at AZ Groeninge in Kortrijk on his own initiative." According to his parents, after just one session with the doctor and psychologist, he was prescribed testosterone in ampoule form. 
> 
> Shortly afterward, the parents noticed a change in his behavior. "No attention was ever paid to any underlying psychological issues, while one must proceed with extreme care during such a drastic transition," says Van Steenbrugge. The mother emailed the head of the department to express her concerns, but received no response until after the trans boy had committed suicide. "The complaint dates back to September of last year, shortly after his death," the lawyer said. "But the parents still haven't been given access to the file. All they want is for this to never happen again." 
> 
> AZ Groeninge confirms that an investigation is underway, but declines to comment further to allow the justice department to conduct its work in complete serenity.
