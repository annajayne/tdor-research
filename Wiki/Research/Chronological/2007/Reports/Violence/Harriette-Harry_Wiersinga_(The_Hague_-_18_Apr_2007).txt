Harriette/Harry (55) was attacked on March 28th 2007, by a black man, in the middle of the day, in broad daylight, and nobody came to her rescue. This was on a busy street called "Spui" in the centre of the city of The Hague in the Netherlands. She was in a coma for a few weeks, but died anyway on April 18th 2007. No one has been sentenced for her murder!!!! Harriette, who was born with a male body, didn't consider herself to be a man or a woman, but said "maybe I'm an angel".
