====== Suleyma (Mexicali - 22 Jun 2026) ======
Created Sunday 05 July 2026
@accidental


Suleyma [Sergio Ramses]
25
22 Jun 2026
Las Truchas canal
Ejido La Coronita
Mexicali
drowned


> Trans woman found dead after entering the Las Truchas canal
> The body was recovered from the water so that forensic services could carry out the corresponding procedures and subsequently be transferred to the Forensic Medical Service (Semefo).
> 
> After more than three hours of searching in the Truchas irrigation canal, in the Ejido La Coronita , firefighters divers located the body of a 25-year-old transgender woman named Suleyma .
> 
> The incident was reported at 3:44 p.m. this Monday. A person reported that their friend had entered the canal and did not come out, so they called 911
> 
> Municipal Police and Fire Department personnel responded; after an intense search, they located the young woman dead.
> 
> The body was removed from the water so that Forensic Services could carry out the corresponding procedures and subsequently be transferred to the Forensic Medical Service (Semefo).
