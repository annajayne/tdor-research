> A 25 year-old tran woman named Katherine Allison Harrison was killed after being struck by a vehicle on October 29, 2026. The driver was an off-duty Kansas City, Kansas fire captain named Tripper L. Sadler. She used the name Allison. Her partner called her Katherine.
> 
> Allison was parked legally on the side of the road, standing behind her vehicle looking into the trunk. Her hazard lights were on. Sadler was driving a 2024 Subaru Crosstrek northbound on the right shoulder of I-35, It is unknown why Sadler was driving on the shoulder.
>
> Sadler hit Allison, propelling her and her car across three lanes of traffic. Not propelling her in her car, but her actual body and the car. She died on the scene.
> 
> Sadler remains employed by the Kansas City, Kansas Fire Department. Department leadership stated they are awaiting the conclusion of the law enforcement investigation before making further personnel determinations. Police have preliminarily ruled out impairment, but are still investigating why Sadler was on the shoulder.
