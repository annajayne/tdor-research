> A transgender individual reportedly died after being electrocuted in a hotel bathroom.
> 
> Batuhan G., a transgender individual, was found motionless in the bathroom of the apart-hotel where he was staying in the Konak district of Izmir and died in the hospital to which he was taken. It is believed that G. may have been electrocuted by a faulty water heater.
> 
> 
> The incident occurred around 00:15 in an apart-hotel on 1463 Street in the Alsancak neighborhood. According to reports, friends of Batuhan G., who occasionally stayed at the hotel, contacted the 112 Emergency Call Center after being unable to reach him. Police and medical teams were dispatched to the address. Upon entering the room, the teams found Batuhan G. motionless in the bathroom. Following initial medical intervention, the transgender individual was taken to Alsancak Nevvar Salih Isgören State Hospital, where, despite all efforts, he could not be saved.
