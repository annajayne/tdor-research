> Elize Matsunaga's ex-boyfriend dies after being run over on a highway in São Paulo.
> The accident happened in São Vicente (SP). Tiago Cheregatte Neves met Elize at Penitentiary 2 in Tremembé.
> By g1 Santos
> 13/05/2026 17h50  Updated6 hours ago
> 
> Elize Matsunaga's ex-boyfriend, Tiago Cheregatte Neves, died after being run over by several vehicles on the Padre Manoel da Nóbrega Highway in São Vicente, on the coast of São Paulo. The two met at Penitentiary 2 in Tremembé, known as the prison for celebrities, where they began a relationship.
> 
> Tiago was a transgender man who was moved to the women's wing of the prison because he was at risk of being assaulted if he were moved to the men's wing. He was serving time for attempting to kill his grandfather.
> 
> According to the police report, obtained by g1 this Wednesday (13), highway patrol officers were called to a traffic accident with a victim on the 5th.
> 
> At the time, the first driver to hit Tiago testified that he was driving attentively and within the speed limit when he noticed the vehicle ahead swerve sharply to avoid hitting a pedestrian. He stated that he tried to brake, but did not have enough time to avoid the impact.
> 
> According to the police report, a police car also ran over the victim, and the first driver to hit Tiago stated that other cars also ran over Elize's ex-boyfriend.
> 
> The driver of the car and the military police officer driving the patrol car were given a breathalyzer test, which showed a negative result for alcohol consumption.
> 
> According to the police report, the death was confirmed by a doctor from Ecovias, the concessionaire responsible for that stretch of highway. The concessionaire's team also found a piece of paper with the man's identification next to his body.
> 
> Relationship documented in a book.
> Elize was convicted of killing and dismembering her husband in 2012. After having dated women, according to information previously released by the author of the unauthorized biography, Ullisses Campbell, the inmate became involved with Tiago, who was in the women's wing because he was at risk of aggression if he were moved to the men's wing.
