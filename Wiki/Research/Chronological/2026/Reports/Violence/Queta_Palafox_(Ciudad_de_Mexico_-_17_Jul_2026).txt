> Two trans women murdered this weekend in Mexico City
> The trans femicides were recorded in the boroughs of Iztapalapa and Coyoacán. Activists denounce that the local Prosecutor's Office has not yet taken over the case of one of the victims to the specialized femicide unit.
> 
> This weekend, two trans women were murdered in Mexico City:On Friday, July 17, Queta Palafox, an elderly woman known by that name to some trans women, was murdered in Iztapalapa. Yesterday, the body of a trans woman was found in her apartment in the Piloto Culhuacán neighborhood of Coyoacán.
> 
> Both bodies were found with visible signs of violence, which points to hate crimes based on the victims' gender identity.
> 
> Victoria Samano, a trans woman and founder of the collective and civil association La Lleca, Escuchando la calle (Listening to the Street), named these transfemicides on her social media and warned that so far,The investigation file for the murder of Queta Palafox has not been brought to the specialized femicide area of ​​the Mexico City prosecutor's office.
> 
> These crimes occurred amidst protests by members of the trans and non-binary community, who held a sit-in for 21 days (until July 10) in front of the Ministry of the Interior, demanding the creation of a Comprehensive Trans Law and the inclusion of hate crimes in the Federal Penal Code, following recommendation 42/2024 from the National Human Rights Commission. Their demands also included a guarantee of a comprehensive healthcare model with adequate supplies and functional medical care, as well as the implementation of effective policies for job placement and identity registration processes free from institutional discrimination.
> 
> The LGBT+ Crime Observatory in Mexico recorded a total of 115 cases of violence against LGBT+ people in the country during 2025: 59 murders, 34 disappearances, 16 attacks and 6 suicides.
> 
> Records documented by the Observatory show that murders continue to be the form of violence with the highest number of casesOf the 59 murders of LGBT+ people documented in 2025, 33 were trans women.25 to gay men and 1 to a muxe person.
> 

> "The high concentration of cases involving trans women demonstrates once again the persistence of specific forms of violence directed toward gender identities that challenge social norms. This violence is closely linked to sexism, transphobia, and exclusion, as well as the demand to maintain a binary, biological status quo that ignores the fundamental characteristics of gender and places trans women in situations of greater vulnerability," the Observatory states in its report , Persistent Violence: Hate Crimes Against LGBT+ People in Mexico .
