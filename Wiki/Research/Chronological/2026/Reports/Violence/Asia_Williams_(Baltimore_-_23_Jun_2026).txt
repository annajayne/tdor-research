> As the US celebrates our 250th anniversary, a 38 year old Black Trans Woman Dies By Gun Violence in Baltimore.
> July 2, 2026 by Sue
> 
> Asia Williams via Facebook
> A 38-year-old Black trans woman was shot to death in Baltimore on June 23, 2026. Her name was Asia Williams.
> 
> The Crime
> On June 23 around 10:49 p.m., officers were called to Mega Tobacco & Convenience, located at 5211 York Road, for a reported shooting, according to authorities.
> 
> There, officers found a 38-year-old woman and a 27-year-old man suffering from gunshot wounds.
> 
> Apparently, a third party – an unidentified Black man wearing a neon yellow vest entered the store, pulled out a gun, and shot. There is no information on his motive, if he knew the victims, or if the victims are connected. You can find more details at this YouTube link.
> 
> Both were transported to a hospital where the woman, Asia Williams, was pronounced dead. The male victim is expected to survive.
> 
> Police have no indicated Asia was targeted, but we won’t know more until they are further in the investigation.
> 
> To remain anonymous, submit a tip on the P3 Tips mobile app, call Metro Crime Stoppers at 1-866-7LOCKUP or visit the MCS of Maryland website. If your tip results in felony charges and an arrest, you may be eligible for a reward of up to $8,000.
> 
> Honoring Asia
> Asia recently celebrated her birthday on June 14. She was born and raised in Baltimore, Maryland. Asia was close with many of her family members, posting often about their gatherings and accomplishments. She graduated from Northern High School in 2005.
> 
> Her socials show her fashion sense, especially her nail art, as well as her appreciation for ‘good Southern soul food.’ She shared content about her inner journey, reposted videos that made her laugh, and
> 
> Her aunt shared “Asia was a bright light in our lives, full of kindness and love, and her sudden departure has left a void that words cannot describe. She was not only a dedicated retail manager but also worked tirelessly driving for DoorDash to support her family. Just before this heartbreaking event, she and her mother moved to a new home.”
> 
> Friends and family have organized a vigil Friday, July 3. A celebration of life is planned on July 11. Her family has established a crowdfund to support with her final expenses.
> 
> If you can donate, please do. Asia was a breadwinner for her family who will feel that additional loss sooner rather than later.
