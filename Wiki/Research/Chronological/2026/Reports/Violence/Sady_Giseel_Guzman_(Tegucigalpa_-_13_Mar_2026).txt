> HONDURAS TODAY
> Details emerge about Giseel Guzmán, a trans woman murdered in El Carrizal
> Giseel Guzmán, 31, was a leader in the LGBT+ community for more than 15 years, an entrepreneur, and an excellent sister.
> 
> The death of Giseel Guzmán (born Darwin Said Aguilar ), a renowned trans leader and defender of the rights of sexual diversity in Honduras, has generated dismay among social organizations and activists who remember her as a figure committed to the defense of the LGBTIQ+ community.
> 
> Giseel, 31, was part of the Violeta Collective Association for more than 15 years , where she actively participated in processes of accompaniment, advocacy and defense of human rights.
> 
> In a public statement, organizations such as Colectivo Violeta, Transfénix and LARBY's Artemis lamented the murder of their colleague and expressed solidarity with her family, friends and members of the collective.
> 
> "We deeply regret the murder of our leader Giseel Aguilar Centeno, who was part of our organization for more than 15 years. We stand in solidarity with the pain of her family, friends, and colleagues," the statement reads.
> 
> Giseel Guzmán's younger sister revealed details about her life while waiting to receive her body to say her final goodbye.
> 
> "She was a very good person, she paid for all my studies ," she tearfully told a local news outlet.
> 
> According to his family member's testimony, Guzmán was dedicated to renting apartments  and was also starting new projects to generate income.
> 
> "She had her tenement ... now she was doing some kind of business to support herself from there," he commented.
> 
> The activist's body was found early Friday morning, March 13, in the return tunnel of the El Carrizal neighborhood in Tegucigalpa, the capital of Honduras.
> 
> According to the preliminary report from the National Police, the body was found around 4:00 a.m. during a preventive patrol.
> 
> Authorities indicated that he had several visible injuries and that evidence was collected at the scene as part of the investigation.
> 
> In the same statement, the groups expressed their concern about the increase in violence against people of diverse sexual orientations in the country.
> 
> According to the Human Rights Observatory of LGBTIQ+ People , Honduras faces a critical situation regarding violence against this population.
> 
> "Violent deaths against LGBTIQ+ people represent a historic and persistent humanitarian crisis, with at least 40 documented homicides in 2025 ," the report cited in the statement indicates.
> 
> They explained that these attacks are usually characterized by high levels of violence, which they consider evidence of crimes motivated by prejudice and discrimination.
> 
> They also noted that so far in 2026 there have been at least four violent deaths of LGBTIQ+ people , the most recent being that of Aguilar Centeno.
> 
> In response to this, the groups demanded that the authorities, especially the Public Prosecutor's Office and the investigative bodies, conduct a prompt investigation with a gender perspective.
> 
> "We demand a swift investigation and punishment for the crimes and violent deaths committed against LGBTIQ+ people. We hope that the case of Giseel Aguilar Centeno will not be added to the percentage of impunity," they stated.



> Entrepreneurial and 'respectful': that's how Sady, the trans woman killed in El Carrizal, was described.
> Darwin Said Aguilar Centeno, a 31-year-old trans person, was found dead in a tunnel in El Carrizal; family members demand justice.
> Radio HRN Newsroom
> March 13, 2026 11:17
> 
> Darwin Said Aguilar Centeno , known in the community as Sady Giseel , 31 years old, was identified after being found dead in a return tunnel in the El Carrizal sector , north of Tegucigalpa.
> 
> According to his sister, Darwin left the La Fátima neighborhood on the night of Thursday, March 12, heading towards the 21 de Febrero sector in Tegucigalpa.
> 
> Furthermore, he confirmed to HRN that Darwin owned a boarding house and was about to start a new business.
> 
> The discovery has generated shock in the LGBTQ+ community and among residents of the capital, who remember Darwin as a respectful and active person within his environment.
> 
> In the early hours of March 13, his body was found in the El Carrizal return tunnel, dressed in women's clothing, a black dress and an ochre-colored jacket.
> 
> Later, his body was taken to the Center for Legal Medicine and Forensic Sciences for an autopsy to determine the cause of death. Preliminary reports from the National Police indicate that the death was due to asphyxiation by strangulation.
> 
> Darwin's family demanded justice for this tragic event and are awaiting the medical-legal report that will determine the cause and manner of death.
> 
> It was indicated that the forensic report will be transferred to the Prosecutor's Office for Crimes Against Life , which is responsible for investigating homicides in the country.
