> Dentro de un bar le quitan la vida a mujer trans en Choluteca
> The victim was socializing with other people when unknown individuals attacked her with gunfire.
> 
> More details have emerged about Solange Herrera, a trans woman who was murdered yesterday afternoon, Monday, inside a bar located in the nightlife area of the city of Choluteca, Choluteca, in southern Honduras.
> 
> According to reports from the scene, she was socializing at the establishment when suddenly two men arrived on a motorcycle, armed and without saying a word, began shooting at her.
> 
> Due to the number of gunshot wounds, Solange died instantly. Her body lay on the floor near one of the bar benches, in a pool of blood.
> 
> According to reports, Solange was originally from the village of Nuevos Colorados, also in the city of Choluteca. Her death has caused outrage among the LGBTQ+ community.
> 
> National Police officers arrived at the bar where the crime occurred to cordon off the scene and collect evidence. They also coordinated with the Forensic Medicine unit for the legal procedures involving the body.
> 
> Members of the LGBTIQ+ community also appeared, who were upset and outraged by what happened to their colleague Solange Herrera.
> 
> One of those present stated that they did not want the body to be transferred to Tegucigalpa, because Solange's family is of limited means and the procedures for removing the body from Forensic Medicine and transferring it to Choluteca would be expensive for them.
> 
> During the removal of the body, a discussion arose between family members and police officers and prosecutors, as they opposed the transfer of the body to the capital. However, the legal process requires an autopsy to determine further details about the murder.
> 
> Municipal authorities in Choluteca also proceeded with the closure of the bar where the violent incident occurred.
> 
> According to the Catrachas Lesbian Network observatory, since 2022 there have been 170 violent deaths of members of the LGBTIQ+ community, and with this one in Choluteca the number rises to 171.
> 
> In Honduras, the violent deaths of members of the LGBTIQ+ community continue to be a serious and persistent problem, marked by high levels of violence and impunity.
> 
> The figures reflect a sustained pattern of attacks, where the main victims are usually gay men and trans women, often in contexts of discrimination and structural violence.
