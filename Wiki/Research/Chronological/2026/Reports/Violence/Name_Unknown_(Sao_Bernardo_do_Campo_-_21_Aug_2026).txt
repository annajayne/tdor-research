> The president of Casa Neon Cunha suspects that the transgender woman found in a suitcase in ABCD is being assisted by the NGO.
> A decapitated body was found in São Bernardo do Campo; official identification depends on forensic analysis by the Civil Police.
> Published 22/08/2026
> 
> 
> The president and founder of Casa Neon Cunha, Paulo Araújo, suspects that the transgender woman found decapitated inside a suitcase in São Bernardo do Campo is one of the people assisted by the institution. Official identification has not yet been confirmed.
> 
> In an interview with TV SBC and ABCD Jornal, Paulo stated that the suspicion arose mainly from the characteristics found on the body, which were similar to those of the woman assisted by the shelter.
> 
> The body was found on Friday (21), in a wooded area on Avenida Ângelo Demarchi, between the Batistini and Demarchi neighborhoods. According to the City Hall, the GCM was called by the Operational Control Center (CCO) after a report and preserved the site for the work of the forensics team.
> 
> The Public Security Secretariat (SSP) reported that a pedestrian passing through the area smelled a strong odor and alerted the police. The body, still without official identification, was inside a suitcase. The case was registered as a homicide at the Specialized Criminal Investigations Department (Deic) in São Bernardo, which is conducting investigations to identify the victim and clarify the crime.
> 
> Initially, authorities did not confirm the victim's sex. Preliminary information obtained by the news report indicated that the body had silicone breast implants.
> 
> Despite the evidence provided by Casa Neon, confirmation that the body is that of the woman assisted by the institution depends on official identification. The Civil Police remain responsible for the investigation.
> 
> SSP NOTE
> “An unidentified body was found inside a suitcase in the early afternoon of this Friday (21), in the Batistini neighborhood, in São Bernardo do Campo. According to the police report, a pedestrian passing by the place smelled a strong odor and alerted the Municipal Civil Guard, who went to the address and located the body. The forensics team was called. The case was registered as a homicide at the Specialized Criminal Investigations Department (Deic) of São Bernardo do Campo, which is carrying out investigations to identify the victim and clarify the facts.”
