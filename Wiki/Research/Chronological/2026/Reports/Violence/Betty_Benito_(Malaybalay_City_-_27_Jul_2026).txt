> Transgender woman found dead in Bukidnon
> Published Jul 28, 2026 03:39 pm
> 
> Police are investigating the murder of a 23-year-old transgender woman in Malaybalay City, Bukidnon.
> 
> The victim was identified as Neilbert “Betty” Donggo Benito of Barangay Manalog, Malaybalay City.
> 
> Police said the victim, a pageant contestant, was last seen alive with a man.
> 
> The man is now considered a person of interest.
> 
> Benito was later found dead with a bloodied head, sprawled on the ground, underwear pulled down, and private parts exposed in Barangay Sumpong on Monday, July 27.
> 
> The victim, a college student, was taken to a hospital where Benito was declared dead.
> 
> The Society of Trans Women of the Philippines (STRAP) mourned and urged justice for the victim.
