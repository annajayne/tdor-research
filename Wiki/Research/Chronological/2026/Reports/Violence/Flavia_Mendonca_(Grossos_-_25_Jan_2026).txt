> Trans woman shot dead on Grossos beach.
> The crime caused outrage among residents and visitors to the area, which is known for its high traffic.
> On 27/01/2026 17:18    
> 
> A trans woman was shot dead on Sunday night (25), on the beach in the municipality of Grossos, located in the Costa Branca region of Rio Grande do Norte. The crime caused commotion among residents and people who were at the scene at the time of the incident.
> 
> The victim was identified as Flávia Mendonça. According to initial reports, she was hit by several gunshots and did not survive her injuries, dying at the scene before medical help arrived.
> 
> The Military Police were called immediately after the crime and cordoned off the area to preserve the scene and ensure the safety of the people present. At this time, there is no information about the perpetrator or the motive for the homicide.
> 
> The Scientific Police and the Civil Police of Mossoró were called to carry out the forensic examination at the scene and to remove the body to the Technical-Scientific Institute of Expertise (ITEP).
> 
> The case will be investigated by the authorities, with the aim of clarifying the circumstances of the crime, identifying those responsible and determining the motive for the murder.




> A 37-year-old trans woman was shot dead on Sunday night (26) in the city of Grossos , on the Costa Branca coast of Rio Grande do Norte. The victim was identified as Flávia Mendonça.
> 
> According to the Military Police, the victim was in a bar in a location known as Prainha when two suspects arrived wearing helmets and approached her.
> 
> The victim noticed the criminals approaching, tried to run, but was caught and shot multiple times, according to the police. The woman died instantly.
> 
> The motive for the crime was unknown to the police. After the crime, the suspects fled. As of Monday morning (26), no suspects had been arrested.
> 
> The case is now being investigated by the Civil Police.
