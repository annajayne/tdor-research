> Trans woman, known as Reina Mengueña, murdered in Chontales
> She was very popular in his hometown of Santo Domingo, Chontales. The crime occurred early Monday morning.
> 
> Known as la Reina Mengueña, a 26-year-old transgender woman was brutally stabbed to death early Monday morning in Santo Domingo, Chontales department. The case is under investigation by the National Police.
> 
> Authorities have launched an operation to identify those responsible for the crime, which is already being investigated as a possible hate crime. 
> 
> According to the news site Punto Noticioso TV, the victim was stabbed in the left side of the chest, a wound that proved fatal.
> 
> A video posted on the Facebook page Santo Domingo Activo shows the last moments of Reina Mengueña's life. The footage shows her walking along one of the streets of the municipality, visibly injured and covered in blood. According to preliminary information, her body was found hours later on a street in downtown Santo Domingo.
> 
> There are still no clear clues about the perpetrator of the attack or the motives that triggered the tragedy.
> 
> The incident has caused consternation in the town where she grew up. Known as the Queen of Mengueña, she was a well-known figure within the LGBT community for her charisma and her constant participation in equestrian events and festivities.
> 
> "When we got downtown, he was already dead. We used to go out dancing; I'm a neighbor, and we're asking for justice because he didn't bother anyone," Aleyda Mercado Suárez told Punto Noticioso TV.
> 
> On social media, they invited people to the wake for Hernaldo Iván Hernández Obando, which was the legal name of the Queen of Mengueña.
> 
> Among the first reactions after his death was that of the Toros de Chontales baseball team, which last year became runner-up in the Germán Pomares 2025 National Championship. The Queen of Mengueña was a prominent fan of this team, which today deeply mourns her passing.
> 
> "The board of directors and the Toros de Chontales team send our most sincere condolences to the family and loved ones in these moments of mourning," reads the post shared on their Facebook page.
> 
> The Waning Queen also maintained an active presence on social media. She was known for her affinity with the Sandinista National Liberation Front (FSLN) and had created a YouTube channel, where she posted a video seven months ago titled "Trying Food from Chinese Stores ," which reached 918 views.
> 
> On her official Facebook page, which has 7,700 followers, a statement confirming the death of the trans woman was posted. In a moving statement, they highlighted "her unparalleled charisma and humility."
> 
> "Today we mourn her passing, but we take comfort in knowing that she leaves behind a legacy of joy, simplicity, and love for her people. Some people pass through life, and some people leave a mark. Reina Mengueña left an indelible mark," the post reads.
