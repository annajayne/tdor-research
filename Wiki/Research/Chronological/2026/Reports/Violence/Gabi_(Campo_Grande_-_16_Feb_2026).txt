> Transgender woman shot by police officer dies on her birthday in Campo Grande.
> Gabriela dos Santos, 27, was a drug user and had previously been admitted to rehabilitation clinics due to her addiction.
> 16/02/2026 22:46
>
>
> Gabriela dos Santos, the 27-year-old transsexual woman who was shot by a military police officer on Avenida Calógeras, died hours later, this Monday (16), in Campo Grande. Today was Gabriela's birthday, according to the victim's brother, roofer Vitor de Paula Rodrigues do Nascimento.
> 
> Shaken, the young man recounted that his sister was a drug user. The addiction worsened after the siblings lost their mother.
> 
> "She'd been in that situation for about four or five years. She had already been in clinics, at the Clínica da Alma, and had even been to São Paulo. I did everything I could to get her out of that life, but she didn't want to. She spent a lot of time at Cetremi, and the people at Cetremi really liked her."
> 
> A friend of the victim told Vitor that Gabriela was working and was going to celebrate her birthday. Vitor says he even took her to live with him in Terenos, but Gabriela couldn't overcome her withdrawal symptoms. The roofer laments the loss and the context of violence his sister found herself in because of her drug addiction.
> 
> “She went out to celebrate her birthday, and then this tragedy happens. It’s complicated, I always told her: ‘Let’s get out of this life, sis, let’s get out of this life, because the way you’re going…’. She had a very short fuse, she wasn’t afraid of anyone.”
> 
> In his testimony, the military police officer who shot Gabriela said he fired three times at her. However, the victim's brother claims that he heard from the doctor who treated her at the emergency care unit that the victim was shot four times.
> 
> ATTMS (Association of Transvestites and Transsexuals of Mato Grosso do Sul) released a lengthy statement lamenting Gabriela's death and demanding a proper investigation of the case.



> The transgender woman shot in downtown Campo Grande on Monday afternoon, the 16th, died before even arriving at the Santa Casa hospital . She suffered at least three cardiorespiratory arrests after being shot by a Military Police officer who allegedly tried to restrain her because she had appropriated another officer's weapon during a confrontation. In total, there were 4 shots, which hit her abdomen, right leg, and right hip.
> 
> According to information obtained by Jornal Midiamax , the shooting occurred during routine patrols by military police on Travessa Lydia Bais. The officers were reportedly surprised by homeless individuals who began attacking the team with stones. A physical altercation then ensued between the suspects and the police.
> 
> At one point during the altercation, which extended to the intersection of Avenida Calógeras and Rua 15 de Novembro, the trans woman allegedly grabbed one of the police officers' weapons and pointed it at him. At that moment, another officer shot her.
> 
> The Fire Department arrived at the scene to provide initial assistance. While still on the ground, the gunshot victim went into cardiac arrest twice, but was stabilized by the rescue team.
> 
> They headed towards the Santa Casa hospital, and according to the report, within a few blocks the trans woman suffered further cardiac complications, passing away before even reaching the hospital.
