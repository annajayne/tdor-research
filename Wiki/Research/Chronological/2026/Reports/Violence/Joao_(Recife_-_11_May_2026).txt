> A transgender man identified as João was brutally murdered on Rua São Mateus, in Recife, on Monday night (11).
> 
> According to residents, armed men arrived in two cars and fired several rifle shots at the victim. At the scene, police found rifle casings, .40 caliber pistol casings, drugs and R$ 570 in cash. Audio shared by residents report between 15 and 20 shots during the execution.
> 
> The case is being investigated by the Department of Homicides and Protection of Persons (DHPP), which is investigating whether the crime is related to a possible settling of accounts linked to drug trafficking.
