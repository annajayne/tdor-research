> The murder of a trans woman, legally identified as Marvin Alejandro Martínez Leal, 31, which occurred on the afternoon of Thursday
> 
> According to the Valledupar Metropolitan Police, the incident occurred around 5:15 p.m. at the intersection of Carrera 48 and Calle 6-8, where the victim was attacked with a firearm by an individual on a motorcycle who, according to witnesses, fired indiscriminately before fleeing the scene. The victim was taken to the Eduardo Arredondo Daza Hospital, La Nevada branch, where he arrived without vital signs.
> 
> The victim, known in the community as 'la Peli', was a trans woman of Venezuelan nationality, who had arrived in the capital of Cesar approximately three months ago and was carrying out informal activities in the area at the time of the attack.
> 
> Following the murder, Yuliana Ángel Sanabria, director of the Prisma Movimiento association and a defender of the rights of the sexually diverse population and women in all their diversity, asserted that this incident cannot be considered isolated. “This should raise alarm bells. Another woman had already been murdered in the same area. It is urgent that security councils adopt an inclusive approach and ensure the participation of human rights defenders,” she stated.
> 
> Ángel Sanabria warned that, although development plans include actions, these do not translate into real protection measures. “Today we see that women and LGBTIQ+ people are not receiving effective protection from local governments. The actions remain mere statistics and do not become concrete strategies to prevent violence,” he stated.
> 
> The community leader called on local authorities to strengthen security policies with a differentiated approach, while also warning about the risks faced by women from all social backgrounds. “It’s not just trans women who are in danger; today all women, in all their diversity, are at risk,” she emphasized.
> 
> The ombudsperson also indicated that, for security reasons, the organizations have not been able to travel to the area where the crime occurred, and that they are awaiting official information from the Ombudsman's Office and the Metropolitan Police, with whom they are providing support for the case, including the corresponding humanitarian assistance because it involves a migrant.
> 
> Meanwhile, the National Police reported that SIJIN units and the quadrant surveillance model are making progress in collecting material evidence and in the investigations, in coordination with the Attorney General's Office, to clarify the motives for the homicide and identify those responsible.



> At approximately 5:40 p.m. on Thursday, January 15, a transgender person was murdered with a firearm in the La Nevada neighborhood , in Valledupar .
> 
> According to preliminary information provided by the community, the victim, who has not yet been identified, was selling gasoline when he was attacked by unknown individuals who shot him repeatedly.
> 
> Seriously injured, she was transferred to the Eduardo Arredondo Daza Hospital, La Nevada branch , where she was admitted without vital signs.
> 
> Authorities arrived at the scene and are conducting investigations to clarify this homicide, which has once again shaken the sector in less than 48 hours, following the murder of Ferminia Pupo on the morning of Wednesday, January 14.
