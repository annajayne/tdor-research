> Black Trans Woman Davonta Curtis Killed in Her Chicago Apartment
> Deandre Bell, 24, allegedly confessed to killing Curtis and stealing her money, phone and car.
> April 15, 2026
> Davonta Curtis, a Black transgender woman from Chicago, was beaten to death in her apartment on the night of April 5. She was 31 years old.
> 
> Police have arrested Curtis’ alleged killer Deandre Bell, 24, and charged him this week with first-degree murder and possession of a stolen vehicle, according to CW news affiliate WGN. Initial media reports of Curtis’ death last week misgendered her as a man.
> 
> Bell, who according to court documents was Curtis’ on-again, off-again boyfriend, allegedly Googled “how to kill someone with a hammer” and “if a person gets hit in the head with a hammer while sleeping what happens” before murdering Curtis, the Chicago Sun-Times reported on Tuesday. Bell and Curtis were seen on video together earlier in the day on April 5, when the two visited Curtis’ sister’s home to drop off Easter supplies, per WGN. They later returned to Curtis’ apartment, with Bell seen on surveillance footage leaving the building alone around 1 a.m.
> 
> Curtis’ sister found her body on April 8 and notified police, who say they found Bell in possession of Curtis’ car and keys, as well as a bloody hammer. Bell allegedly confessed to killing Curtis and taking her money, phone, and car, per the Sun-Times. An examination by the Cook County Medical Examiner’s office found that Curtis died due to multiple blunt force injuries.
> 
> Prosecutors have not put forth a motive for Curtis’ murder, but said in court that she was a sex worker in addition to her possible romantic relationship with Bell, the Sun-Times reported. Three out of four trans sex workers report experiencing sexual or intimate partner violence at some point during their lifetime, per the Transgender Law Center.
> 
> Bell was ordered held in jail pending trial this week. A first-degree murder charge in Illinois carries a possible sentence between 20 and 60 years in prison.
> 
> Curtis’ murder is at least the second violent killing of a Black trans or gender-nonconforming person this year. In March, activist and drag performer Shyyell Diamond Sanchez-McCray was shot and killed in Virginia, but no arrests have been announced since.



> A Thursday autopsy found a 31-year-old man was fatally assaulted in a Garfield Park neighborhood apartment, officials said.
> 
> Police found Davonta F. Curtis unresponsive with a gunshot wound to the head inside a home in the 3100 block of West Madison Street about 9:15 a.m. Wednesday, Chicago police said.
> 
> But Curtis, who was pronounced dead on the scene, died of multiple blunt force injuries during an assault, according to the Cook County medical examiner’s office. Curtis, whose death was ruled a homicide, was of the 2600 block of South Kedvale Avenue, the office said.
