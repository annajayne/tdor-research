> Homicide in a barbershop in Durango
> Ivonne worked as a sex worker through digital platforms. / Photo: Kathia Gamero / El Sol de Durango
> Follow us on:
> Joshael Rojas
> 
> Originally from San Dimas, she left her municipality a decade ago to escape discrimination; she died without that right being guaranteed in Durango.
> 
> Ivonne was 34 years old and a decade ago she had arrived in the city of Durango with the intention of getting away from the discrimination she faced in her home municipality and starting a new stage of her life as a trans woman . She was looking for an environment where she could advance in her transition with greater peace of mind, far from the transphobia that had marked part of her history.
> 
> Her life was largely spent away from the public spaces of the LGBTQ+ community, but she was not completely disconnected from the organizations that support trans people. Months before her death, she went to Casa Arcoíris to request information about changing her legal identity, a process that would allow her to have documents in accordance with the woman she was.
> 
> Tadeo Campagne, LGBTQ+ rights activist and director of the organization , recalled that he met Ivonne approximately ten years ago and is now the voice that tells us a little about her.
> 
> Tadeo explained that Ivonne did not usually participate closely in community activities , a situation that, he said, may also be related to the discrimination faced by trans women engaged in sex work , even within their own social environments.
> 
> Ivonne worked as a sex worker through digital platforms. Tadeo clarified that this activity should not be used to judge her or diminish her dignity, and maintained that the lack of educational and employment opportunities places many trans people in situations of exclusion.
> 
> “It’s not that sex work isn’t undignified, it’s just not the best option and the dangers they are exposed to, like these, are serious,” the activist said.
> 
> Regarding her family environment, Campagne indicated that, as far as he knew, Ivonne went through difficulties with her family during the first years, although in more recent times she had regained a close relationship with them.
> 
> That is one of the few details known about her life outside of support spaces: a trans woman who arrived in the capital young, who sought to build a life in accordance with her identity and who was still trying to resolve the legal recognition of who she was.
> 
> For the activist , the fact that Ivonne continues to be referred to as a man after her death represents yet another form of violence and discrimination . Therefore, he asked that publications and the institutional treatment of the case respect her identity, without the name that appears in official documents becoming a reason to ignore who she was.
> 
> Campagne described the murder as a transfeminicide and a hate crime, and questioned the arguments that, according to her account, the alleged perpetrator expressed to justify the attack . She also warned that this is not an isolated incident, recalling that for years the organization has documented serious attacks against trans women in Durango and mentioned the recent case of a trans person threatened with a firearm, whose crime, according to her account, was classified as threats.
> 
> “And nothing justifies violence of that level and the murder of a person ,” he stated.
> 
> Campagne asked the State Congress to move forward with the classification of hate crimes , the creation of specialized mechanisms to address crimes against LGBTQ+ people , and legislation that allows for the legal recognition of the identity of trans people.
> 
> In her opinion, omissions in education, employment and access to justice keep this population in vulnerable conditions. She also raised the need to regulate sex work without criminalizing it and to guarantee safety and services to those who practice it, whether or not they belong to the LGBTQ+ community.
> 
> Alan Espinosa de la O , a councilman from the Citizen Movement party, agreed that the lack of legislative progress continues to create obstacles for transgender people, both in the recognition of their identity and in their access to employment and education.
> 
> Currently in the State Congress is the reform that would allow transgender people to have an identity that aligns with their personality. Alan Espinosa
> According to the councilman, legal recognition is a starting point for trans people to be able to carry out different procedures and access without their documents becoming a barrier.
> 
> "We're talking about the fact that in Mexico City there's already recognition of trans femicides . We must start by having the State Congress recognize trans people and their gender identity. It's the very least we can do," she said.
> 
> Espinosa de la O also pointed out that workplace discrimination is not limited to public institutions, but also involves companies and society at large. Without documents recognizing their identity , she explained, transgender people may face greater difficulties entering the private sector or continuing their studies.
> 
> “Without an identity that matches your true self, it is very difficult to find a job ,” he said, warning that such exclusion can deepen economic and social segregation.
> 
> The activist also recalled that the average life expectancy of trans women in Mexico has been estimated at around 35 to 37 years , Ivonne died at 34 , an age that, he argued, reflects the severity of the conditions of violence and exclusion that the authorities must address.






> The LGBTQ+ community of Durango expressed great dismay at the news of the murder of Scarlet, a trans girl originally from San Dimas, for whom they demand justice and respect for her identity.
> 
> From Casa Arcoíris, headquarters of the National Observatory of Hate Crimes, activist Tadeo Campagne Escalante stated that there is a lot of anger and frustration over the crime that occurred in the early hours of October 29th, in a barbershop in the city.
> 
> She called for an end to hate speech on social media and for respect for the identity of the person who identified as a woman, a 34-year-old trans woman from the municipality of San Dimas.
> 
> Campagne highlighted the need to legislate in Durango on issues related to the LGBT community, including the creation of a specialized prosecutor's office to handle crimes involving people from this community.
> 
> “What happened today was a transfemicide, what happened today was a hate crime. Today in the State Congress there are initiatives related to this issue that are stalled, to classify hate crimes and transfemicides as such, as transfemicides of a trans woman from the LGBT community,” she stated.
> 
> He pointed out that Scarlet was a sex worker, but that does not justify any act of violence with this degree of cruelty and malice.
> 
> “I saw the alleged statements of the alleged aggressor, the murderer, who justified this brutal, sadistic attack—because that is the correct word—by saying that she had identified herself as a trans woman and had deceived him. I believe that is not a justification of any kind for committing such a sadistic, violent act against a person.”
> 
> She emphasized that this is a transfeminicide and a hate crime , hence the demand to all three levels of government to pay attention, as it is an issue whose attention has been demanded for months.
> 
> “This is not the first time that the LGBT community has demanded the classification of hate crimes, femicide, and the creation of this prosecutor's office, precisely because we understand the problems suffered by our colleagues who belong to the LGBT community, particularly trans women,” she said.
> 
> She pointed out the stark and cruel statistic that the average life expectancy for transgender women in this country is 35 years, while Scarlet was 34. She added that most murders of transgender women are just as violent, aggressive, and sadistic.
> 
> The activist called for an end to the criminalization of sex workers , who have also expressed shock and fear over the incident. He added that several complaints related to attacks against transgender women and members of the LGBT community have already been filed in Durango this year.





> A 34-year-old transgender woman, identified as Jairo Iovane, was murdered early Thursday morning inside a barbershop in Durango City. A 25-year-old man was arrested in connection with the crime and is being held at the disposal of the Public Prosecutor's Office.
> 
> According to initial reports from local media, the establishment is located in the Puerta de San Ignacio neighborhood, near the Eucaliptos housing development, at the intersection of Isla Ángel de la Guarda and Isla Cozumel streets. Some reports identify the business as Barber Shelby.
> 
> The victim was originally from Tayoltita, the municipal seat of San Dimas, and was found dead inside the shop with head injuries, presumably caused by a sharp object.
> 
> According to local media reports, emergency services were called in the morning after a woman discovered the situation at the barbershop.
> 
> Paramedics from the State Emergency Medical System confirmed that Jairo had no vital signs. Subsequently, forensic personnel from the State Attorney General's Office carried out the corresponding procedures and transferred the body to the morgue for the legally required autopsy.
> 
> The alleged perpetrator was apprehended by municipal police officers and turned over to the Public Prosecutor's Office, which will be responsible for clarifying the circumstances of the death and determining his possible criminal liability.






> Trans woman murdered in a barbershop in Durango, who was she and why was she killed?
> A transgender woman was murdered on the morning of Thursday, October 29th, and this is what is known about the case that occurred in a barbershop in Durango.
> October 29, 2026, 07:55
> 
> On the morning of Thursday, October 29, 2026, a transgender woman was murdered . The incident occurred at a well-known barbershop in the city, located in the Puertas de San Ignacio neighborhood, in the capital city.
> 
> According to the information obtained, this young woman was murdered after being brutally attacked by a man inside a business that operates as a barbershop and is called "Barber Shelby".
> 
> The events occurred around 3 a.m. when Karla realized that her husband, Erick Israel, had not yet arrived home, so she called his phone without getting an answer. Worried, she decided to locate him through GPS, finding him still at his workplace, at "Barber Shelby". 
> 
> At that moment, she decides to go to the location, and on the way, she receives a call from her husband telling her that there had been a serious accident. When she arrives, the man turns off the lights, opens the door for Karla, and explains that there is a dead body inside.
> 
> He told him that he was giving him a haircut and he refused to pay, then "became aggressive" to the point of taking scissors and physically assaulting him, so he defended himself and began to struggle, causing head injuries , at which point the now deceased fell to the floor.
> 
> Upon arriving at the scene, Karla and Erick Israel discussed the matter, and she convinced him to report what had happened to 911 around 6:00 a.m. When officers arrived, they found the crime scene and began their investigation, where the deceased was identified as  34-year-old Jairo Iovane .
> 
> According to the report, Jairo Iovane is a trans woman who was probably a sex worker and is originally from Tayoltita San Dimas, Durango. She was allegedly murdered by Erick Israel over payment for a barber service . 
> 
> The agents found in one of the rooms of this business the lifeless body of Jairo Iovane, who had injuries caused by a bladed weapon in the left parietal region and the body was lying in a pool of blood .
> 
> Because of the events, Erick Israel , who was also injured, was arrested and taken to the facilities of the Attorney General's Office of the State of Durango, to be placed at the disposal of the Public Prosecutor , for the responsibility that may result.
> 
> Meanwhile, the Public Ministry ordered the Forensic Medical Service to remove and transfer the body of Jairo Iovane to the morgue of the Attorney General's Office of the State of Durango for the legal autopsy to determine the cause of death.
