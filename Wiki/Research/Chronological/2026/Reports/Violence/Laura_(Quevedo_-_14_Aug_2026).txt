> A trans woman was murdered and her remains were found in two locations in Quevedo.
> A trans woman, identified by the organization with the protected name of Laura, was murdered and her remains were found in two locations in the Venus del Río Quevedo parish, in the Quevedo canton, Los Ríos province.
> 
> According to a bulletin issued on August 15 by the Runa Sipiy Observatory of the Silueta X Association , the discovery occurred on Friday, August 14, around noon. The organization indicates that the events likely took place between the night of Thursday, August 13, and the morning of Friday.
> 
> The first package was located in the 20 de Febrero cooperative. Hours later, residents of the Gritos de Libertad sector found a sack on a bridge, from which blood was seeing. Upon examining its contents, the presence of human remains was confirmed.
> 
> According to testimonies gathered by the Observatory, individuals traveling in a van with their faces reportedly covered abandoned one of the bundles and immediately left the scene. The organization preliminarily maintains that the victim is a transgender woman and is not originally from the area.
> 
> Runa Sipiy preliminarily classifies the act as a transfemicide, under a profile related to organized crime or contract killing. Among the elements mentioned are the distribution of the remains in two locations, the use of a pickup truck, the concealment of the faces of the alleged perpetrators, and the decapitation.
> 
> However, these are preliminary assessments by the Observatory and do not equate to a judicial conclusion regarding the authorship or the definitive circumstances of the crime.
> 
> The bulletin also notes that, as of August 15, no formal investigation had been opened. The organization is demanding a response from the authorities and is requesting that the victim be fully identified through appropriate forensic procedures.
> 
> Organization investigation demands
> Diane Rodríguez, president of the Silueta X Association and director of the Runa Sipiy Observatory, asked the Prosecutor's Office to open a case, appoint a specialized prosecutor and move forward in identifying the victim.
> 
> "What they did to Laura is what the Ecuadorian State allows to happen when it doesn't investigate, when it doesn't register, when it doesn't protect ," said Rodríguez, who also asked that the case be investigated.
> 
> CoaliTrans, which brings together 22 collectives, also demanded information about the operations carried out in Quevedo and that trans women be considered within the protection protocols for vulnerable groups.
> 
> Federación Ecuatoriana de Organizaciones LGBT+,, for its part, joined the call for identification of the victim, dignified delivery of her remains and clarification of the crime.
> 
> The Observatory also announced that it will request that the case be monitored by national and international human rights organizations.
