> ADVERTISING
> Police news – A body with signs of violence was found on Tuesday morning (17) in a stream located behind the headquarters of Manaus Previdência (Manausprev), on Avenida Constantino Nery, Chapada neighborhood, South-Central zone of Manaus.
> 
> According to initial reports, the victim showed signs of violence and was found with her hair shaved. There are also preliminary reports that she is a transgender woman, information that will still need to be officially confirmed by the authorities.
> 
> The call was registered at around 8:57 am, after the presence of the body was reported by people in the area. Local employees tried to prevent the body from being carried away by the stream's current until rescue teams arrived.
> 
> The case is being treated as the discovery of a body with signs of violence.
> 
> The Military Police and the Civil Police were called and cordoned off the area for forensic procedures. The case was forwarded to the 22nd Integrated Police District (DIP), which will be responsible for the investigation.
> 
> The body will be removed by the Forensic Medical Institute (IML), where it will undergo examinations to confirm the victim's identity and clarify the cause of death, as well as officially verify the existence of injuries and possible signs of aggression.
> 
> At this time, there is no confirmation regarding the details of the case or the victim's identity. The investigation is ongoing.





> Employees find body floating in a stream on Avenida Constantino Nery

> Manaus/AM – A body was found this Wednesday morning (17) behind the Bosque Grill restaurant, in a stream located on Avenida Constantino Nery, Chapada neighborhood, in the Central-South zone of the capital.
> 
> The victim, who has not yet been identified, had a shaved head and was wearing a black shirt and black shorts. Employees in the area located the body and alerted the authorities.
> 
> One of them had to hold onto the body to prevent it from being swept away by the current of the stream until the police arrived. The Institute of Forensic Medicine (IML) was at the scene, conducted the forensic examination, and removed the body.
> 
> The incident is under the responsibility of the 22nd Interactive Police Station (22DIP), which will continue the investigations.
