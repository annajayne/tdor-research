> Four Arrested Over Brutal Murder of Bodies Poured in Concrete in Phnom Penh
> Two Chinese and two Cambodians have been arrested for committing the murder of a transgender woman and taxi driver whose bodies were buried under concrete in Phnom Penh’s Chbar Ampov district in May
> Wed, 03 Jun 2026 - 9:43 AM(GMT+7)
> 
> Phnom Penh police have arrested four suspects, including two Chinese nationals accused of the kidnapping and premeditated murder of a transgender woman and a taxi driver, whose bodies were discovered buried beneath concrete in the capital’s Chbar Ampov district on May 23.
> 
> A June 3 post on Phnom Penh Police’s Facebook page, named the suspects, who were arrested on June 1, as Sokha Dani, 27, Yun Kimhorn, 40, Wong Yenlong, 31, and Yu Weiming, 26. On the same day as their arrest, the suspects confessed to the crimes during questioning, leading investigators to a site where the bodies of the two victims were recovered that day.​
> 
> The victims have been identified as Taing Simeng, 35, a transgender woman who worked at a karaoke venue, and 36-year-old taxi driver, Em Kim Srun. The crime took place in Koh Krabey village, Prek Thmey commune, Chbar Ampov district, on May 23, 2026 at 23.30pm.
> 
> Police said the victims' hands and feet had been bound before they were beaten and killed. The suspects buried the bodies together in a pit and poured concrete over them on a plot of land in Prek Leap commune. The taxi driver's vehicle was later found abandoned at a landfill site in Prek Ek, Chbar Ampov district.
> 
> An autopsy concluded that Kim Srun died from a severe wound cut to his left leg, while Simeng died from knife attacks. Authorities classified both deaths as murder.
> 
> Police said they seized several items linked to the crime, including a wooden stick, an axe, electrical wires and an iPhone. Currently police are investigating any remaining accomplices in the case.
