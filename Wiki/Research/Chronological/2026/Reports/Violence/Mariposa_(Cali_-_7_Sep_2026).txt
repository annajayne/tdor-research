> Violence once again shook downtown Cali with the murder of a trans woman, approximately 35 years old, who was attacked with a knife in the middle of a public street.
> 
> The victim, known as 'Mariposa,' was reportedly attacked early Tuesday morning, according to preliminary information. Authorities are investigating the case to determine the exact circumstances surrounding the crime and identify the perpetrator.
> 
> One of the main hypotheses investigators are considering points to a possible altercation with another transgender woman. However, authorities will have to determine if this version of events is accurate and what triggered the attack.
> 
> The attack generated concern among those in this sector of the Valle del Cauca capital, where the victim was seriously injured after receiving knife wounds.
> 
> After being alerted to the situation, emergency services and authorities arrived at the scene. Despite the care provided, the woman died as a result of her injuries.
> 
> The investigation was handed over to the judicial authorities, who are making progress in gathering testimonies and other elements that will allow them to reconstruct the victim's last moments and establish responsibilities.
> 
> The crime also brings to the forefront once again the situation of violence faced by trans people in Colombia and the need to quickly clarify the cases in which they are victims of homicide.
> 
> For now, authorities have not publicly stated whether the murder was related to the victim's gender identity. This will need to be determined during the investigation.
