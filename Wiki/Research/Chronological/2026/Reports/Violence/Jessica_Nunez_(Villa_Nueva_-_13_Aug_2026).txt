> A homicide that occurred in the early hours of Thursday on a public road in the city of Villa Nueva, Córdoba, led to the arrest of two people from the same family, accused of being directly linked to the incident, according to the San Martín Department of the Córdoba Police.
> 
> Following an alert received at the Police Communications Center, Preventive Patrol personnel went to San Luis Street at 200 in Villa Nueva, where they found a woman very seriously injured.
> 
> The officers requested the intervention of Volunteer Firefighters to urgently transfer the victim to the Pasteur Regional Hospital in the city of Villa María, where unfortunately he died shortly after entering.
> 
> At the scene, officers from the General Directorate of Criminal Investigation and the Scientific Police worked, with the presence of the prosecutor of Multiple Jurisdiction of Villa María, Juliana Companys, who was in charge of the investigation of the case.
> 
> One of the two arrested for the murder of a trans person in the city of Villa Nueva, this Thursday.
> One of the two people arrested for the murder of a transgender person in the city of Villa Nueva, this Thursday. ( La Voz )
> Based on the initial data collected at the scene, the police identified and apprehended the alleged aggressors: Johana Mailén Rogero, 22, and her father, Cristian Oscar Rogero, 48.
> 
> According to initial findings of the investigation, there were pre-existing personal conflicts between the victim and the accused, which did not appear to be related to gender-based violence. The circumstances surrounding the incident are under investigation.
> 
> By order of prosecutor Companys, the young woman was charged as the alleged perpetrator of the crime of simple homicide (article 79 of the Penal Code), while her father was charged as an alleged necessary participant in the same crime.
> 
> Both remain housed in the Villa María city jail awaiting the corresponding judicial investigations.
> 
> According to representatives of the LGBT collective in the area, to which she belonged, the deceased person was Jessica Núñez, 44 years old, residing in Villa María.



> The Villa María Multiple Jurisdiction Prosecutor's Office No. 2, headed by Juliana Companys, arrested a 22-year-old woman and her father and charged them with the simple homicide of a transgender woman murdered in the La Floresta neighborhood of Villa Nueva. Neighbors of the victim requested police presence in the early morning hours of August 13, 2026, at 200 San Luis Street. The patrol on the scene summoned personnel from the Fire Department, the General Directorate of Criminal Investigation, and the Scientific Police, who provided assistance. The victim died upon arrival at Pasteur Hospital in Villa María. The perpetrators and the victim had pre-existing conflicts unrelated to gender-based violence and were detained at the Villa María jail at the disposal of the Prosecutor's Office.
