> She is "La Chakira de Cotija", a trans influencer from Jalisco who was murdered
> LGBTQ+ groups demanded that the case be investigated with a gender perspective and that it not go unpunished.
> July 24, 2026 18:20
> 
> A trans woman who worked as a content creator was murdered in a targeted attack in the state of Michoacán, an incident that is already being investigated by state authorities.
> 
> According to initial reports, the victim was attacked with gunfire by armed individuals , who fled the scene after committing the attack.
> 
> Security personnel and emergency services arrived at the scene, where they could only confirm that the woman no longer had vital signs. 
> 
> The Attorney General's Office of the State of Michoacán opened an investigation to clarify the homicide and identify those responsible for the crime. They also indicated that the victim was José Ignacio H.,  24 years old, who, as a result of the rains, began to be an activist in his community and started cleaning drains. 
> 
> Following the murder, LGBT+ organizations and groups condemned the events and demanded an investigation with a gender perspective , as well as consideration of the victim's gender identity throughout the process.
> 
> They also demanded that the case not go unpunished and called for greater action to stop the violence faced by the trans population in Mexico. 
> 
> Violence against trans people
> The crime occurred amid growing concern over acts of violence against LGBTQ+ individuals in various parts of the country. Human rights organizations have emphasized the need to strengthen investigations and guarantee access to justice for victims and their families. 
> 
> So far, authorities have not reported any arrests or revealed a possible motive for the attack, so the investigation continues .
