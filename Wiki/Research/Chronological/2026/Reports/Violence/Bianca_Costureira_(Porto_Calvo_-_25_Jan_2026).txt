> Murder of Bianca the Seamstress
> A trans woman identified as Bianca Costureira, 50 years old, was murdered with stab wounds last Sunday (25) in Porto Calvo, Alagoas. The crime shocked the community and raises questions about safety and respect for diversity in Brazil.
> 
> Crime Details
> According to information from the Military Police of Alagoas (PM/AL), a team from the 6th Battalion was called to investigate a homicide at a residence on Travessa Boa Vista. At the scene, the police found the woman's body with injuries caused by a bladed weapon. Witnesses reported that the crime occurred during the early morning hours.
> 
> Ongoing Investigations
> The Military Police continue to conduct investigations to clarify the case. Furthermore, the police emphasize that information regarding the perpetrator and motive of the crime can be anonymously reported through the Crime Stoppers hotline (181). Public cooperation is crucial to identifying those responsible for this tragedy.
> 
> Reflections on Violence
> This incident highlights a sad reality faced by many people in the LGBTQIA+ community. Violence, both physical and verbal, remains a constant concern, and it is necessary for society to take a stand against these practices. Respect for life and diversity must be a priority for all of us.
> 
> How to Report
> Any information that could help the investigation is crucial. If you know anything about the case, do not hesitate to contact us and help the police bring justice. The anonymous tip line (181) offers a secure and confidential channel.
> 
> Final Considerations
> The death of Bianca Costureira serves as a reminder of the need for protection and respect for the LGBTQIA+ community. May we act collectively to build a more just and equal society, where everyone can live without fear.



> A trans woman identified as Bianca, 50 years old, was stabbed to death inside her own home, in the early hours of this Sunday (25), in the municipality of Porto Calvo, in the North of Alagoas. The victim worked as a seamstress.
> 
> The crime occurred at a residence located on Travessa Boa Vista, in the Centro neighborhood. The Military Police were called in the morning following a report and found the property with the door open, showing no signs of forced entry.
> 
> Inside the house, police found the victim's body with multiple stab wounds. According to residents, the murder occurred during the early morning hours.
> 
> The area was cordoned off for forensic work, and teams from the Criminalistics Institute carried out technical procedures at the scene. Searches were conducted in the region, but no suspects have been arrested so far.
> 
> On social media, friends and acquaintances mourned Bianca's death and paid tribute to her. Some comments also recalled that the victim had reported receiving threats months earlier.
> 
> The motive for the crime is still unknown. The Civil Police have opened an investigation to determine the circumstances of the homicide and identify the perpetrator.
