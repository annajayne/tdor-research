> Man stabbed his own sister after argument in a bar.
> 01/04/2026 1:50 PM
> Updated on: April 1, 2026, 4:42 PM

> A 31-year-old man, identified as Amarildo Ramos de Souza, was arrested on Tuesday (31), suspected of killing his own sister, a trans woman, by stabbing her in the municipality of Barra, in western Bahia. The crime occurred last Friday (27) and was recorded by security cameras.
> 
> In the images, the 28-year-old victim, known as Estrelinha, is seen lying on the ground after being stabbed in front of a bar in the city.
> 
> According to witnesses, during an argument at the establishment, Estrelinha called the suspect's attention to his behavior. The assailant left, returned armed with two knives, and stabbed the victim in the chest area.
> 
> The suspect was located and arrested in the act of illegally carrying a firearm, after being found with a revolver on a street in the municipality.
> 
> Estrelinha was rescued by people who were at the scene and taken to Ana Mariani Hospital, but she did not survive her injuries.
> 
> Amarildo was taken to the Territorial Police Station (DT) in Barra, where he remains at the disposal of the Justice system.
