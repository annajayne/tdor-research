> Trans woman dies after being shot in Manizales by former mayor of a municipality in Caldas
> 2026-03-05
> 
> The violent events occurred on February 25th at Residencias Media Luna, in downtown Manizales.
> 
> A trans woman, who was shot on February 25 by the former mayor of Philadelphia, William Jairo Noreña, at a residence in downtown Manizales, died on Thursday morning (March 5) at the Caldas Hospital.
> 
> The victim, 30 years old and a native of Riohacha (La Guajira), was in delicate health, in the Intensive Care Unit (ICU) and under sedation.
> 
> The other injured man, who was shot in the buttocks, is also still in an ICU at another clinic in Manizales.
> 
> Noreña was sent to jail and on Thursday his resignation from the Commercial Sub-Management of Inficaldas was accepted.
> 
> Noreña Vásquez, 50, was sent to jail last Friday, although this does not make him guilty, and it will be a judge of the Republic who determines his responsibility.
> 
> Before this person's death, the Prosecutor's Office charged the official with attempted aggravated homicide and attempted simple homicide, which carries a minimum prison sentence of 16 years.
> 
> This process does not make him guilty. A judge will determine whether or not he is responsible for the accusations against him.
