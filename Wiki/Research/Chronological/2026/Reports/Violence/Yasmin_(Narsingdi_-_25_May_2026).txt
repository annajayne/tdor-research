> Transgender killed, another injured in Narsingdi
> Our Correspondent . Narsingdi 26 May, 2026, 01:02
> 
> The police recovered the throat slit body of a third gender and rescued another in critical condition in Raipura upazila of Narsingdi on Monday.
> 
> The deceased is Yasmin, 26, also known as Suman, from Pirijkandi village in the upazila.
> 
> The injured, identified as Chandni, 26, also known as Babu, is from the same area, and was sent to Dhaka Medical College Hospital in critical condition.
> 
> Raipura police station officer-in-charge Mujibur Rahman said that the incident occurred around midnight at a poultry farm in the Pirijkandi railgate area of Mirzapur union.
> 
> On information, the police went to the scene and recovered the body, he said, adding that multiple injury marks were found on the victim’s body.
> 
> ‘It was primarily suspected that the murder may have stemmed from previous enmity. However, the exact reason of the murder will be known after an investigation,’ Mujibur said.
> 
> The police were trying to identify those involved and determine the motive behind the killing, he said, adding that no case was filed as of Monday evening.
