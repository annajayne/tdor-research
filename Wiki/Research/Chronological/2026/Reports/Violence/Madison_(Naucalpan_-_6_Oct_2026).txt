> Authorities are investigating the transphobic murder of Madison, a young trans woman found dead in her home in Naucalpan.
> The case began to be investigated as a possible transfeminicide motivated by hate; family members suspect her ex-partner.
> Madison, 19, was found dead in Naucalpan; her family points to her ex-partner as the main suspect.
> 13.10.2026 19:45:00
> 
> 
> Madison, a 19-year-old trans woman , was found dead inside her home in the municipality of Naucalpan on October 6. 
> 
> According to her family, the body showed multiple signs of violence and her ex-partner, Alejandro Marín Moya, is identified as the main suspect in the crime.
> 
> The case is being investigated as a transfeminicide motivated by hate. 
> 
> Marcela Robledo, Madison's mother, reported that the case began to be investigated as a possible transfeminicide motivated by hate, due to the conditions in which her daughter's body was found.
> 
> The woman recounted that, upon going to identify the remains, she observed that the young woman had numerous bruises and a bruised neck, leading her to presume that she had been suffocated.
> 
> According to family members, Madison had a romantic relationship for five years with Alejandro Marín Moya, a period in which she allegedly suffered physical, emotional and psychological violence.
> 
> They also indicated that the young woman had filed a complaint for domestic violence in October 2025.
> 
> The family also stated that there are video surveillance recordings that allegedly show Alejandro leaving Madison's home on October 6, the date on which the young woman was found dead.
> 
> They report the alleged arrest and escape of the suspect.
> According to the testimony of family members , on Sunday, October 11, Alejandro Marín Moya was seen in Cuautitlán Izcalli, so they notified the authorities.
> 
> Madison's mother said they were later notified about the man's arrest; however, she claimed he escaped after being apprehended.
> 
> This weekend, family and friends said goodbye and buried Madison , while demanding justice and the clarification of her death.
> 
> The case has raised concerns about the violence faced by trans women and the need for authorities to investigate the events with a gender and sexual diversity perspective.
