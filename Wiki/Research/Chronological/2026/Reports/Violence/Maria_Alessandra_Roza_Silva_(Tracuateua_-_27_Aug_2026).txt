> A man identified by the Civil Police as the main suspect in the death of Maria Alessandra Roza Silva, a 46-year-old trans woman found dead in Tracuateua last Thursday (27), was arrested this Friday (28), during a police operation in Capanema, in the northeast of Pará.
> 
> The suspect was at a fair in that city, where he was located by the police, but ended up being taken to the Tracuateua Civil Police station , which is responsible for the investigation, and remains at the disposal of the Justice system.
> 
> Upon arriving at the city's police station, the suspect needed to be protected by the police officers, as family members and locals were outraged by the death of Maria Alessandra Roza Silva and wanted to attack the man.
> 
> Police are now investigating whether a debt of R$1,500 may be related to the death of Alessandra, who was found dead in the Vila Fátima community in Tracuateua.
> 
> Based on the initial information gathered in the case, investigators began to consider the possibility that the crime occurred during a robbery, which, in theory, constitutes a possible case of robbery resulting in death.



> The Civil Police of Pará arrested this Friday (28) a man suspected of involvement in the murder of a trans woman in the municipality of Tracuateua , located in the northeast of the state.
> 
> The suspect, identified as Genivaldo Miranda Soares, was located and arrested in the neighboring city of Capanema , more than 160 kilometers away from where the crime occurred, two days after the homicide.
> 
> In his statement to the police, Genivaldo confessed to committing the crime. According to the suspect's version, the motive for the murder was related to an alleged debt collection of R$ 1,500.
> 
> Despite the confession, the Civil Police reported that the case continues to be investigated by the Tracuateua Police Station to confirm the true motive for the crime. Initially, the line of investigation is treating the case as a robbery followed by murder.
> 
> At the time of publication of this report, Genivaldo Miranda Soares' defense team had not been reached for comment.




> Transgender woman found dead in wooded area in Tracuateua, Pará.
> Written on August 27, 2026
> 
> Maria Alessandra Roza Silva, 46 years old, Personal archive A 46-year-old trans woman was found dead this Thursday (27) in a wooded area in the rural area of Tracuateua, in northeastern Pará.
> 
> The victim was identified as Maria Alessandra Roza Silva. According to the Military Police, Maria Alessandra was left seriously injured on a branch road near Vila de Fátima. The body was later located in the region.
> 
> Family members told the PM that Maria Alessandra had left accompanied by three other people. During the return to the locality, she was apparently violently assaulted, according to the family's account to the police. The Scientific Police were called to carry out the forensic examination and identified that Maria Alessandra was assaulted with a piece of wood in the head area.
> 
> The Tracuateua Police Station is investigating the case and seeks to identify those involved in the assault and clarify the motivation for the crime.
