The body of Ágata Mendes was found with a stab wound to the neck in undergrowth in Santo Antônio da Platina, Paraná (Brazil) on 7 Feb 2026.

She was 18.



> The Civil Police arrested this Monday (9) a 39-year-old man suspected of murdering Ágatha Mendes, an 18-year-old trans woman, in the Monte Carlo neighborhood, in Santo Antônio da Platina. The victim's body was found by her own mother in a thicket, with perforations caused by a firearm and a cut on her neck. At the crime scene, investigators seized a revolver, which may have been lost by the aggressor during a possible reaction from the young woman.
