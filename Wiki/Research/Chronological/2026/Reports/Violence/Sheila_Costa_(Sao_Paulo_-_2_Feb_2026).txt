> Transgender woman from Votorantim is shot dead by a civil police officer in São Paulo.
> Brother struggles to bring her body back for final tributes to family and friends.
> Portal Porque
> 03/02/2026 - 11:44
> 
> Sheila Costa, 35, was shot and killed by a civil police officer in the southern zone of São Paulo. Photo: Social media reproduction.
> 
> A transgender woman, resident of Jardim Serrano, in Votorantim, was shot dead in the early hours of Monday (2), in the South Zone of São Paulo. According to investigations, Sheila Costa, 35, was murdered by civil police officer Paulino Domiciano Antonio. A brother of the victim went to the capital on Tuesday morning (3) to bring Sheila's body to be viewed in Votorantim. There is still no information about the location and time of the wake.
> 
> According to G1 São Paulo , the police officer, who voluntarily presented himself at the police station after the crime, claimed he was the victim of an attempted robbery. Reports indicate he did not call the police or file a police report after the incident.
> 
> Witnesses told police they heard five gunshots around 4:55 a.m. Images from cameras near Alameda dos Guainumbis, in the Planalto Paulista neighborhood, show that she was shot in the back and appeared to have nothing in her hands.
> 
> Before the shots were fired, a witness heard someone shout: "Go, go, go, you son of a bitch." According to the witness, the perpetrator was in an old car, the model of which could not be identified.
> 
> The shooter claimed the woman had stolen his cell phone. According to the São Paulo State Public Security Secretariat (SSP), the police officer was arrested on the spot and taken to the headquarters of the Civil Police Internal Affairs Department, which will investigate the circumstances of the crime and the responsibility of the individual involved. The case will be investigated as a femicide.
> 
> Sheila was found by military police with a gunshot wound to the chest, already without vital signs. The SAMU (Emergency Medical Service) was called, but the victim's death was confirmed at the crime scene.
> 
> A bag containing belongings, but without a cell phone or documents, and some cash were found at the scene. Sheila's body was only removed from the location four hours later, around 9:30 AM.



> A civil police officer was arrested on Monday (February 2nd) on suspicion of killing a transgender woman in the southern zone of São Paulo . According to the Public Security Secretariat (SSP), the Civil Police Internal Affairs Department is monitoring the incident and will take the necessary measures.
> 
> The victim was found shot dead in the early hours of the morning on Alameda dos Guainumbis, in the Planalto Paulista region. The ambulance service (SAMU) was called and confirmed the death at the scene.
> 
> The case was registered with the Homicide and Personal Protection Department (DHPP). Authorities are working to clarify the facts.



> A trans woman, resident of Votorantim, was found dead after being shot in the early hours of Monday (2) in the south of São Paulo. The crime took place on Alameda dos Guainumbis, corner with Rua Irerê, in the Planalto Paulista neighborhood.
> 
> The victim was found lying on the street with a wound to the chest, consistent with a gunshot wound. The Mobile Emergency Care Service (SAMU) was called and confirmed the death at the scene.
> 
> According to the police report, residents of the area reported hearing gunshots around 5:00 AM, followed by screams. The perpetrator reportedly fled in an old vehicle, but it was not possible to identify the car or its license plate.
> 
> At the scene, marks were found on a wall indicating other shots. Near the body there was a bag with personal belongings, money and a container containing a white substance. The family travelled to the Capital last night (2) to identify the body and arrange for its transfer.
> 
> The perpetrator of the crime has been identified as an investigator with the São Paulo Civil Police and is currently in custody awaiting trial. The case will be investigated by the police internal affairs department.



> Transgender woman shot dead in prostitution area in southern São Paulo.
> The crime occurred in an area known as a prostitution hub around Indianópolis Avenue, in the southern part of São Paulo.
> 02/02/2026 17:48 ? 1 day ago by Folhapress
> 
> SSÃO PAULO, SP (FOLHAPRESS) - A trans woman was shot dead in the early hours of Monday (2) on Alameda dos Guainumbis, in Planalto Paulista, south of São Paulo. As of Monday afternoon, the victim had not yet been identified.
> 
> The crime occurred in an area known as a red-light district around Indianópolis Avenue.
> 
> Military police officers patrolling the area were dispatched via radio to respond to a report of gunfire. At the scene, they found a woman with a wound to her chest. The SAMU (Emergency Medical Service) team confirmed her death at the scene.
> 
> The victim was not carrying any identification. Next to the body was a bag with belongings, such as toiletries. There was also R$ 37.90 and a small plastic vial containing white powder. According to the police report, the woman is 1.85 meters tall and has no tattoos.
> 
> A resident of the area reported to the police that he heard three gunshots around 4:55 am. Before the shots, he heard someone shout: "Go, go, go, you son of a bitch." According to the witness, the perpetrator was in an old car, the model of which could not be identified.
> 
> Surveillance cameras identified in the vicinity of the crime may help the police locate the person responsible for the homicide.
> 
> The case was registered as a homicide at the 27th Police Precinct (Campo Belo) and forwarded to the DHPP (Department of Homicides and Protection of Persons). Experts and investigators from the specialized precinct were at the scene. The vehicle for removing the body was dispatched at 9:30 AM.
