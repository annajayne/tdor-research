> BOGOTÁ DC, COLOMBIA (JAN 4, 2026) – A violent crime that occurred early Thursday morning has caused outrage and dismay in the Engativá district , in northwestern Bogotá. A transgender woman was beaten to death by two men in the Florida Blanca neighborhood , a few meters from the intersection of Carrera 91 and Calle 68 .
> 
> The victim, whose identity has not yet been established by authorities, was with a friend and, according to witnesses, was attacked with a blunt object by two men who arrived on a motorcycle. The attack was direct and brutal. After beating her, the assailants fled the scene , leaving her lying on the sidewalk, where she died from the severity of her injuries .
> 
> Residents of the area reported that the crime occurred in a neighborhood that is deserted and poorly lit at night. “This neighborhood is very lonely at night; you don’t see a soul,” commented one resident.
> 
> According to preliminary reports, the trans woman had been at a nearby bar hours earlier , and minutes before the attack she was talking with her companion on a platform when the attackers arrived without warning.
> 
> Testimonies gathered by local media indicate that the victim was approximately 20 years old , short in stature, and was wearing a red top . It was also mentioned that she worked in the area and was known to some residents, although the reasons for the attack are still under investigation .
> 
> The Technical Investigation Corps (CTI) of the Prosecutor's Office inspected the scene and carried out urgent procedures to collect evidence that will allow them to identify and capture those responsible . Authorities are not ruling out any hypothesis and are calling on the public to provide any information that may help solve this crime.


> Early yesterday morning, a transgender woman was murdered in a violent incident in the Engativá neighborhood, in northwestern Bogotá. The victim was with a friend when she was attacked by two men who apparently took advantage of her vulnerability. Her identity has not yet been established.
> 
> According to witnesses, the attackers used a blunt object to strike her, killing her at the scene. The crime occurred a few meters from the corner of Carrera 91 and Calle 68, in the Florida Blanca neighborhood, an area that, according to residents, remains deserted at night.
> 
> “This neighborhood is very lonely at night, you don’t see a soul and it’s heavy; that’s why nobody realized what had happened,” said a resident of the area.
