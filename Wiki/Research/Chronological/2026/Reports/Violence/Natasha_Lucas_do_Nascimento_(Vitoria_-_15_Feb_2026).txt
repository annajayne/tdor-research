====== Natasha Lucas do Nascimento (Vitoria - 15 Feb 2026) ======

> A 27-year-old trans woman, identified as Natasha Lucas do Nascimento, died after being shot by a Municipal Guard officer on Sunday night (15), in Praça Costa Pereira, in the center of Vitória.
> 
> The incident occurred around 10 PM, shortly after the Carnival parades that had passed through the area had ended. The square was crowded at the time of the event.
> 
> According to initial reports, Natasha was involved in an altercation after encountering someone with whom she had a dispute. Reports indicate she was carrying a machete and attempted to attack another transgender woman. The victim reported being injured in the shoulder during the altercation.
> 
> In an interview with reporters, the woman who was shot said she tried to take cover behind a Municipal Guard officer who was at the scene. The officer reportedly ordered Natasha several times to stop the aggression and warned that if she didn't back down, he might shoot. According to the victim, the suspect continued advancing, trying to hit her and the officer as well.
> 
> Faced with the situation, the guard fired shots. Natasha did not survive her injuries and died.
> 
> In an interview with the TV SIM team, the Municipal Guard said that officers were called and tried in various ways to stop the aggression, but were unsuccessful.
> 
> “One of the aggressors inflicted two knife wounds on the victim, intending to kill her, even with the presence of two teams from the Municipal Guard, who were trying to resolve the situation. The aggressor continued to attack the victim, potentially even hitting the Municipal Guard officer, and at that moment it was necessary to fire a shot to stop the unjust aggression, in legitimate self-defense of the victim who had already been hit twice,” highlighted Hélvio Souza Alves Junior, Undersecretary of Urban Security.
> 
> According to the Civil Police, the incident is still under investigation.

> Natasha Lucas do Nascimento stabbed another woman in front of guards, in Costa Pereira Square
> A 27-year-old trans woman, identified as Natasha Lucas do Nascimento, was killed by the Municipal Guard after stabbing another woman in Costa Pereira Square, in downtown Vitória, late Sunday night (15). The place was crowded, due to Carnival (see image above), and the scene frightened many people.
> 
> According to TV Gazeta's investigation, it all started during a fight between Natasha, who was carrying a knife, and another woman. The two reportedly had a long-standing disagreement.
> 
> "She (Natasha) hit my friend. I ran to a patrol car and called the guards. She wanted to stab me. I started hiding behind the guard. She came at me and stabbed me next to the guard. He told her to stop, otherwise he would have to shoot, and even then she didn't stop," said the woman stabbed by Natasha, in an interview with TV Gazeta.
> 
> It was at that moment, after the stabbing, that the guard shot. Natasha was taken to the State Hospital of Urgency and Emergency (Heue), but did not survive her injuries. Two women who were with her were taken to the police station to give statements.
> 
> When questioned about the case, the Municipal Guard of Vitória stated that a team on duty in Praça Costa Pereira was called by two women who reported being threatened.
> 
> "During the response to the incident and the journey to the indicated location, the officers encountered individuals armed with knives. Even with the presence of the Municipal Guard, the aggressor advanced towards one of the victims. Verbal orders were given for the aggression to cease, but they were ignored. Subsequently, there was another attempted attack, during which one of the victims was wounded in the arm. Given the current and imminent aggression, it was necessary for the Municipal Guard to use force to stop the action and save the victim's life. During the intervention, the aggressor was injured," the statement reads.
> 
> The Municipal Guard also states that the action was carried out in accordance with current legislation and operational protocols, "respecting the principles of legality, necessity, and proportionality, prioritizing the preservation of life and the safety of the population."
> 
> In a statement, the Civil Police informed that "the incident is currently being handled by the on-duty shift at the Regional Police Station of Vitória. Only after the conclusion of the hearings regarding the incident will we have information about the procedure that will be adopted by the delegate of the Central Tele-flagrante with the two people taken into custody. The body was sent to the Institute of Forensic Medicine (IML) of the Scientific Police to be autopsied and, subsequently, released to the family."

*With information from reporter Caíque Verli, from TV Gazeta
