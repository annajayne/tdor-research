> Trans woman known as 'The girl with the blue hair' found dead in Mexicaltzingo
> Fanny was known for her distinctive blue hair in videos she uploaded to the TikTok platform.
> Mexicaltzingo /07.05.2026 19:16:00
> 
> A transgender woman identified as Fanny 'N', known on social media as 'The girl with the bluuu hair',  was found dead inside  her home located in the municipality of Mexicaltzingo , State of Mexico, on Thursday afternoon.
> 
> According to initial reports, the 27-year-old victim was found in a building located on Josefa Ortiz de Domínguez Street. The body showed signs of violence, apparently caused by a bladed weapon.
> 
> Paramedics arrived at the scene to provide first aid; however,  they confirmed that the young woman no longer had vital signs.
> 
> The property was secured by members of the Mexicaltzingo Municipal Police so that experts from the Attorney General's Office of the State of Mexico (FGJEM) could carry out the corresponding procedures and the removal of the body.
> 
> So far no arrests have been reported and authorities have already begun investigations to clarify the motive for the crime, which could be investigated under the transfeminicide protocol.
> 
> 'The girl with the blue hair'  was known for her characteristic blue hair, as she presented herself in videos she uploaded to the TikTok platform, where she can be seen as a dancer at events and sound system dances.
> 
> She also gave dance classes and choreographed dances for various social events in the municipality of Mexicaltzingo.
> 
> Sound system operators mourned his passing 
> Following the confirmation of his death, several sonidero dance groups mourned his passing and expressed their condolences to his family.
> 
> “Sound Dancers at Heart, we join in mourning the passing of a great dancer, who today joins the Creator on another dance floor. Our prayers and affection are with her family and with all of us who remember her with love and admiration,” they posted.
> They mentioned that Fanny will be remembered for her great charisma and skill. “Her passing leaves a great void among family, friends, and the entire community that knew her and shared moments with her. She will always be remembered for her charisma, joy, and presence within the sound system scene.”
