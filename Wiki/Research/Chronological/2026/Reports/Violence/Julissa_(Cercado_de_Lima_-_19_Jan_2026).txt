> Cercado de Lima: Man murders his trans partner and sleeps next to the corpse before being captured
> The detainee, identified as Yilmer Medina Rafael, has a record for robbery and sexual offenses. 
> The crime occurred in a house on Peñaloza Street. Photo: Press Release.
> 1/19/2026 - 12:38
> 
> A horrific crime has shocked the residents of Cercado de Lima. A 31-year-old trans woman, known to her loved ones as Julissa, was found dead inside a room after being attacked with a knife 
> 
> The main suspect is her partner, Yilmer Alexander Medina Rafael, who was arrested by the National Police as he tried to leave the scene.
> 
> The bloody episode came to light when the victim's relatives, worried about his absence, went to the property located on Jirón Peñaloz.
> 
> Fiorella Velarde, the deceased's sister, was the one who found Medina Rafael with traces of blood on his face.
> 
> According to initial investigations, the suspect allegedly stabbed Julissa up to seven times with a table knife. 
> 
> What is most disturbing for the authorities is that, after the attack, the assailant allegedly remained in the room and slept next to the body for several hours before being discovered.
> 
> When cornered by family members and the police, the suspect confessed to committing the act, although he claimed it was "self-defense" after an alleged mutual fight, showing cuts on his own body to support his version.
> 
> However, the detainee's criminal record complicates his legal situation. PNP Colonel Marcial Flores revealed that Medina Rafael is not unknown to the justice system, as he has a criminal record for aggravated robbery and crimes against sexual freedom.





> A 31-year-old transgender woman, known as Julissa, was stabbed to death inside a room in downtown Lima . Police arrested Yilmer Alexander Medina Rafael , the victim's partner, who is considered the alleged perpetrator of the crime, which occurred in a house on Jirón Peñaloza.
> 
> According to police reports, the suspect allegedly attacked the victim with a table knife, stabbing her seven times. After the attack, the man reportedly remained in the room, asleep next to the body for several hours until he was discovered by the victim's family.
> 
> How was Yilmer Alexander Medina Rafael discovered?
> Fiorella Velarde, the victim's sister, recounted that she went to look for her relative and found the man with bloodstains on his face. According to the witness, when he was discovered, the detainee confessed that they had had a fight and that he had stabbed her after an alleged prior attack.
> 
> Police apprehended Yilmer Alexander Medina Rafael as he was allegedly preparing to leave the property in the Cercado de Lima district . During his arrest, the man claimed to authorities that he acted in self-defense, as he also had cuts on his body resulting from the altercation.
> 
> According to statements from family and friends, the motive for the murder was the perpetrator's excessive jealousy. The victim's sister indicated that the couple argued frequently due to the controlling behavior the suspect repeatedly displayed.
> 
> What is the alleged murderer's background?
> Colonel Marcial Flores of the Peruvian National Police reported that the detainee has a prior criminal record. According to the police chief, Yilmer Alexander Medina Rafael has a record for aggravated robbery and sexual offenses, specifically indecent touching.
> 
> The suspect remains in police custody while authorities conduct their investigation at the crime scene. According to police, the suspect will face charges for alleged homicide with a bladed weapon based on evidence found in the room.
> 
> The Peruvian National Police's Public Order and Security Division continues its investigation to clarify the details of the attack that occurred on Peñaloza Street. The victim's body was taken to the central morgue after forensic experts arrived in downtown Lima.
