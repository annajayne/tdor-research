> Young trans woman shot as she left her home in Bello: authorities investigate what happened
> The young woman was taken to a hospital, but died before receiving medical attention.
> 
> A gunshot left Mariana Ballesteros Restrepo, a 19-year-old trans woman, seriously injured as she had just left a house where a meeting between friends and family had been taking place since the previous night.
> 
> The attack occurred around 3:00 am this Thursday, in the vicinity of Carrera 66 with Calle 79, an area close to the road that connects the urban area of Bello with the township of San Félix, in Antioquia.
> 
> According to authorities, two men traveling on a motorcycle approached the young woman and one of them fired a gun before fleeing the scene.
> 
> Witnesses reported that Mariana fell injured in the middle of the street while bystanders tried to help her and desperately called for assistance. Officers from the Valadares Police Station arrived minutes later after receiving an alert from the community.
> 
> When the police arrived at the scene, the young woman still had vital signs, so she was rushed to Marco Fidel Suárez Hospital. However, the severity of her injuries prevented her from receiving specialized medical attention.
> 
> Sources close to the case indicated that Mariana died during the journey to the medical center due to the large amount of blood she lost after the gunshot wound.
> 
> As the initial investigations progress, authorities are reviewing security camera footage and gathering testimonies to establish who was responsible and what the motives for the crime might have been.
> 
> “The case is being handled by the National Police, who are currently conducting the corresponding investigations to clarify the circumstances and motives of the event and to bring justice ,” said Wber Zapata, Secretary of Security and Coexistence of Bello.
> 
> The official added that the Mayor's Office will accompany the judicial process and support the actions of the authorities in charge of the case.
> 
> Mariana's death sparked outrage among human rights organizations and LGBTIQ+ groups, who called for swift investigations and for the case to be addressed with a gender perspective.
> 
> “ We demand a swift, serious, and gender-sensitive investigation from the competent authorities, and that this crime does not go unpunished, ” the Caribe Afirmativo corporation stated in a press release.
> 
> The also noted that, with this latest homicide, the number of LGBTIQ+ organization people murdered in Colombia this year has reached 27, a figure that continues to raise alarms about the violence against this community in the country.
