> Who was Flávia Cunha, the transgender woman shot dead in Divinópolis?
> Updated on: March 2, 2026 16:38
> 
> The transgender woman shot dead on Monday morning, February 2nd, in Divinópolis, is Flavia Cunha, 42 years old. According to the portal Nosso Jornal.TV, Flavia is originally from Abaeté, lived in Brasília, and had been in Divinópolis for just over a month to help care for her 81-year-old grandmother.
> 
> According to the Civil Police, the crime occurred around 7:50 am on Paraná Avenue, in Sidil. Flávia was hit by several shots in the kitchen of the property. Forensics found 9mm caliber casings at the scene.
> 
> The main suspect is a 34-year-old cousin of the victim, who fled on foot after the crime. He has already been identified and is being sought. According to the police, the man had a criminal record and had been released from prison in January of this year, moving into the same building. The motive is believed to be a dispute over family matters.
> 
> The time of the burial has not yet been announced.





> The femicide in the Sidil neighborhood, in Divinópolis, was committed by her own cousin on Monday morning (2). The crime took place in her grandmother's apartment, on Avenida Paraná.
> 
> Initial information from the Military Police indicates that there was a family argument between him and the victim, a 42-year-old transgender woman. She is originally from the Federal District and had been living in the city for about a month to take care of her grandmother, who was in another room of the house and did not witness the crime, which occurred in the kitchen.
> 
> The ambulance service (SAMU) was called around 7:59 am and confirmed the victim's death at the scene. The suspect, who also lived at the location, fled on foot and is still being sought. According to the police, he has a criminal record and was recently in prison.
> 
> Forensic experts were called in and found several bullet holes from a 9mm pistol. In a statement, the Civil Police claim they have begun investigations to determine the circumstances.
> 
> Regarding the femicide that occurred today (March 2nd) at approximately 7:50 AM on Rua Paraná, in the Centro neighborhood of Divinópolis, the Civil Police of Minas Gerais (PCMG) reports that, upon being notified, they promptly responded to the scene . The team, composed of a police delegate, investigators, and a forensic expert, secured the area, conducted a forensic investigation, collected evidence, captured images, and conducted preliminary interviews with family members and witnesses .
