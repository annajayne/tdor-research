> Murder at a San Antonio nightclub
> Monday, August 24, 2026
> The incident caused panic among those who were socializing at the nightclub.
> 
> A Venezuelan trans woman was murdered early Sunday morning, August 23, in a nightclub in San Antonio del Táchira, in the border municipality of Bolívar.
> 
> Although he had initially been identified as Mackley Maldonado, the authorities' report confirmed his last name: Mackley Anaya Cáceres, 22 years old, a border resident.
> 
> It was reported that the young transgender man was enjoying himself at the nightclub, located just meters from the Sucre Bridge, when an armed man shot him, killing him instantly. The perpetrator reportedly fled immediately.
> 
> The incident caused panic among those enjoying themselves at the establishment. "Let's get out of here!" could be heard in a video circulating among groups, while others approached to see the lifeless body. It was around 4:00 a.m.
> 
> Officials from the Scientific, Criminal and Forensic Investigations Corps (CICPC) arrived at the scene to remove the body and begin investigations to find the person responsible.
> 
> "La Mackley," as she called herself, lived in the upper part of the Simón Bolívar neighborhood with her mother.




> A 23-year-old trans woman was shot dead in a nightclub in Táchira.
> 23/08/2026
> #Trans person murdered in Táchira , #Táchira news
> 
> An act of violence shocked residents of the border region after the shooting death of Mackley Maldonado, a 23-year-old trans woman , which occurred during the early hours of Sunday, August 23, in the state of Táchira.
> 
> According to information reported by Última Página Táchira , the crime occurred around 4:00 am , at the facilities of a nightclub located a few meters from the Sucre bridge.
> 
> According to testimonies gathered at the scene, an unidentified man entered the establishment carrying a firearm and went directly towards Maldonado, against whom he fired multiple shots before leaving the site.
> 
> The victim, affectionately known to her relatives as "La Mackley" , lived with her mother in the upper part of the Simón Bolívar sector.
> 
> The Cicpc is investigating the murder of "La Mackley".
> Commissions from the Scientific, Criminal and Forensic Investigation Corps (Cicpc) began investigations to clarify the circumstances of the homicide, identify the perpetrator and determine the motive for the attack.
> 
> Civil organizations and human rights groups asked the authorities to investigate the case with a gender perspective and to consider, among the hypotheses, the possibility that it was a transfemicide or a hate crime .
> 
> So far, authorities have not reported on the identity or capture of the alleged perpetrator.
