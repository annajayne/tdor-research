> MEXICO CITY - The lifeless body of a trans woman who had been reported missing since February 26 was found in a park in the Venustiano Carranza borough , with signs of violence and sexual abuse.
> 
> It was early yesterday morning when relatives of the young Nayrobi Flores Arcia , 27 years old, a member of the LGBTTTI community, found her body in the Ave Fénix Linear Park, located at Gran Canal del Desagüe Avenue 390B, in the Ampliación Venustiano Carranza neighborhood ; the body was hidden inside an improvised tent.
> 
> It should be remembered that Nayrobi had been missing since February 26, when she left a friend's house in the Morelos neighborhood and nothing more was heard from her.
> 
> After eight days of intense searching, the young trans woman was located by one of her sisters, who said that Nayrobi was beaten, had stab wounds in her back and signs of having been raped, so she demanded that the authorities of the capital's Prosecutor's Office capture the person responsible.
> 
> The victim's family provided the capital's authorities with the name of a man they suspect may be behind the horrific murder.
> 
> formally requested the authorities to initiate an investigation focused on a particular individual, whom they suspect may have direct responsibility for the death of the 27-year-old woman.





> Missing trans woman found dead in a tent in Mexico City park
> 06/03/2026 - 1 day ago
> 
> The Attorney General's Office of Mexico City confirmed the discovery of the body of Nayrobi Flores , a 27-year-old trans woman who had been reported missing since February 26, 2026. Her body was found in the early morning of March 6 in a park in the Venustiano Carranza borough, inside a makeshift tent .
> 
> Nayrobi was last seen leaving a friend's house in the Morelos neighborhood of the Cuauhtémoc borough . After losing contact with her, family and friends began a search and shared her missing person report on social media and local news outlets in the hope of finding her alive.
> 
> After several days without information about her whereabouts, the discovery of her body confirmed the tragic outcome of her disappearance, which provoked outrage and demands for justice from her family.
> 
> Body found in a park
> According to the Mexico City Attorney General's Office, the body was found inside a makeshift tent in a park in the Venustiano Carranza borough. Authorities reported that the body showed signs of violence , and an investigation has been launched to determine the cause of death and whether any third parties were involved.
> 
> The institution also stated that it is maintaining communication with the victim's family to provide support during the investigation. According to Alicia Flores, Nayrobi's sister, a man told them that the young woman might be in the area of ??the Linear Park on Avenida.
> 
> Upon arriving at the location, a person in a vulnerable situation pointed out a tent made of tarpaulin and plastic bags, from which —according to him— a strong smell was coming.
> 
> Family demands justice
> It was at that location that family members confirmed the discovery of Nayrobi's body. Alicia Flores indicated that the victim had burns and multiple stab wounds on her back , in addition to other signs of violence.
> 
> For his part, Alberto Flores , the young woman's father, stated that the family has evidence that could point to a possible suspect and asked the authorities to act quickly. "The authorities already know who it is. I just ask that they bring the perpetrator to justice as quickly as possible ," he declared.
> 
> The father also explained that he initially recognized his daughter by the clothes she was wearing. The case is now under investigation by Mexico City authorities, while family members and advocacy groups have demanded that the crime not go unpunished.
