> TRANSFEMICIDE: TRANS WOMAN MURDERED WITH A SHOT TO THE HEAD IN POSORJA – GUAYAS – ECUADOR
> 
> The Runa Sipiy Observatory, the only national community monitoring system for violence against LGBT+ people in Ecuador, active since 2013, records the murder of a trans woman in the province of Guayas.
> 
> GUAYAQUIL, ECUADOR.  
> 
> a Asociación Silueta X has registered, via Observatorio Runa Sipiy, the murder of a trans woman in Posorja, Guayas province. The case was received through the national community alert network maintained by Silueta X in conjunction with the National Trans Revolution Platform – CoaliTrans (22 collectives) and the Ecuadorian Federation of LGBT+ Organizations (73 collectives).
> 
> The victim has been identified as Sherazade, a transgender woman from Ecuador. She was murdered in a high-crime area known locally as "La Poza," within Posorja. Local activist groups, who preferred to remain anonymous, stated that she was shot in the head. There is no information indicating that the Attorney General's Office has opened an investigation or that any suspects have been arrested.
> 
> STRUCTURAL ECONOMIC VULNERABILITY AS A RISK FACTOR
> 
> Sherazade's case reveals a pattern of multiple vulnerability that the Runa Sipiy Observatory has systematically identified in its historical data series: the intersection between trans gender identity, economic precarity, and exposure to high-risk areas.
> 
> This fact —common in the transfemicides recorded by Runa Sipiy— shows that violence against trans women does not end with the crime: it continues in the indignity of post-mortem institutional abandonment.
> 
> CRIMINOLOGICAL ANALYSIS AND TERRITORIAL ALERT
> 
> According to the Runa Sipiy Observatory's analysis, the Posorja/"La Poza" sector in the Playas canton represents a new area of risk, adding to the critical zones already identified on the national map of violence against transgender people. The lack of active judicial investigation, its location in an area difficult for institutional control, and the limited media coverage of the incident constitute what the Observatory calls a new area of ??risk.
> 
> "zone of silent impunity" : crimes that occur, but that the system does not see.
> 
> The Silueta X Association, in compliance with its mandate as administrator of the Runa Sipiy Observatory, demands that the Attorney General's Office immediately open a preliminary investigation, correctly classify the act according to the standards of the Inter-American Court of Human Rights, and recognize Sherazade's gender identity in all official documents of the process.
>
