> URUAPAN, Mich., August 23, 2026 - A transgender woman was shot to death early Sunday morning in the El Vergel neighborhood of Zamora.
> 
> Around 1 a.m., motorists reported to authorities that, on the road that connects to the El Vergel neighborhood, next to the train tracks, there was a body, apparently lifeless.
> 
> Moments later, members of the National Guard and the Mexican Army were mobilized to the site, who confirmed the report upon locating the body.
> 
> The area was cordoned off and secured by officers. Minutes later, personnel from the Zamora Regional Prosecutor's Office arrived to carry out the corresponding procedures.
> 
> During the inspection of the body, experts confirmed that it was a transgender woman, who had several bullet wounds.
> 
> Finally, the body was transferred to the Forensic Medical Service, where it will remain until it is identified and claimed by family members.
