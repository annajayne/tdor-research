> What did they find on the cell phone of the trans woman who was burned alive in El Cimarrón?
> The case has taken a new turn after investigators confirmed that the contents of the trans woman's cell phone would be key to solving the triple murder.
> Updated: May 18, 2026 at 10:49
> 
> The Public Ministry's investigations continue to reveal details about the deaths of three people found charred in the village of El Cimarrón, including Axel Obed Cerrato Pineda, a trans woman known as Tatiana.
> 
> According to initial investigations, one of the key elements in the case was the review of Cerrato's cell phone, a situation that allegedly triggered a series of violent events that ended in a triple homicide.




> Honduran authorities captured a suspected member of the 18th Street gang accused of participating in the brutal murder of three people found burned to death on El Cimarrón hill, located on the northwestern outskirts of the Honduran capital.
> 
> The arrest was carried out by agents of the Police Directorate of Investigations (DPI), who indicated that the arrest is the result of a complex investigation based on technical analysis, evidence collection and intelligence work developed since the day the bodies were found.
> 
> The detainee was identified as Víctor Javier Castro Rivas, 37 years old, originally from Residencial Plaza, in the Central District, Francisco Morazán department.
> 
> The important operational action was carried out through a self-motivated preventive detention by specialists from the Division of Crimes Against Life (DDCV), who located the suspect inside a house located in the Monterrey neighborhood of Comayagüela, between the Los Llanos and Lomas del Naboo neighborhoods.
> 
> According to preliminary investigations, the victims were allegedly kidnapped, tortured, and then murdered before the criminals attempted to destroy all evidence by burning the bodies in a mountainous area of ??the village of El Cimarrón.
> 
> Authorities have preliminarily identified the victims as Alejandro José Marcilla Reyes, Bowey Burke Midence, and Axel Obed Cerrato Pineda. (Photo: Confidencial Hn)
> Authorities have preliminarily identified the victims as Alejandro José Marcilla Reyes, Bowey Burke Midence, and Axel Obed Cerrato Pineda. (Photo: Confidencial Hn)
> Authorities presume the victims are Alejandro José Marcilla Reyes, Bowey Burke Midence, and Axel Obed Cerrato Pineda, who had been reported missing since Monday, May 11.
> 
> According to the DPI, the investigations began immediately after the discovery of the human remains and since then allowed the collection of a series of evidence that led to the first arrest related to the case.
> 
> The discovery occurred on Tuesday, May 12, when members of the Fire Department received an alert through the 911 National Emergency System about a forest fire in a mountainous area of ??the village of El Cimarrón.
> 
> Upon arriving at the scene to control the flames, rescuers discovered a shocking sight: inside an old freezer were two completely charred bodies . A third, equally burned, body was found a few meters away.
> 
> Following the discovery, forensic investigators carried out the removal of the body and began the investigative procedures to clarify the triple murder that has caused consternation among the inhabitants of the capital.
> 
> Authorities indicated that inside the house where Castro Rivas was captured, they found several pieces of evidence that allegedly link him, along with other individuals, to the kidnapping, torture, and murder of the victims.
> 
> The suspect will face charges for the crime of murder and has already been referred to the competent authorities to continue with the judicial process in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Code.
> 
> The DPI indicated that the investigations are ongoing and they do not rule out further arrests in the next 48 hours , as an intense search continues to identify and locate other possible individuals involved in the crime.
> 
> In addition, police officers reported that they are continuing to process evidence collected both at the crime scene and at the raided home, where they found relevant clues to strengthen the investigative file.
> 
> The extreme violence with which the bodies were found considerably complicated the work of identifying the victims and made the case one of the most complex recorded in recent months in the Honduran capital.
> 
> Forensic medicine specialists explained that the level of destruction caused by the fire virtually eliminated any possibility of using traditional identification methods.
> 
> Authorities confirmed that the human remains were subjected to extreme burning , a situation that prevented obtaining fingerprints or performing dental analysis.
> 
> The spokesperson for Forensic Medicine, Issa Alvarado , explained that the conditions in which the bodies were found make it impossible to use conventional identification procedures.




> More people are being investigated in connection with the triple murder in El Cimarrón.
> The DPI reported that new arrest warrants could be issued for the triple murder in El Cimarrón, where two men and a trans woman were found burned to death in a freezer.
> 
> Tegucigalpa, Honduras
> Investigations into the triple murder in the village of El Cimarrón continue to progress, and new arrest warrants could be issued this week against other alleged perpetrators of the massacre, reported Edgardo Barahona, spokesperson for the Police Directorate of Investigations (DPI).
> 
> The official indicated that the Public Prosecutor's Office is already identifying more suspects and that, at the latest by Wednesday, there could be new developments in the case.
> 
> Barahona explained that they will not provide further details to avoid hindering the investigations or alerting those involved.
> 
> The statements come after the capture of Víctor Javier Rivas Castro (37 years old), an alleged member of Pandilla 18, accused of the murder of three people found burned in the village of El Cimarrón, in the Central District.
> 
> The victims were identified as Alejandro José Marcia Reyes, Bowey Burke Midence, and Axel Obed Cerrato Pineda. Their bodies were found inside and near a freezer in a mountainous area of the region.
> 
> According to the investigation, the victims had been reported missing on Monday, May 11.
> 
> The following day, members of the Fire Department responded to an alert about a forest fire in a mountainous area of the village of El Cimarrón.
> 
> Upon arriving at the scene, rescuers found two completely charred bodies inside a freezer, while a third body was located a few meters from the scene.
> 
> Authorities are continuing their investigation to determine the motive for the crime and the possible involvement of other people.
