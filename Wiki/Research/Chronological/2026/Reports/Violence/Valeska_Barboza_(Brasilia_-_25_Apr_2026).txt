> Family believes death of transgender woman in Brasília was "premeditated"
> Valeska Barboza, 36 years old, was found dead inside her own apartment. To date, no suspects have been arrested for the crime.
> 27/04/2026 20:41updated27/04/2026 22:37
> 
> Relatives of Valeska Barboza ( pictured ), 36, are trying to understand what happened to the elderly caregiver who was brutally murdered inside her own apartment in Santa Maria (DF). The transgender woman's body was found on Saturday night (April 25) with signs of violent death.
> 
> The crime is being investigated by the 33rd Police Precinct . As of the last update to this article, no suspects had been arrested for Valeska's murder.
> 
> The victim had been missing since last Wednesday (April 22). The military was called after a friend reported her disappearance and noticed a strong odor coming from the property where she lived, which was locked.
> 
> The room where the victim was found had a large amount of blood splattered on it. Valeska was struck with a sharp object.
> 
> In an emotional account, the victim's sister, Sandra Amâncio, described the last few days before the crime and raised suspicions about the possible motive. However, the authority has not been confirmed by the Civil Police of the Federal District.
> 
> According to her, the victim had taken in a man from São Paulo, whom she was trying to help start a new life. "She brought him into her home, helped him send out resumes, tried to find him a job. But, towards the end, he no longer wanted help," she said.
> 
> Faced with the situation, Valeska reportedly asked the man to leave the property, which, according to the family, may have motivated the crime.
> 
> The sister claims that a neighbor witnessed an argument between the two on Tuesday (April 21), the last day the victim was seen.
> 
> "A neighbor heard them arguing, my sister asking him to leave. For us, it was something premeditated," she said. The family also reports that, after the disappearance, the suspect continued to respond to messages on the victim's cell phone until Friday (April 24).
> 
> Valeska's body was found after friends and relatives became concerned about her lack of contact. "Everyone started to find it strange because she never disappeared. A friend started looking for her, but nobody knew where she was. That's when the police entered the house," he reported.
> 
> According to Sandra, personal belongings such as Valeska's laptop, documents, cell phone, and television were taken from the residence.
> 
> The victim, described by her family as a "strong-willed, cheerful, and very communicative" person, worked as an elderly caregiver and was studying nursing. "She was very hardworking and was close to being able to buy her own house," her sister stated.
> 
> The caregiver's life story was also marked by overcoming challenges. According to her sister, the victim was rescued and adopted by her family when she was still a newborn. "She was always a strong person who fought hard to get where she was," she said.
> 
> Valeska's burial will take place this Tuesday (April 28th) at the Parque Memorial Novo Gama Cemetery, in the vicinity of the Federal District, at 3 pm.
> 
> "Many people liked her. It's a very difficult loss, because of the person she was. What we want most is justice," Sandra lamented.
> A free bus, scheduled to depart at 2 PM from the sports court at Blocks 208/308 in Santa Maria, was also made available for family and friends who wish to say goodbye to the victim.




> The life story of Valeska Barbosa , 36, a transgender woman found dead inside her home in the Porto Rico Condominium , in Santa Maria, last Saturday (April 25), is marked by survival since birth and a history of affection with the family that took her in. Speaking to Correio , the victim's sister, Sandra Amâncio, 53, recalled the past and the last days before the crime, and demanded justice.
> 
> According to Sandra, Valeska was adopted as a baby after a traumatic event. “She was born in my arms, at home. The daughter of a friend. She was very small, but perfect. Then, they tried to bury her alive, and I was desperate,” she recounted. The sister reports that she mobilized her own family to rescue the baby. “We went back to the place, searched until we heard her crying. That's how we managed to save her. My mother decided to adopt her and raised her as her daughter, with a lot of love,” she stated.
> 
> Raised alongside her six siblings, Valeska always knew her history and, according to her family, transformed her pain into strength. “She was an incredible human being, with a huge heart, very cheerful. She liked to help everyone, she didn't discriminate against anyone,” said Sandra. “She left a very beautiful mark on the lives of those who knew her,” she affirmed. 
> 
> Shaken, Sandra describes the family's pain at the loss. “We can't believe it. It's a very great pain. She was a happy person, who loved life and made everyone smile,” she stated. The family is now seeking answers and accountability for the crime. “We just want justice,” she said.





> Transgender woman found dead in condominium in Brasília; body showed signs of violence.
> Civil Police are investigating the cause of death; Military Police were called by a friend of Valeska Barboza, who noticed her disappearance and a 'strong odor' coming from the property. No suspects have been identified.
> By g1 DF
> 26/04/2026 14h48
> 
> The Civil Police of the Federal District is investigating the circumstances of the death of a trans woman found, already lifeless and in a state of decomposition, in an apartment in the Porto Rico Condominium, in Santa Maria, on Saturday night (25).
> 
> The body was found by the Military Police of the Federal District after a friend reported the victim's disappearance and said he had identified a "strong odor" coming from the locked property.
> 
> The victim was identified as Valeska Barboza.
> 
> According to the police, the body showed signs of violent death, "including puncture wounds and bloodstains at the scene."
> 
> The area was cordoned off for forensic examination, and the incident was registered at the 27th Police Station (Recanto das Emas). The investigation will be handled by the 33rd Police Station (Santa Maria).
> 
> As of early afternoon on Sunday (26), no suspect had been identified or arrested. On social media, family and friends held a fundraiser to cover the funeral costs.
