> The LGBTQ+ community in Paarl has been left shattered after 17-year-old Justine Davids was brutally killed in an attack in Paarl East over the langnaweek.
> 
> Justine, a learner at L.K. Zeeman Academy, was a proud member of the LGBTQ+ community and known as Justine Jameelia Evangelista.
> 
> Three suspects, aged 17, 18 and 20, have been arrested in connection with Davdis's murder. They appeared in the Paarl Magistrates’ Court on Tuesday, 11 August 2026.
> 
> According to police, Davids and their friends were walking through an open field in Paulus Street, Lantana, Paarl East, at about 1am on Sunday when they were allegedly attacked by three suspects. Davids was the only person who suffered fatal injuries.
> 
> Western Cape police spokesperson Sergeant Wesley Twigg confirmed the murder investigation.
> 
> Twigg reported: “Paarl East police registered a murder case for investigation following a stabbing incident on an open field in Paulus Street, Lantana, Paarl, on Sunday morning, 9 August 2026, at about 1am, which claimed the life of a 17-year-old boy.”
> 
> Twigg said the circumstances surrounding the attack were still being investigated.
> 
> The brutal has sparked an outpouring of grief and anger, with members of the community calling for justice and demanding that the accused be denied bail.
> 
> A petition has been launched calling on the court to consider the seriousness of the charges, community safety and the safety of LGBTQ+ and queer people in Paarl and surrounding areas.
> 
> Justine’s school also paid tribute to the teenager.
> 
> The school wrote on its Facebook page: “With heavy hearts, the L.K. Zeeman Academy family shares the deeply sad news of the passing of our beloved learner, Justine Davids.”
> 
> The school described Justin as someone whose “warm presence, bright spirit, and the quiet strength he carried” would be deeply missed.
> 
> Popular content creator and LGBTQ+ community member Lito Darling also spoke out, asking how many more people must be hurt or killed before LGBTQ+ people are treated with dignity.

> Paarl Rocked by Murder of 17-Year-Old LGBTQ+ Learner Justine Davids
> 
> The LGBTQ+ community in the Western Cape mourned the murder of 17-year-old transgender learner Justine Davids, also known as Justine Jameelia Evangelista. 
> 
> She was stabbed to death in an open field in Lantana, Paarl, in the early hours of 9 August. Justine had reportedly been walking with a friend, who escaped the attack. Police arrested three suspects, aged 17, 18 and 20, who appeared in the Paarl Magistrates’ Court on 11 August, as community members gathered outside to demand justice and oppose bail; an online petition similarly called for the accused to remain in custody. Justine’s murder prompted widespread concern that the attack may have been motivated by queerphobia, although this had not been confirmed by police.
