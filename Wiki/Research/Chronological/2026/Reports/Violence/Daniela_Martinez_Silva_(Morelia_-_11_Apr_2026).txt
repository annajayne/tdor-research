> LGBTIQ+ groups in Michoacán demanded justice for the alleged hate crime committed against Daniela, a trans woman whose body was found this Saturday in the Río Grande, northwest of the city of Morelia.
> 
> Through its Facebook account, the civil association Pride Michoacán asked that the crime not go unpunished and that those responsible be found, as well as that the State Attorney General's Office ( FGE ) investigate the case with a gender and diversity perspective.
> 
> Hate crimes must stop! Attorney General's Office, we demand an investigation with a gender and diversity perspective. Her death must not go unpunished, and those responsible must be brought to justice. Rest in peace, Daniela, cross the rainbow bridge.
> 
> PRIDE Michoacán
> Similarly, the Michoacán es Diversidad collective joined the call for justice and also requested that the FGE carry out the corresponding investigations under a line focused on the rights of the sex-diverse community , which continues to be a victim of crimes and hate speech.
> 
> For her part, activist and Miss Teen Latina Mexico 2020, Nicole Navarrete Contreras , denounced the attack against Daniela and the lack of attention from the authorities to address cases of violence against people in the LGBTIQ+ community, mainly directed towards trans women and men.
> 
> Today Michoacán mourns the loss caused by the hatred of a heartless animal, by cowardice, by the hatred felt at seeing a person happy. Today the trans community of Morelia, Michoacán, Mexico mourns because she was taken from us in the most vile and cowardly way.
> Nicole Navarrete Contreras, LGBTIQ+ activist
> According to reports, this morning citizens traveling along the Paseo de la República ring road, near El Realito , notified authorities about the presence of a deceased person, prompting an operation to cordon off the area.
> 
> Later, forensic experts from the Attorney General's Office arrived and carried out the procedures established by law, removing the body to the Forensic Medical Service ( SEMEFO ) to perform the corresponding autopsy and clarify the motives for the crime.



> Morelia, Michoacán (MiMorelia.com) - The discovery of the lifeless body of a trans woman on the Libramiento Norte in Morelia generated a public demand for justice from social groups.
> 
> According to initial reports, the victim was found Saturday morning in a drainage ditch located on the median, near Huaniqueo Street, in the Solidaridad neighborhood, and showed signs of violence.
> 
> Unofficially, it was reported that the woman was part of the LGBT community, which prompted a statement from the Pride Michoacán collective, which requested that the case be investigated with a gender, diversity and human rights perspective.
> 
> The organization expressed its position on the matter through social media:
> 
> “Hate crimes must stop!
> @fgemichoacan we demand an investigation with a gender and diversity perspective.
> Her death must not go unpunished and those responsible must be held accountable.
> Rest in peace, Daniela, cross the rainbow bridge”
> 
> The group asked the authorities to ensure that the case does not go unpunished and that justice is guaranteed for the victim.
