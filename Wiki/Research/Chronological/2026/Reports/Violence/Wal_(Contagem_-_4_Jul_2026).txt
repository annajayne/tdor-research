> A 45-year-old transgender woman was stabbed to death on Saturday night (July 4th) in the Carajás neighborhood, in Contagem, in the Metropolitan Region of Belo Horizonte . The suspect, a 21-year-old man, was beaten by residents, tied to a traffic sign , and ended up confessing to the crime to the Military Police (PMMG) .
> 
> The officers who responded to the incident found the woman on the bed, with several stab wounds and burn marks on her hair. A knife and two lighters were seized at the scene.
> 
> Police found the suspect tied to a traffic sign, being beaten by men with pieces of wood and stones after they learned of the murder . The perpetrators of the attack were not located.
> 
> Since the suspect was injured, he received medical attention and was taken to the UPA Ressaca emergency care unit. During questioning by the police, he confessed to the crime, according to the Military Police.
> 
> According to the suspect's account, the two met at a liquor store, where they drank and used drugs together, and then went to the transgender woman's house, where the crime occurred.
> 
> The suspect stated that, at one point, the victim allegedly attempted to rape him by trying to remove his clothing without consent. In response, the suspect claims he struck her multiple times.
> 
> According to the testimony, he remained in the residence after the crime and even slept there. Afterwards, he tried to set the body on fire using a lighter, left the property, and returned to a liquor store, where he continued consuming alcoholic beverages .
> 
> In a statement, the Civil Police reported that they received the report through the State Digital Duty Center. The suspect, 21 years old, was taken to the police station, questioned, and formally charged with homicide. He was then held at the disposal of the Justice system. After a custody hearing, he will remain in custody.

> A 45-year-old trans woman was stabbed to death on Saturday night (4), in the Carajás neighborhood, in Contagem, in the Metropolitan Region of Belo Horizonte. The suspect of the crime, a 21-year-old man, was arrested in flagrante after being located and assaulted by residents of the region.
> 
> According to the Military Police (PMMG), the victim and the perpetrator met at a local liquor store, where they consumed alcoholic beverages and narcotics. Afterwards, they both went to the woman's residence to continue drinking.
> 
> Inside the property, a disagreement arose between the two, at which point the man grabbed a knife and stabbed the victim multiple times. After the murder, the suspect remained in the house and slept. Upon waking, he attempted to set fire to the woman's body and the house, but the flames did not spread.
> 
> According to the police, the man left the scene and returned to the bar where he had met the victim, where he recounted what had happened. Locals in the area identified him and began assaulting him with sticks and stones. When the military police arrived at the indicated location, they found the suspect injured, bloodied, and tied to a traffic sign. He was taken to the UPA Ressaca (emergency care unit).
> 
> During medical treatment, the young man confessed to the murder to the police. The case was forwarded to the Civil Police (PCMG), which will be responsible for investigating the motives and circumstances of the crime.
