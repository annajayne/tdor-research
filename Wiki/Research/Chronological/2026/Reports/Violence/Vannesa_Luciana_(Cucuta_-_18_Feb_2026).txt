> Vanessa Luciana found dead in a dirt road in Cúcuta
> Friday, February 20, 2026
> 
> A Venezuelan trans woman, identified as Vanessa Luciana, was found dead on a path near the Western Ring Road of the city of Cúcuta, in Norte de Santander.
> 
> The body was discovered in the early hours of Wednesday, February 18, by residents of the area. The body showed signs of violence.
> 
> Vanessa Luciana became very popular at the recent 57th edition of the Border Carnival, due to her dancing style and eccentric personality.
> 
> It was learned that Vanesa Luciana had emigrated from Portuguesa to Norte de Santander, where she resided. Authorities are investigating the murder to find those responsible.
