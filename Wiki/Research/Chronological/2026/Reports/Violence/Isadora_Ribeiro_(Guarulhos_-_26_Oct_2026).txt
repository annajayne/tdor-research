> A transgender woman identified as Isadora Ribeiro was found dead inside a residence on Monday (26), in Jardim São João. The victim's boyfriend was arrested on suspicion of involvement in the death. The case is being investigated by the Civil Police, who are seeking to clarify the circumstances of the incident. According to initial information from the incident, the Military Police were called to respond to a death at a property located on the Guarulhos-Nazaré road. Upon arriving at the address, the police found Isadora lifeless and with signs of violence, including injuries to the neck area.
> 
> In his statement to the police, the boyfriend reported that the two had an argument during the early morning hours. According to the suspect's version of events, he left the residence to buy cigarettes and prevent the disagreement from escalating. Shortly afterward, he decided to return to the property.
> 
> The man stated that upon returning home, he found his partner hanged in the entrance hallway of the residence. According to his account, he cut the sheet from which she was hanging and attempted resuscitation maneuvers, including CPR.
> 
> The version presented by the suspect, however, will be confronted with the results of the forensic examination. During the analysis of the scene, injuries were identified that, according to the initial information about the incident, were not clarified by the man's account. Among the injuries observed were cuts in the neck area, a circumstance that raised questions about the circumstances of the death.
> 
> During the response to the incident, police seized five hunting knives, a sharpener, an object resembling a sword, as well as a cell phone and a tablet. The materials were collected and may assist in the progress of the investigations, according to the analysis of the responsible authorities.
> 
> The Civil Police have launched an investigation to determine what happened at the residence in Jardim São João. The case was initially registered as a suspicious death, and the police authority requested the preventive detention of Isadora's boyfriend, who was arrested on suspicion of involvement in the case.
> 
> The victim's body was sent to the Institute of Forensic Medicine (IML), where it will undergo examinations that may help determine the circumstances of the death. The forensic results should also assist in analyzing the identified injuries and verifying the information provided by the suspect.
> 
> As part of the investigation, the police are seeking security camera footage from the area and will interview witnesses who may provide relevant information about the events. The investigation is ongoing to clarify the dynamics of the case and determine any potential liabilities. Until the investigation is concluded, the circumstances of Isadora Ribeiro's death remain under investigation.

> São Paulo — A transgender woman was found dead inside her home with cuts to her neck and hanged , on Monday morning (October 26), in Guarulhos, metropolitan region of São Paulo. The victim's boyfriend was arrested on suspicion of involvement in the crime. He admitted that the couple had a fight before she was found dead.
> 
> According to the Military Police, the initial report was of a suspected suicide at a house on the Guarulhos-Nazaré road, in the São João neighborhood.
> 
> At the scene, Bruno Valdevino Nascimento said that he had argued with his girlfriend Isadora Ribeiro during the early hours of the morning, left the house, and went to buy cigarettes to prevent the "situation from getting worse."
> 
> On the way, he said, he regretted his actions and decided to return to make amends, but upon entering the residence he found his partner hanged in the entrance hallway.
> 
> Nascimento's version of events is that he cut the sheet his girlfriend was hanging from, performed CPR to try and revive her, but she ended up dying. Despite this statement, forensic experts found injuries not reported by the suspect, such as cuts on the victim's neck.
> 
> Police seized five hunting knives, a sharpener, a sword-shaped object, as well as a cell phone and a tablet.
> 
> The Civil Police opened an investigation to gather more details about the death and requested the preventive detention of the suspect.
> 
> Metrópoles contacted the Court of Justice (TJSP) to inquire whether the request was granted and is awaiting a response.
