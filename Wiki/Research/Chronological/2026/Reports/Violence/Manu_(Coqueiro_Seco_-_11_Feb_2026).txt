> The body of a young trans woman who had been missing was found on Wednesday (11), in the municipality of Coqueiro Seco , in the metropolitan region of Maceió . During searches carried out by family and friends, the young woman's father found her head . The Civil Police are investigating the case.
> 
> According to information gathered by TV Asa Branca Alagoas , the victim was identified as Jhonata Amaro da Silva , was 19 years old and used the social name "Manu" . She had been missing since last Saturday (7).
> 
> The young woman had no prior criminal record, and the murder may be related to drug trafficking, according to police.
> 
> Hours after finding the head, Manu's body was located by firefighters in a ravine approximately 200 meters deep, on the Fazenda das Flores farm, located in the municipality. It was handed over to the Forensic Medical Institute (IML).
> 
> The Civil Police reported that during the forensic examination of the body, at least six gunshot wounds were identified, five to the head and one to the chest . No ammunition casings or the firearm were found at the crime scene.
> 
> The forensic team seized a knife near the victim's head , which may have been used in the decapitation. The object will undergo technical analysis.
> 
> TV Asa Branca Alagoas also learned that after the body was found, a suspect came forward to the police, gave a statement, and was released . As of the publication of this report, no one has been arrested.
> 
> The State Council for Combating Discrimination and Promoting the Rights of the LGBTQIA+ Population of Alagoas condemned the crime and stated that "the case provokes outrage, pain, and dismay."
> 
> The council reiterated that it demands a thorough, swift, and transparent investigation from the authorities to hold those responsible accountable.
> 
> Manu was a student and former member of the Brilho Lunar quadrille group. On social media, a post was made in her honor, lamenting her death and emphasizing the joy the victim had experienced.
> 
> "Her presence illuminated our rehearsals, our performances, and above all, our lives. Manu wasn't just a part of Brilho Lunar – she was brilliance, she was affection, she was shared joy in every step, in every smile, in every moment lived by our side," read part of the post.




> The body of a young transgender woman who had been missing was found headless and with numerous signs of violence in a wooded area in the municipality of Coqueiro Seco, in the metropolitan region of Maceió. The case was registered this Wednesday, the 11th.
> 
> The victim was known as Manu, 17 years old, but had the death name (a name a trans person used before beginning gender transition) of "Jhonatan," and had been missing since last Saturday, the 7th. She resided in the municipality and was still a student. José Cícero, an acquaintance of the young woman, said that she always participated in local culture, joining quadrille dances and coco de roda (a traditional Brazilian dance).
> 
> The news outlet received images of the victim's body; however, due to the high level of violence, the photos will not be released. The head was found first, around 7:30 am, by the victim's own father by the side of the road. The body was located hours later in a gully in the dense wooded area, around 1:20 pm, about 300 meters downstream.
> 
> Her right arm was completely bruised, and there were several holes in her head, similar to gunshot wounds. She was wearing only a piece of underwear on her lower body.
> 
> The Military Police of Alagoas were called to the scene and are awaiting the Fire Department to remove the body, which is in a difficult-to-access location. The Criminalistics Institute (IC) and the Forensic Medical Institute (IML) are also present to conduct the forensic examination and collect the body.
> 
> According to the Military Police, a suspect, who allegedly confessed to the crime, gave a statement at the police station but did not reveal the location of the body. He appeared accompanied by his uncle and was released shortly afterward. The police reported that he was a member of the Comando Vermelho faction.
