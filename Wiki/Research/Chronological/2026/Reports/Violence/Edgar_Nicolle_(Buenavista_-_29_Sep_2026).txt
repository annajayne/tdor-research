> We deeply mourn the killing of Edgar Nicolle, a trans person from Buenavista, Sucre. She passed away at a clinic in Barranquilla after falling victim to a violent attack in Magangué.
>
> The incident has caused shock and sadness in her municipality, as she was known for her joy and kindness.



> A well-known young content creator from Buena Vista died after suffering physical assault. 
>
> Edgar Nicolle, originally from Buena Vista, Sucre, and a resident of the Siete de Agosto neighborhood, was the man who suffered severe blows to various parts of his body in Magangué, Bolívar, several weeks prior. Due to the severity of his injuries, he was transferred to a medical center in the same municipality, where he remained hospitalized for approximately two weeks.
>
> Following health complications, Edgar had to be transferred to a more specialized clinic in Barranquilla, Atlántico, where he was scheduled to undergo surgery on one of his legs, but unfortunately, he passed away prematurely. 
>
> The motive for the attack is unknown; the authorities will be in charge of the investigation.
>
> The incident has caused sadness in the Buena Vista community, as the young man was a content creator, known for his cheerful personality and for organizing several beauty pageants. Residents are demanding that the authorities conduct a thorough investigation and apprehend those responsible.



 
 
> Edgar Correa Dávila, affectionately known as “Edgar Nicole,” passed away after several days of medical treatment for severe injuries sustained during an alleged physical assault.
> 
> According to reports, Edgar was attacked with sticks in the municipality of Magangué, Bolívar. The attack caused serious injuries, requiring him to be hospitalized in that town for nearly two weeks.
> 
> He was later transferred to Barranquilla, where he underwent surgery on one of his legs. However, despite the medical care he received, he ultimately succumbed to his injuries.
> 
> His passing has caused grief and shock in Buenavista, Sucre, especially among family, friends, and acquaintances. There, “Edgar Nicole” was a beloved figure, remembered for his cheerful, spontaneous, and approachable personality.
> 
> Authorities will need to clarify the circumstances of the alleged attack and determine what happened, as well as assign responsibility for these events.



**Bio**: Correa Dávila was a well-known trans person, content creator, and cultural organizer from Buenavista, Sucre, recognized for organizing local carnival pageants and events. Human rights organization Caribe Afirmativo explicitly flagged the case, mourning the violent death as an attack on a trans community member.
