> Butantã: Trans woman killed with 5 shots by motorcyclist.
> The crime occurred in an area known for prostitution in Butantã, in the western part of São Paulo; learn more about the case.
> 24/09/2026
>  
> A trans woman was killed after being shot five times in Butantã, in the West Zone of the city of São Paulo. The incident occurred on Wednesday night (23), at the intersection of ruas Catequese and Praça Monte Castelo. A motorcyclist, the perpetrator of the shooting, fled without taking anything from her.
> 
> According to the news portal G1, neither the shooter nor the victim have yet been identified by the authorities. The crime occurred in an area known for prostitution in Butantã.
> 
> The incident was recorded by security cameras. The footage is being analyzed by the Civil Police. The case is being investigated as a murder.
> 
> In the images, the victim can be seen sitting on a bench, next to other people. A man arrives on a motorcycle, wearing a helmet and carrying a delivery bag. He stops near the group, and the woman approaches and appears to be talking to him.
> 
> Next, she hands a bag to someone who was nearby. Shortly after, the motorcyclist shoots the victim.
> 
> The police report does not clarify what was inside the bag, nor what the relationship was between the victim, the motorcyclist, and the person who received the item. The motives and circumstances of the crime are still under investigation.
> 
> Police want to know if the woman was a victim of transphobia or was killed in revenge for some other unclear reason.
> 
> According to police, five shots were fired, two of which hit the woman, in her right leg and back. After the shooting, the man fled on a motorcycle. The images also show that he entered Monte Castelo Square against the flow of traffic and, after the attack, continued towards Romão Gomes Street, in Butantã.
> 
> The victim was taken by ambulance to the University Hospital of the University of São Paulo (HU-USP), also in the Butantã region, but did not survive the injuries and died.
> 
> At the crime scene, police also found marks apparently caused by gunshots on a wall and the gate of a nearby property. However, no casings, projectiles, or other apparent traces of ammunition were found.
> 
> The forensics team was called in, and the case was registered as a homicide at the 14th Police District (DP) in Pinheiros.
> 
> According to G1, the victim used the social name Andressa and was around 30 years old, according to initial police information. Her official identity is yet to be confirmed by authorities.
> 
> According to the São Paulo State Public Security Secretariat (SSP), the body was sent to the Institute of Forensic Medicine (IML), where it will undergo a necropsy and fingerprint identification.
