Both names are masculine in Italian; I am including both for completeness as most news outlets only report the first one.

> Their mother and them were murdered by their father after tensions due to their sexual orientation (they previously identified as a gay man), financial issues, and, presumably, their "considered" gender transition. I would count this as an homicide of a trans person, since it happened very close to their supposed coming out. They were killed in Camaiore, Italy, on June 24, 2026, and they were 24 years old. 



> Listening and reading about the death of Mirko Moricone, I learn more and more from sources that this person wanted to embark on a path of gender affirmation. Therefore, we are not dealing with a gay person, but with a trans person. And the difference is not secondary: it is radical.
>
> Transgender identity refers to the entire set of perceptions and projections of oneself, the presence of a gender dysphoria, that is, a non-correspondence, diagnosed by doctors, between the gender assigned at birth and the one experienced for oneself. Gay, on the other hand, refers to the sexual orientation: who you like, towards whom you feel attraction. These are two completely different levels. One concerns who you are, the other concerns who you love.
>

>

People who do not know what they are talking about, who confuse everything, who reduce the complexity of a human life to a useful slogan for their own argument.

> There's one more step he would have liked to take: gender transition . Mirko Moriconi, killed yesterday afternoon along with his mother Kety by his father in front of their home in Camaiore, had long been out as gay . This sparked countless family arguments: he was on one side, his mother supported him, his father opposed them. But recently, the 24-year-old seemed to have also been planning to begin a process of gender transition . He had confided this to his closest friends. Those with whom he could truly express himself honestly, to be himself without fear of judgment or threats. It seems he hadn't yet contacted medical facilities or specialists. But he had begun to talk about this idea.
> 
> It's still unclear whether he confided this to his family , nor whether this decision may have further exacerbated the tense tension within the home. Several neighbors report constant screams, even in the middle of the night , coming from their apartment. Just a week ago, an ambulance was called, fearing the worst. A scenario that came to fruition yesterday when Moriconi, 63, grabbed his hunting rifle and shot his wife, Kety , 52, and the boy.
>
> He had changed his name, he was called Michelangelo
>
> A neighbor of the Moriconi family: "We often heard them arguing."
> 
> Mirko had already changed his name. He did so in 2022. He began calling himself Michelangelo Andreoni, thus taking the surname of his mother, to whom he was very close. Carabinieri investigators are now delving into the twenty-four-year-old's past and family relationships. They are trying to understand every possible motive and what was the final straw that triggered his father's blind rage. And whether this idea of ​​undergoing gender transition might also have played a role, creating new friction.


> Versilia, man kills son and wife. The boy said in a video, "My father thinks it's better to be dead than gay."
> After the double homicide in Camaiore, the man climbed onto the roof of the house and was later arrested by the Carabinieri. "I got rid of them."
> June 24, 2026
> 
> He kills his son and wife in Versilia. A 63-year-old man, Piero Moriconi , shot his wife, Kathy Andreoni, 52, and his 24-year-old son, Mirko, with a rifle . It happened in the early afternoon of today, June 24, in Camaiore (Lucca). Shortly before 3:00 PM, the man grabbed a rifle and opened fire in the home. He was arrested shortly thereafter by the Carabinieri. The investigation is focusing on an act that culminated years of conflict. The father was unable to accept his son's homosexuality.
> 
> The young man also posted a video in which he said that after he came out, his parent had commented: “Better dead than gay.”
> 
> The alarm was raised by a nephew who, hearing the shots, approached the house and requested immediate help. The 118 operations center received the call at 2:32 PM, reporting a shooting in progress in Camaiore, in the Pieve area, and the presence of an armed man on the roof of the house.
> 
> Several emergency vehicles were dispatched to the scene: the Viareggio 118 medical car, an ambulance with nurses from the Misericordia di Camaiore hospital, the Viareggio Red Cross, the Pegaso 3 air ambulance, and the Carabinieri (Italian Carabinieri) police, in addition to a call for the fire department. Once the area was secured by law enforcement, paramedics confirmed the death of the woman and the young man. The perpetrator remained on the scene until the Carabinieri arrived. An investigation is underway to reconstruct the precise sequence of events and clarify the context in which the tragedy unfolded.
>
> "I got rid of them"
>
> This is how Moriconi, a 63-year-old bricklayer, allegedly justified the murder of his wife and son. To relatives and neighbors who rushed to his home in Pieve di Camaiore, he later told them he had put the hunting rifle used to shoot the two back in the house.
> 
> Mirko was an artist. He rapped , and on social media (where he signed with a different name, Michelangelo, and his mother's surname, Andreoni), on Mother's Day he posted a long poem with music dedicated to Kathy, a clear sign of his immense affection and connection with her: "You are the essence of silence when I don't need words, you are love, you are sweetness, understanding." But in one of his videos, he also talked about how his father hated his homosexuality.
