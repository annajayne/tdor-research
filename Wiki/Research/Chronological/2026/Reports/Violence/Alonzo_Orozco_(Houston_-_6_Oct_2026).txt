> Jose Alonzo Orozco, 23, has been identified as the person killed at a home in the 4400 block of Sandydale Ln, near John F. Kennedy Blvd and Aldine Mail Route Rd. His family described Jose as someone full of light and joy who was deeply loved by those who knew him.
> 
> Harris County Sheriff’s Office Deputies responded to the home after an out-of-state man requested a welfare check. According to HCSO Homicide Sgt. Michael Ritchie, the caller said his brother had told him he killed his girlfriend.
> 
> Deputies found Jose unresponsive with multiple stab wounds. He was pronounced deceased at the scene.
> 
> According to HCSO, Jose and 20-year-old Jaime Castillo had been in a relationship. HPD located Castillo and took him into custody. Following consultation with the Harris County District Attorney’s Office, Castillo was charged with Murder and booked into the Harris County Jail.
> 
> Jose’s family is raising money to help cover funeral expenses and honor his memory. They said any donations, shares, prayers and support are deeply appreciated as they cope with this heartbreaking loss.
> 
> Our thoughts and prayers are with Jose’s family and loved ones during this difficult time.
