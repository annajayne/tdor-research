> Person found dead in Olanchito identified: belonged to the LGBTIQ+ community
> Organizations demand urgent action to stop violence against the LGBTIQ+ community and guarantee protection in Honduras.
> 
> Jason Morazán
> July 8, 2026 - 9:16 am
> 
> Image of person found dead in Olanchito identified: belonged to the LGBTIQ+ community
> A violent incident occurred on the night of Tuesday, July 7th, in Olanchito, Yoro , resulting in the death of a man belonging to the LGBTQ+ community. The case has generated dismay among his acquaintances and representatives of that community.
> 
> The victim was identified as Abner Ely Padilla, known as "Salomé ," the name she used as a transgender woman. According to preliminary information, unknown assailants killed her.
> 
> However, authorities have not confirmed the victim's cause of death. However, witnesses have indicated on social media that she was violently murdered on the streets where she worked as a sex worker.
> 
> In response to this situation, the National Police have already launched an investigation to clarify the violent incident, while organizations defending the rights of the LGBTIQ+ community are demanding justice and better conditions for this sector of the population in the country.
> 
> Through social media, friends, acquaintances, and community members have mourned Salomé's passing and expressed messages of affection and sadness at her departure.
> 
> Similarly, it was revealed that the victim was originally from the community of Guadalupe Carney, in Trujillo, where his family awaited the arrival of his remains, which were transferred from Yoro to the morgue in La Ceiba.
> 
> His wake and burial took place there. Amidst their grief, his family called for justice and peace for the community.
> 
> "My deepest condolences to the family," "I always advised her that the world is full of evil," "The streets are tough," "Oh, son, how much pain you have left in my heart," "I am so sorry that they took your life, my friend," "She was beautiful, what a shame"; are some of the comments that can be read.
> 
> According to the  Human Rights Observatory of LGBTIQ+ People , Honduras faces a critical situation regarding violence against this population.
> 
> "Violent deaths against LGBTIQ+ people represent a historic and persistent humanitarian crisis, with at  least 40 documented homicides in 2025 ," the Observatory report states.
