> A man kills his ex-partner in the middle of the street in Figueres (Girona)
> The detainee, 48, had a restraining order against the victim, 33, which he had violated twice in just two days.
> Girona - MAY 19, 2026 - 15:02 WEST
> 
> A man from Figueres (Alt Empordà, Girona) who had a restraining order against him stabbed his partner to death early Tuesday afternoon and was arrested at the scene. According to initial reports from municipal sources, the incident occurred in the street, in Plaça Josep Tarradellas in the capital of Alt Empordà, and was witnessed by numerous people who recorded the scene with their cell phones and managed to detain the assailant after confronting him. The attacker was arrested at the scene after brutally assaulting his ex-partner, stabbing her in the chest, back, arms, and neck.
> 
> According to police sources, the fatal attack took place around 2:45 p.m., when the man, Andrés RC, 48, a Spanish national with a long criminal record, violated a restraining order against him. He had been ordered to stay away from the victim, a 33-year-old transgender woman of Honduran origin, and killed her, according to sources close to the case, in an attack described as “extremely brutal.” The alleged attacker acted with extreme coldness, discarding the murder weapon, calmly walking across the square, and going to a fountain to wash his arms, which were covered in the victim's blood, as seen in a 15-second video circulating on social media. Half a dozen people confronted, chased, and cornered him. He defended himself by attempting to hit them, but they eventually managed to knock him to the ground and restrain him while they waited for the police to arrive.
> 
> The victim lay sprawled on the sand at one end, next to the bench where she had been sitting, to the shock and despair of numerous witnesses who called emergency services for an ambulance, as seen in another video lasting just under two minutes. During that time, no one is seen approaching to help her. Many residents who were in the centrally located square, near a primary and secondary school, filmed the incident, which quickly spread to social media.
> 
> Several patrols from the Figueres Municipal Police, the Mossos d'Esquadra (Catalan Police), and paramedics from the Medical Emergency System (SEM) arrived at the scene, but were unable to save her life. According to sources close to the investigation, the detainee, with a long history of violence, was arrested on Sunday for abuse and threats, and again on Monday for assault, abuse, property damage, and violating a restraining order. After appearing before a judge and being released on Monday, he killed his ex-partner.
> 
> Sources at the High Court of Justice of Catalonia have confirmed that a summary trial was held on Monday in courtroom 7 of the Figueres Court of First Instance, which has jurisdiction over cases of violence against women. There, the alleged perpetrator of the murder "accepted a plea bargain for domestic abuse, receiving a six-month prison sentence and a restraining order prohibiting him from approaching the victim within 250 meters and communicating with her for one year and four months." Hours later, the man was arrested again for "alleged injuries to the same victim," his ex-partner, who was summoned for a forensic examination and to appear before a duty judge in another court on Tuesday.
> 
> Since he failed to appear, the court ordered his release with restrictions, referring him to Court 7, given his prior conviction and the measures agreed upon therein, according to the same sources. Now that the young woman's death has been confirmed, that same court will handle the case for gender-based violence.
> 
> The officers from the Criminal Investigation Division (DIC) of the Mossos d'Esquadra (Catalan Police) in Girona, who arrived at the scene and conducted the Technical Police On-Site Inspection (IOTP), have taken charge of the investigation. They also recovered the knife used to stab her multiple times, which had been thrown under a parked car near the victim. The judicial delegation arrived at the scene, and at approximately 5:30 p.m., the body was removed and taken to the Institute of Legal Medicine (IMLC) in Girona, where forensic experts will perform an autopsy tomorrow.
> 
> The City Council has announced that it will hold a minute of silence this Wednesday at 11:00 a.m. in the Town Hall Square. According to his social media, the detainee is originally from Madrid, although he studied at a school in Ripoll before moving to Vilafant. His social media also shows him, exactly one year ago, with a woman and a young child, with a message that reads: “I didn’t know how to take care of what was most important, I didn’t know how to love with my soul” and “I lost him.” He also tells his son that “he is his greatest treasure” and that he “would give everything” for him.



> Shock in Spain: Man kills his ex-partner in public square
> The victim, a 33-year-old trans woman originally from Honduras, was stabbed by her ex-partner, who had a restraining order against him for violence.
> May 19, 2026 - 4:49 PM
> 
> A new case of violence has shocked Spain, after a 33-year-old transgender woman was brutally murdered by her ex-partner in the middle of a public street, in an incident that occurred this Tuesday, May 19, in the city of Figueres, province of Girona.
> 
> The victim was attacked with a knife in plaza Josep Tarradellas in the capital of Alt Empordà, a busy area close to commercial establishments and spaces frequented by families and students.
> 
> The attacker was her ex-partner, a 48-year-old Spanish man with a history of violence and a current restraining order. Despite these court restrictions, the man allegedly approached the woman several times in recent days.
> 
> According to information known so far, this crime occurred in the afternoon, when the woman was in a public place and in a matter of seconds was intercepted by Andrés RC, who attacked her repeatedly with a knife.
> 
> Videos posted on social media show the woman lying on the grass next to a bench, where she said she had been before the horrific attack. Despite the rapid arrival of medical and police teams, several people alerted the Emergency Medical System (SEM). The victim died at the scene due to the severity of her injuries, which were reportedly to her chest, arms, and neck.
> 
> This incident sparked outrage among residents of the area, especially because the assailant acted with complete coldness after attacking the woman, as can be seen in the videos. After committing the crime, he tried to walk away and wiped the blood from his arms and hands in a nearby fountain before being detained by bystanders and subsequently arrested by the Mossos d'Esquadra (Catalan Police) and the Figueres Municipal Police.
> 
> The man arrested today was reportedly recently arrested for violating a restraining order and for new episodes of violence related to the same victim.
> 
> According to the High Court of Justice of Catalonia (TSJC), the man had faced a fast-track trial in which he received a six-month prison sentence for domestic abuse and a restraining order against the woman. Even so, he ended up being released again.
> 
> This case reopened the debate in Spain about the effectiveness of protection measures for victims of assault and gender violence.
> 
> Following the murder, local authorities called for moments of silence in memory of the victim, while social organizations demanded greater protection and prevention mechanisms in the face of such cases.
