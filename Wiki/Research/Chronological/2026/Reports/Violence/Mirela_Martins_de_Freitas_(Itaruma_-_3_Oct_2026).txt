Summary of the G1 video:
The video reports on the killing of a trans woman who was stabbed to death after an argument at the bus station in Itarumã, a municipality in the southwest of Goiás, Brazil.

Key points shown and narrated:

* The incident happened at the Itarumã bus terminal.
* The victim is described as a mulher trans (trans woman).
* According to the report, she and the suspect had a discussion shortly before the attack.
* The suspect then used a knife to stab her multiple times.
* Emergency services were called, but she died at the scene.

Police are investigating:

* the motivation for the attack,
* the identity and whereabouts of the suspect,
* and whether the crime may have had transphobic elements (not confirmed in the report).
