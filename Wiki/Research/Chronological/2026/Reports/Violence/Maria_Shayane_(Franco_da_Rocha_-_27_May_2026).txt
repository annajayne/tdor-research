> The body of a transgender woman was found lifeless and bearing signs of violence in a wooded area of ​​Franco da Rocha, in the metropolitan region of São Paulo. She was only wearing underwear.
> 
> The discovery occurred after an employee at a farm located the body and alerted the Municipal Guard. The previous day, there had been a party at the property that ended around 7:00 AM. The farm owner was unable to confirm whether the woman had been present at the celebration.
> 
> It is suspected that she fell from a slab after the party. However, the police are investigating whether she died at the scene or if her body was taken to the woods. The area has been cordoned off and no suspects have been located so far.





> A transgender woman was found dead in a wooded area in Franco da Rocha, Greater São Paulo, a day after a party with about 150 people at a nearby farm. The body was found by an employee who was doing maintenance at the site. Civil Guard and fire department teams had difficulty accessing the area. Police are investigating the circumstances of the death and working to clarify what happened.
