> Murder of two transgender women in 24 hours reignites debate about hate crimes in Alagoas.
> The crimes occurred in Santa Luzia do Norte and Coruripe and are being investigated by the Civil Police.
> 24/10/2026 09:09 - Updated on 24/10/2026 09:09
> 
> Two murders of trans women were registered in Alagoas between Thursday night (22) and Friday morning (23). The crimes occurred in different municipalities: Santa Luzia do Norte, in the Metropolitan Region of Maceió, and Coruripe, on the South Coast of the state. Both incidents are being investigated by the police authorities.
> 
> One of the cases involved Paolla Paullyne Freitas Araújo de Alencar Vital, 34, known as Paolla do CRAS or Paolla Araújo. A social activist, she also ran for a seat on the Coqueiro Seco City Council in the 2024 elections, representing the MDB party, and was an alternate.
> 
> Paolla's body was found on Thursday night (22), on Rua Tiradentes in the center of Santa Luzia do Norte. The victim had gunshot wounds.
> 
> According to preliminary information from the investigation, one of the lines of inquiry being pursued by the Civil Police considers the possibility of involvement with drug trafficking in the region, in an area under the influence of a criminal faction. So far, according to the information released, no formal evidence of political or transphobic motivation for the homicide has been identified.
> 
> The case also mobilized the State Secretariat for Women and Human Rights (SEDH), which sent an official letter to the Public Security area requesting rigor and speed in the investigations. The agency reinforced the need to respect the victim's gender identity during the investigation.
> 
> On Friday morning (23), another trans woman was found dead in Alagoas. Known as Pâmela, she was in a wooded area in the village of Barreiras, in the rural area of Coruripe.
> 
> Initial suspicions suggest the victim was also shot to death. The Civil Police have opened an investigation to determine the circumstances of the crime, identify those responsible, and clarify the motive.
> 
> To date, information available on the second case is limited, and there is no confirmation of a link between the two murders.
> 
> The two crimes, recorded within a few hours of each other, once again draw attention to violence against transgender women and the importance of clarifying the circumstances of each incident.
> 
> Determining the motives is fundamental to determining the legal classification of homicides. The eventual characterization of the crime as transphobic or femicide depends on the evidence gathered during the investigations and the analysis of the circumstances of each case.
> 
> In Brazil, the Supreme Federal Court has recognized that acts of homophobia and transphobia can be classified under the terms of racism legislation. Furthermore, the application of the aggravating circumstance of femicide to transgender women is legally admissible, provided the relevant legal requirements are met.
> 
> The investigations into the two murders remain under the responsibility of the Civil Police. Identifying the perpetrators and clarifying the motives will be crucial for the progress of the police procedures.
