> Police in Bahawalnagar, Pakistan, have detained two suspects after a transgender person, known as Zoya, died from injuries sustained in an alleged attack.
> 
> Law enforcement said Zoya was attacked by four people on 23 October and died the following day. Police have registered a murder case against four suspects, and said two of them, reportedly brothers, were detained.
> 
> UA.NEWS published an account saying Zoya was assaulted with sticks while travelling with her sister, Mehnaz Bano, to the DHQ district hospital.
> 
> Law enforcement shared Zoya had filed a complaint previously, against the people she named over harassment and violence, who had allegedly sent death threats too. The same police account said the suspects allegedly wanted a relationship with her and began harassing and threatening her after she refused.
> 
> Police said Zoya registered a complaint at the B Division police station about a month before the attack.




> BAHAWALNAGAR - The Bahawalnagar district police arrested two suspects in connection with the murder a young transgender person, police sources said on Monday.  According to the preliminary police investigation, the suspects killed Rashid alias Zoya in the limits of Farooqabad area of Bahawalnagar. The deceased was former employee of the DPO Office’s Tahafuz Khidmat Markaz.
> 
> Reportedly, transgender person Rashid alias Zoya was tortured to death. The City B Division Police registered a case of murder of Transgender Rashid alias Zoya on the complaint of his sister Mehnaz Bano and arrested two accused persons. In the FIR, 757/26 the victim’s sister Mehnaz Bano told the police that he and his brother Rashid alias Zoya were going to the hospital to get medicine.
> 
> Meanwhile, the accused attacked Rashid alias Zoya with batons and sticks and severely beat him up. Rashid alias Zoya succumbed to his injuries. He had previously got registered a case number 1002/25 under Section 506/506 B of the IPC against the same accused persons. The accused had tortured him out of resentment over the previous case.  The transgender community gathered after receiving information about the unfortunate incident. On the orders of District Police Officer Muhammad Imran, the police arrested the accused involved in the killing. In this regard, the District Police Officer said that that the demands of justice will be fulfilled for the heirs of the deceased.
