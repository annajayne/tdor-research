> A 21-year-old transgender man from Russia found dead in an apartment in Batumi
> SOVA SOVA 14 hours ago
> Reports of the young man’s death emerged in the media on August 22—police entered the apartment of Dmitry Menyaev after neighbors complained of a foul odor. According to the family, no visible signs of violence were found on the body.
> 
> The body was discovered on August 15, and a forensic examination to establish the cause of death has already been conducted, reports the Georgian Service of Radio Free Europe/Radio Liberty. According to neighbors, the young man stayed indoors almost all the time, worked remotely, and ordered food and other necessities largely online—couriers from Glovo and Wolt were frequently seen near his apartment.
> 
> The family leans toward the theory of murder rather than suicide,» attorney Zviad Markoidze told Radio Free Europe/Radio Liberty. According to him, the deceased had a warm, positive relationship with his loved ones, though neither his brother nor his sister considered his disappearance from messaging apps suspicious, and neither filed a missing person report with the police.
> 
> The family received official notification of Dmitry’s death from the Georgian authorities only after his body had been found.

> A 21-year-old Russian citizen, Dmitry Magomedovich Menyaev, who had lived in Georgia since 2022, was found dead in Batumi.
> 
> The young man was born in Moscow, lived for a time in St. Petersburg, and then moved to Georgia. In recent years he had been living in his brother's apartment in Batumi.
> 
> One relative describes him as a very quiet, calm, and intelligent young man. She says he was very handsome and highly intelligent. He grew up largely without parents, raised by his older brother, and had been living alone in Georgia in recent years.
> 
> According to his brother, he was murdered.
> 
> On August 15, rescuers discovered his body after neighbors reported a strong smell coming from the apartment.
> Relatives last spoke with him on August 1. He was last online on August 3.
> 
> According to his brother, traces were found in the apartment that could indicate a violent death. The victim's brother was present during the examination of the scene. According to him, evidence was found at the scene that could suggest a third person was present in the apartment at the time of death.
> 
> Family and friends believe police are not giving these circumstances the attention they deserve.
