> RISARALDA, COLOMBIA (JUNE 9, 2026) – A judge ordered the detention of Mariana Mejía Sánchez, 33, in a prison facility. Authorities have identified her as the alleged perpetrator of the murder of Adhara Andrea Benavides Álvarez, 32, in events that occurred in the municipality of Dosquebradas.
> 
> According to the investigation, the case was registered at around 3:10 a.m. on June 7th at Carrera 16 with Calle 47 in the Los Naranjos neighborhood.
> 
> 
> According to the Prosecutor's Office, Mariana Mejía Sánchez allegedly had an altercation with Adhara Andrea Benavides Álvarez, a member of the LGBTQ+ community, and during the confrontation, she allegedly used a bladed weapon, possibly a knife, to attack her. The victim suffered multiple injuries.
> 
> Upon arriving at the scene, uniformed police officers found a woman lying on the ground and several people present pointed to Mariana Mejía Sánchez as the alleged aggressor.
> 
> Adhara Andrea was transported by ambulance to Santa Monica Hospital, where she was admitted with severe injuries to her chest, torso, arms, and legs. Despite resuscitation efforts by medical personnel, she died due to the severity of her injuries.
> 
> Since the victim was not carrying identification documents upon arrival, her body was initially taken to the Forensic Medicine Institute as an unidentified body. Later, through forensic procedures and fingerprint verification, her identity was fully established.
> 
> The 17th Local Prosecutor's Office (URI) of Pereira charged Mariana Mejía Sánchez with homicide. The defendant pleaded not guilty, and the Second Municipal Court of Belén de Umbría granted the prosecution's request, ordering her transfer to a prison while the legal proceedings continue.
> 
> The Plataformas Foundation condemned the murder of Adhara Andrea Benavides Álvarez, known by her gender identity name Abi Bexo, and requested that the investigation proceed with a human rights approach, in addition to implementing actions that contribute to guaranteeing the protection of the trans population.
>
> A trans woman was murdered in a fight: she was stabbed multiple times on an avenue in this city in the Coffee Region.
> In the early hours of Sunday, June 7, a transgender woman was murdered during a fight. Authorities have arrested the suspected perpetrator.
> To date, 19 homicides have occurred in Dosquebradas, according to figures from Legal Medicine.
> 
> In the early hours of Sunday, June 7, a trans woman was murdered in the middle of a fight  that occurred in Dosquebradas (Risaralda). 
> 
> The incident occurred  on Avenida Simón Bolívar in the Los Naranjos sector, a few blocks from the police station in the industrial municipality, where the victim was injured with a sharp weapon. 
> 
> The victim was identified as Adhara Andrea Benavides Álvarez, born in 1994 in Bogotá, with an ID card issued in Armenia, but with updated information in Dosquebradas. 
> 
> The woman received about ten wounds on her body, which, despite efforts to help her, caused her death in a hospital as a result of the injuries.
> 
> The Medical Examiner's Office indicated that they are looking for the victim's family members,  as they have not yet come to claim the body. 
> 
> The authorities reacted quickly and found the person believed to be responsible for the murder. 
> 
> According to information provided by the National Police, they were able to locate and arrest the alleged aggressor shortly after the incident occurred.
> 
> While investigations continue  to fully clarify the circumstances of what happened , the Mayor's Office of Dosquebradas expressed its rejection of this new act of violence that brings grief to the municipality.
> 
> In a statement, social manager  Jackeline Moná Raigoza and the municipal administration expressed their solidarity with the family, friends and loved ones of the victim, as well as with the LGBTIQ+ community.
> 
> The social manager regretted what happened and reiterated the institutional commitment  to protecting the lives and rights of all citizens.
> 
> "We reject any manifestation of violence. Together with the authorities, we continue working to strengthen security, promote coexistence, and protect the lives of all residents of Dosquebradas . No difference can be resolved through violence, and we will continue to promote actions that allow us to build a safer and more respectful city,"  stated Jackeline Moná Raigoza.
> 
> The municipal administration reported that there is ongoing coordination with the National Police, the Attorney General's Office, and other competent bodies to help clarify the facts and strengthen preventative actions in the territory.
> 
> Authorities called on citizens to reject all forms of violence, discrimination, and intolerance, and reiterated the need to continue building safe spaces where respect, inclusion, and healthy coexistence prevail.
> 
> For its part, the National Police reiterated its call to resolve conflicts peacefully and assured that it will continue working to guarantee security and citizen coexistence in the municipality.
