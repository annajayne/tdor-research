> NOTE The Municipal Council for Sexual Diversity expresses deep regret at the death of Maria Vanúbia, a trans woman from Franco da Rocha, found lifeless in the municipality of Franco da Rocha on May 27, 2026. 
> 
> We received this news with sadness, indignation and concern, especially in a context where trans people and transvestites still face situations of violence, social exclusion, prejudice and vulnerability on a daily basis. 
> 
> At this time, we sympathize with family, friends, and the entire LGBTQIAPN+ community that feels this loss in a painful way. Every life matters, and no existence can be treated with invisibility or neglect. 
> 
> We reinforce the importance of a rigorous, responsible and transparent investigation of the facts by the competent authorities, ensuring that all the circumstances of the case are properly investigated. 
> 
> The Municipal Council for Sexual Diversity remains committed to the defense of dignity, human rights, respect for diversity and the fight against all forms of violence against LGBTQIAPN+ people. 
> 
> Maria Vanúbia deserves respect, memory and justice. 
> 
> Franco da Rocha, May 28, 2026. 



> The body of a trans woman was found lifeless and with marks of violence in a forest area of Franco da Rocha, in the metropolitan region of São Paulo. She was only in her underwear.
> 
> The discovery happened after an employee of a farm located the body and called the Municipal Guard. The day before, there was a party at the site that ended around 7 am. The owner of the farm could not say if the woman was present at the celebration.
> 
> The suspicion is that she fell from a slab after the party. However, the police are investigating whether she died on the spot or had her body taken to the woods. The area has been cordoned off and no suspects have been located so far.

> A transgender woman was found dead in a wooded area in Franco da Rocha, Greater São Paulo, a day after a party with about 150 people at a nearby farm. The body was found by an employee who was doing maintenance at the site. Civil Guard and fire department teams had difficulty accessing the area. Police are investigating the circumstances of the death and working to clarify what happened.
