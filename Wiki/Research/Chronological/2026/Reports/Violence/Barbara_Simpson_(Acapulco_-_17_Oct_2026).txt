> They demand justice for Bárbara Simpson, a trans woman murdered in Acapulco
> October 19, 2026 Tags: Access to Justice , Murders , LGBTTI Rights , Sexual Diversity , Guerrero , Impunity
> Home » Blog » Demands for Justice for Bárbara Simpson, Trans Woman Murdered in Acapulco
> 
> LGBTI+ community groups condemned the murder of Bárbara Simpson in the Zapata neighborhood of Acapulco. Simpson, a trans woman, was killed on October 17. The LGBTI+ collective " Orgullo Guerrero " condemned the murder of the well-known member of the gay community. The Trans-Vive Support Center in Iguala, AC, demanded that the Guerrero Attorney General's Office clarify the circumstances of Bárbara Simpson's murder.
> 
> Bárbara was the Reyna Gay 2026 [Gay Queen 2026] of the Zapata neighborhood, and from a very young age she bravely fought to stand out in the LGBTI+ community. With Bárbara Simpson's case, Guerrero has recorded at least eight hate crimes in 2026, including four cases of transfemicide : Ares Navarrete , in Ocotito; Estrella Vázquez , in the municipality of Azoyú; and Alexa Valadéz , on July 30 in the municipality of Iguala.
> 
> According to figures documented by the Trans-Vive Support Center, Guerrero has registered 166 murders of people of sexual diversity from 2000 to date, without any known arrests or substantial progress in the corresponding investigations.
> 
> Given this alarming situation, they called on the competent authorities to act with diligence, transparency and commitment, and to guarantee access to justice, not only for Bárbara, but also for all people of sexual diversity whose lives have been taken.
> 
> In this context of impunity, the Guerrero Diverse State Front urged the authorities of the Guerrero Congress to legislate to properly classify the murder of trans women in the state.
