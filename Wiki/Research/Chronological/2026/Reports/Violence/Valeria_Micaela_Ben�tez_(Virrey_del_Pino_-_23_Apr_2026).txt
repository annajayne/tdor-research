> A 37-year-old transgender woman was found murdered inside her home in Virrey del Pino, La Matanza . The main hypothesis of the investigation points to her partner, a 28-year-old man who remains at large .
> 
> The victim, identified as Micaela Valeria Benítez, was found dead in her apartment on calles Santiago del Estero and Horacio Quiroga, at kilometer 44 of Route 3.
> 
> Upon entering the premises, the police discovered that the woman was lying on the floor of one of the rooms , with multiple stab wounds in different parts of her body, mainly on her neck, chest and back.
> 
> Meanwhile, a knife with bloodstains was found in the bathroom.
> 
> Initially, investigators did not rule out that the crime was linked to an attempted robbery , since, according to a neighbor, the victim had planned to sell his car, a maroon Ford Fiesta , and had even been in contact with a potential buyer.
> 
> However, as the case progressed, Benítez's partner was identified as the main suspect, who allegedly escaped after the incident, taking the victim's vehicle with him.
> 
> The accused is subject to an arrest warrant both nationally and internationally, while operations continue to find his whereabouts.
> 
> The case was assigned to prosecutor Adrián Arribas, from the Functional Unit of Homicide Investigation of La Matanza.
