> Body of Trans Woman Found with Signs of Violence in Farmland of Culiacán
> April 1, 2026 | 6:50 PM CST
> A body was found in a farmland area in Culiacán, Sinaloa, showing signs of violence. The victim's identity is currently unknown.
> 
> 
> On the afternoon of Wednesday, April 1st, the discovery of a body was reported north of Culiacán. The body showed apparent signs of violence.
> 
> The lifeless body of a person was located Wednesday afternoon in an area of ​​farmland, which is located in front of the Stanza Granada subdivision, in the La Conquista sector, located north of the Bellavista police station, in the Culiacancito district.
> 
> According to initial reports, the discovery was made minutes before 2:00 p.m., after a call was received by the 911 emergency service alerting about a deceased person, who was found bound hand and foot with tape, and lying in the middle of an agricultural property.
> 
> Authorities stated that, at the time she was found, the victim was wearing a green dress and had dyed blonde hair ; so far, these are the only characteristics that have been provided.
> 
> The identity of the victim found in farmland in Culiacán is unknown.
> 
> They also mentioned that  the body supposedly showed signs of violence, which, according to the authorities, were wounds caused by gunshots.
> 
> After the discovery was reported, officers from various police agencies arrived at the scene and cordoned off the area with yellow tape to preserve the items found there.
> 
> Minutes after the discovery , personnel from the State Attorney General's Office (FGE) arrived to conduct the corresponding investigations and document the finding. The victim's identity has not yet been released.
