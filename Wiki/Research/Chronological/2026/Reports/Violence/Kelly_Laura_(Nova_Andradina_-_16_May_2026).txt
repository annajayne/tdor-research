> Man who shot and killed transgender woman in nightclub turns himself in to police.
> The disagreement began after the author disagreed with the price charged by the establishment.
> 18/05/2026 17:23
> 
> Responsible for shooting and killing a transvestite, nightclub manager in Nova Andradina, Erik Lacerda Chagas, 32, turned himself in to police on Sunday afternoon (17). The crime occurred in the early hours of Friday (15). 
> 
> According to delegate Caio Bicalho, the perpetrator went to the establishment, accompanied by two friends, around 10 pm on Thursday (14). Four hours later, already in the early hours of Friday, the man decided to leave the place. 
> 
> “At the time the decision was made, there was a disagreement between the perpetrator and the victim, who is the manager of the establishment, regarding the amounts owed. Witnesses interviewed by the police stated that the perpetrator had paid the bill indicated by the victim and left the premises without any animosity between them. However, a few minutes after leaving, the perpetrator allegedly returned alone, already carrying a firearm, and fired several shots at the victim,” the police chief pointed out. 
> 
> The victim managed to run and hide at the back of the nightclub, where she was rescued by teams from the Fire Department. However, due to the severity of her injuries, she died on Saturday afternoon (16). 
> 
> The case will remain under investigation and the perpetrator remains at the disposal of the Judiciary. In an interview, the accused's defense attorney, Henaglyton Corneto, stated that his client claimed he was at the location with a sex worker, paid for the service, the room, and the drinks, but was charged more than he thought he should have paid.
> 
> After the commotion, Erik went home and went to sleep. However, the next day, friends told him what had happened, and he said he didn't remember. But upon realizing that he had indeed committed the crime, he decided to turn himself in to the police.
> 
> “He is married, has minor daughters, and he had a breakdown. That is unfortunately what happened. In his testimony, he stated that he regrets what he did and that he threw his whole life away, but that he voluntarily presented himself to the authorities,” declared the defense.


> Trans woman Kelly Laura dies after being shot in Nova Andradina.
> The crime occurred in the early hours of Friday (15); Erik Lacerda Chagas is identified as the main suspect and the case is being investigated by the SIG.
> Published on: May 16, 2026
> 
> The Civil Police, through the General Investigations Section (SIG), is investigating the death of transvestite Kelly Laura, victim of gunshot wounds in Nova Andradina. The crime took place in the early hours of Friday (15), and the victim died on the afternoon of this Saturday (16), at the Regional Hospital.
> 
> According to the police report, the Military Police were called around 2:40 am to respond to a report of gunshots at a bar in the city. At the scene, on Rua Espírito Santo, witnesses reported that the victim was in the establishment when a disagreement occurred with a man, who left the premises and returned armed.
> 
> According to the report, the perpetrator fired shots at Kelly Laura. The victim was rescued by the Military Fire Department and taken to the Regional Hospital, where she received medical attention, but did not survive her injuries.
> 
> During the investigation, security camera footage helped identify people who were at the scene. The name of Erik Lacerda Chagas, 32 years old, emerged as the main suspect in the crime, following information passed on to the police.
> 
> On the day of the crime, the Military Police brought in a suspect, but he was released shortly afterward without any involvement.
> 
> The investigation is being conducted by the SIG (Special Investigations Group) of Nova Andradina, under the coordination of Delegate Caio Bicalho, who will request the preventive detention of the suspect. The case remains under investigation to clarify the motivation, the circumstances of the crime, and to locate the suspect.
