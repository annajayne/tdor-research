> Transgender woman found dead on a property in Guarujá.
> The body of Kayra Kamilly, a 33-year-old transgender woman, was found in a residence in the Enseada neighborhood of Guarujá.
> 28/08/2026 16:00
> 
> The body of a 33-year-old trans woman was found on an abandoned plot of land on Thursday morning (27), in the Enseada neighborhood of Guarujá. According to the police report, military police were called to attend to an incident involving the discovery of a body on Rua Cláudio dos Santos, corner of Rua Azor Gomes de Almeida.
> 
> Upon arriving at the address, the police gained access to the property through a wooden door. Inside, they detected a strong odor and, upon further investigation, found the body, which was in an advanced state of decomposition and dressed in women's clothing.
> 
> According to the police report, the victim had breast implants and long hair. Initially, she did not have any documents that would allow her identification. Later, the victim was identified as Kayra Kamilly, a resident of Guarujá.



> A 33-year-old trans woman was found dead on Thursday afternoon (27), in a vacant lot in the Jardim Três Marias neighborhood, in Guarujá , on the coast of São Paulo . According to the Public Security Secretariat (SSP-SP) , the body showed signs of violence and was already in a state of decomposition .
> 
> In a statement, the department reported that military police were called after a complaint and located the victim on a plot of land on Rua Cláudio dos Santos, at the corner of Rua Azor Gomes de Almeida, around 5:30 pm. The body showed an apparent injury to the head , consistent with crushing .
