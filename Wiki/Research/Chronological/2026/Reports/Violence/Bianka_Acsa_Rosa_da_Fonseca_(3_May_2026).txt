> Transgender teacher dies after suffering burns to 80% of her body in an attack in Curvelo; suspect is in custody.
> Bianka Acsa Rosa da Fonseca had been undergoing treatment in Belo Horizonte since the crime, which occurred on April 7th, and her death was confirmed by the school where she worked; the Civil Police are investigating a possible motive linked to jealousy.
> 04/05/2026 at 10:40 AM — by Plox Editorial Staff
> 
> Share the news:
> A transgender woman who suffered burns to approximately 80% of her body after an attack in Curvelo , in the Central region of Minas Gerais, on April 7th, died this Sunday (May 3rd). She was undergoing treatment in Belo Horizonte , but did not survive the consequences of her injuries.
> 
> The death has been confirmed.by the Interventor Alcides Lins State School , where the victim worked as a teacher .
> 
> The victim was identified as Bianka Acsa Rosa da Fonseca . In a statement, the institution lamented the teacher's death and highlighted her career within the school community.
> 
> His daily struggle, marked by courage and determination.[Name], leaves behind a legacy of inspiration at our school. We stand in solidarity with family and friends during this time of grief.
> 
> According to the Civil Police , three days after the crime, a 25-year-old man was arrested, suspected of attempting to kill his ex-partner and also injuring the teacher, who suffered burns to approximately 80% of her body . The motive, according to the investigation, was...jealousy.
> 
> Bianka, 36, was not the intended target of the attack. Investigations indicate that the suspect planned the attack against her ex-partner, an 18-year- old who is currently the victim's partner. By using a flammable substance and setting fire to the property, the flames engulfed the teacher.
> 
> The young man identified as the target of the attack was unharmed, despite being in the house at the time of the fire. According to police chief Rodrigo Vieira Antunes , the suspect had previously made threats against his ex-partner.
> 
> After the attack, the man fled, but was located and arrested by the Civil Police after the court authorized his preventive detention . According to the police chief, the corporation highlighted the seriousness of the action and informed that the investigations continue for the complete clarification of the case.


> Transgender woman suffers burns to 80% of her body after her house is invaded and set on fire in Curvelo.
> According to the police, her boyfriend said they were sleeping when they came across the flames. The man's ex-partner is suspected of the crime.
> By Michelly Oda , Marcos Leite, g1 Grande Minas
> 
> 07/04/2026 19h20  Updated3 days ago
> 
> A 36-year-old trans woman suffered burns to 80% of her body after the house where she was sleeping with her boyfriend was invaded and set on fire in Curvelo , in the early hours of Tuesday (7). A suspect in the crime has been identified, but has not yet been located.
> 
> According to Lieutenant Rodrigo Fonseca of the Military Police, the perpetrator jumped over the wall of the residence and set fire to the kitchen. The couple heard the noise and went outside to investigate, finding the room engulfed in flames.
> 
> “While attempting to exit through the kitchen door, the female victim slipped, fell to the ground, and soiled her clothes with alcohol. As a result, her body caught fire. She went to the bathroom to try to extinguish the flames with shower water, but the perpetrator threw more flammable substance out the bathroom window, igniting her again.”
> 
> According to the lieutenant, the woman managed to escape through the front door and jumped into the pool to try to put out the flames.
> 
> The victim's partner told police that he identified the arsonist as her ex-boyfriend, who fled the scene with his clothes on fire.
> 
> "According to the applicant, he maintained a relationship with the author for three years, which ended recently."
> 
> At the hospital, police were informed that the victim's injuries were extremely serious and that she needed to be transferred to another health unit. The family informed the police that the woman suffered burns to 80% of her body.
> 
> A witness told police that the couple had been together for just over a month and that the man's ex-boyfriend had previously threatened the victim.
> 
> At the house, the military police found a gallon jug on the kitchen floor which, according to the resident, had been used by the suspect. The container was not consumed by the flames, as the fire was extinguished with a hose.
> 
> The search for the suspect continued as of the last update to this report.
