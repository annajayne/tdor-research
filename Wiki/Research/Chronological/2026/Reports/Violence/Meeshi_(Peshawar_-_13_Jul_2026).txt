> Two transgender persons, Meeshi and Tara, were shot near Chamkani Police Station in Peshawar.
> 
> According to initial reports from transgender community, Meeshi had gone with her friend Tara to recover her mobile phone and money from her intimate friend, who had refused to return them. A heated argument broke out, during which the accused opened fire on both of them.
> 
> Meeshi was killed in the attack, and her body has been handed over to her family. Tara sustained critical injuries and is currently hospitalized.
> Arzoo Khan, President of the KP Transgender Community and Executive Director of Manzil Foundation, strongly condemned this brutal killing, stating that the continued targeting of transgender persons is unacceptable and demands immediate action. She urged the KP Police to arrest the accused without delay and called on the Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa to ensure justice, strengthen protection measures, and end the impunity surrounding violence against the transgender community.


 

> PESHAWAR: One transgender person was killed and another injured after unidentified gunmen opened fire in an abandoned field near Dando Bridge, close to the vegetable market in Chamkani, police said.
> 
> According to police, the incident occurred within the jurisdiction of Chamkani Police Station late on Tuesday night. 
> 
> The deceased was identified as Mishal alias Mashi, who died at the scene, while Basir Wali, 22, son of Syed Wali, an Afridi resident of Parvez Plaza, Hashnagri, sustained injuries.
> 
> Police said initial information suggests the shooting may have stemmed from a personal dispute. A case has been registered against unidentified suspects, and an investigation is underway from multiple angles.
> 
> Authorities said the exact motive behind the attack will be determined after the investigation is completed.
