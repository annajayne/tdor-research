> A trans woman  was found dead on Thursday morning (21), under a bridge over the  Mamanguape River, in the municipality of  Itapororoca, in the Mamanguape Valley.
> 
> The structure connects the municipalities of  Itapororoca  and  Cuité de Mamanguape, according to the  Civil Police.
> 
> According to police chief  Sylvio Rabello, the victim was known as  Tutu, and the main suspicion is that she was murdered by being beaten with a stick.
> 
> According to the police chief, the body showed many signs of assault. Police also reported that the victim lived in Itapororoca and there is suspicion that the crime may be related to  drug trafficking.
> 
> The  Forensic Medicine and Dentistry Center (Numol)  of Guarabira was called in to perform the autopsy. The  Civil Police  are continuing their investigations to identify and locate the suspects involved in the case.

> According to information based on data from the Civil Police, the main suspicion is that Tutu was murdered by being beaten with a stick.
>

>
