> Young trans woman murdered in Quilichao causes pain and outrage
> 
> The murder of a young trans woman in Santander de Quilichao , Cauca, has generated profound shock within the LGBTIQ+ community. The incident, which occurred in the early hours of March 19, has reignited the debate about LGBTIQ+ violence in Colombia and the lack of protections for this population.
> 
> The case of the young trans woman murdered in Quilichao was uncovered in the Morales Duque neighborhood, where the lifeless body of Jorge Tróchez, known as "Vaiolet", a young transvestite recognized in the sector, was found.
> 
> According to preliminary information, the victim had a stab wound to the chest, near the heart, which would have caused his immediate death. He was initially unidentified, as he was not carrying any documents, but hours later community leaders confirmed his identity.
> 
> The body was transferred to Forensic Medicine with the support of the community, while investigations into this crime against a trans woman in Cauca continue .
> 
> The case of the murdered young trans woman has generated a strong reaction from social groups and human rights organizations, who reject the act and demand a quick and effective investigation.
> 
> The LGBTIQ+ community has expressed its sorrow at what they consider a new episode of violence against vulnerable populations, and has called for real guarantees of protection, as well as decisive actions to prevent these events from happening again.
> 
> Family members, neighbors, and friends remember the victim as a close and well-known person in their community, which has intensified the collective sadness in the municipality.
> 
> The case of the young trans woman murdered in Quilichao is not an isolated incident. According to local reports, at least three homicides have been recorded in the municipality in recent days, increasing fear among residents.
> 
> Recent cases include the murder of a delivery driver identified as Camilo Andrés Ipia, 33, and the homicide of Yorlady Liliana Flórez Pantoja, 25.
> 
> This series of violent events has raised alarms in the community, which is calling for a greater institutional presence and urgent action to restore security.
> 
> Beyond the statistics, the case of the murdered young trans woman leaves an open wound in society. Social organizations insist that this is not an isolated incident, but rather a structural problem related to LGBTIQ+ violence in Colombia.
> 
> As the investigations progress, the community and citizens agree on one clear message: do not normalize violence and demand justice.




> Young trans man murdered with a white gun. The body presented a wound in the chest, as high as the heart.
> 
> The murder of a person belonging to the LGBTIQ+ community is being investigated by authorities, who seek to clarify the circumstances of the incident and establish accountability.
> 
> The victim was identified as Jorge Trochez, a young trans man recognized in his community, who lived with his family in the sector where the events occurred.
> 
> According to the official report, the body presented a shotgun wound to the chest, at the height of the heart, injury that would have caused his immediate death.
> 
> Initially, the deceased could not be identified due to lack of documents. Subsequently, social leaders and residents of the sector confirmed their identity.
> 
> The corpse was found on a public road and subsequently taken to the Legal Medicine by the authorities, with the support of the community, while the relevant judicial proceedings were advancing.
> 
> The case was registered in the Morales Duque neighborhood, in the municipality of Santander de Quilichao, department of Cauca.
