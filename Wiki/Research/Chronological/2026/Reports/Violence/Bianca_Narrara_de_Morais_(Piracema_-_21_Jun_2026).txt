> Transgender woman stabbed to death in a bar in the interior of Minas Gerais.
> The 31-year-old victim died at the scene; the suspect fled after the crime and is being sought by the Military Police.
> June 22, 2026 | 4:59 PM - Updated 9 hours ago
> 
> A 31-year-old transgender woman,  identified as Bianca Narrara de Morais, was stabbed to death in the early hours of Sunday (June 21) in a bar in the city of Piracema, in the Central region of Minas Gerais. A 34-year-old man was also stabbed when he tried to intervene in the attack and was rescued alive.
> 
> According to the Military Police (PMMG), when authorities arrived at the establishment, they found the two victims lying on the ground, both with stab wounds. Near them, the police located the weapon used in the crime, which was seized.
> 
> According to information gathered at the scene, a 28-year-old man allegedly started a verbal argument with one of the victims and, during the altercation, stabbed him. The 34-year-old man tried to stop the attack but was also injured.
> 
> Bianca succumbed to her injuries and died at the scene. The other victim was rescued and taken for medical treatment. The suspect fled after the crime and has not yet been located. 
> 
> Funeral and burial
> The body of Bianca Narrara de Morais was laid to rest this Sunday (21) at the Central Municipal Funeral Home, in Piracema. The burial took place at the Cemetery of the Village of Pari – Passa Tempo.
> 
> Civil Police are investigating the crime.
> The Civil Police of Minas Gerais (PCMG) reported that it has opened an investigation to determine the authorship, motivation, and circumstances of the homicide registered in Piracema.
> The official forensic team was at the scene, carried out the standard procedures, and then the body of Bianca Narrara de Morais was sent to the region's Medical-Legal Post for forensic examinations.
> 
> According to the corporation, further information will be released during the course of the investigations, provided it does not compromise the progress of the inquiry.




> Lucas Olímpio de Carvalho, 28, suspected of murdering transgender woman Bianca Narrara de Morais, 31, was arrested this Thursday (June 25) in Carmópolis de Minas, in the Central-West region of the state.
> 
> According to the Military Police, the arrest took place around 4 pm in the village of Pará. The officers received information about the suspect's whereabouts and went to the address with an arrest warrant.
> 
> At the scene, Lucas surrendered without resistance and was taken to the Civil Police Station.
