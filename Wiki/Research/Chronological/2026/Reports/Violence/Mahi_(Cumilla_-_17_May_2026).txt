> Murder case filed after hijra individual dies from burn injuries
> Victim’s father accuses 'guruma' and associates of setting Mahi on fire in Cumilla’s Burichang
> 17 May 2026, 19:52 PM
> 
> A hijra individual who was set on fire allegedly by her "guruma" (community leader) in Cumilla’s Burichang died under treatment this afternoon.
> 
> The victim, Enamul Haque Shishir alias Mahi, had suffered severe burn injuries and died in the ICU of the National Burn Institute in Dhaka, said police.
> 
> Mahi’s father, Kamrul Haque Shohag, filed a murder case with Burichang Police Station naming three, including Dhopa Hijra Dola and Guruma Zahir, and seven-eight unidentified accused, confirmed Burichang Police Station Officer-in-Charge Lutfor Rahman.
> 
> He said police have arrested Dola alias Dhopa Hijra in connection with the killing.
> 
> According to the case statement, between 9:00pm and 10:00pm on Wednesday (May 13), Guruma Zahir and associates allegedly tied up Mahi in front of their akhra (community home) at Burichang Uttar Bazar. They then poured kerosene and set Mahi on fire.
> 
> Locals rescued Mahi and first took them to Cumilla Medical College Hospital. Later, Mahi was transferred to National Burn Institute in Dhaka where they died under treatment.
> 
> The family alleged that local police were initially unwilling to accept the case.
> 
> Burichang Police Station OC told The Daily Star that Guruma Zahir had allegedly been operating a yaba trade and extortion racket from the akhra built on government land in Burichang Uttar Bazar for nearly one and a half years. Mahi was reportedly a member of that group.




> A third-gender person was allegedly burned to death after pouring petrol on the victim during a dispute reportedly linked to drug trade rivalry in Cumilla's Burichang upazila.
> 
> The victim, Enamul Haque Shishir alias Mahi, 35, died around 5pm yesterday (17 May) while undergoing treatment at the burn unit of Dhaka Medical College Hospital after battling critical injuries for four days.
> 
> According to police and local sources, the incident took place on 13 May near the CNG stand in front of Muktijoddha Complex area of Burichang municipality.
> 
> The victim was allegedly set on fire by another member of the same community, identified as Zahirul Islam Apon alias Zahir.
> 
> Locals rescued Mahi in a critically injured condition and first took the victim to Cumilla Medical College Hospital. The victim was later shifted to Dhaka Medical after the condition deteriorated.
> 
> Doctors said nearly 44% of Mahi's body, including the respiratory tract, had been burned.
> 
> Local residents claimed several third-gender individuals had been living in makeshift homes on government land near the Muktijoddha Complex area and that some had become involved in drug trading.
> 
> According to locals, the victim had previously been arrested in drug-related cases and had served jail time.
> 
> They alleged that a dispute over the distribution of drug money recently developed among members of the group, which may have triggered the attack.
> 
> Following the incident, the victim's father Kamrul Haque Farhad filed a murder case with Burichang Police Station.
> 
> Police have so far arrested one accused in connection with the case, while the prime suspect, Zahir, remains absconding.
> 
> Burichang Police Station Officer-in-Charge (OC) Mohammad Lutfor Rahman said police operations are ongoing to arrest the main accused.




> A Hijra person identified as Mahi, also known as  Enamul Haque Shishir (35), died on May 17 after sustaining severe burn injuries in Burichang, Cumilla. Mahi had been receiving treatment at the burn unit of Dhaka Medical College Hospital since the attack and died while in intensive care.
> 
> According to police and local sources, the incident took place on May 13 near the CNG stand area in front of the Muktijoddha Complex in Burichang municipality. Investigators allege that another Hijra person, identified as Apon, also known as Zahirul Islam, poured petrol on Mahi and set her on fire during a dispute. Local residents rescued Mahi after hearing screams and initially took her to Cumilla Medical College Hospital before she was transferred to Dhaka due to the severity of her injuries.
> 
> Following Mahi’s death, her father filed a murder case at Burichang Police Station against several individuals, naming three accused and listing others as unidentified. Police stated that one person has already been arrested and that operations are ongoing to apprehend additional accused individuals.
> 
> Law enforcement officials alleged that tensions had developed between groups of Hijra residents in the area over territorial control and the distribution of money connected to drug-related activities. Local residents stated that several transgender individuals had been living in makeshift housing on government land in the Muktijoddha Complex area for years.
> 
> Police said multiple teams are currently conducting operations to locate the main accused and others connected to the killing.
