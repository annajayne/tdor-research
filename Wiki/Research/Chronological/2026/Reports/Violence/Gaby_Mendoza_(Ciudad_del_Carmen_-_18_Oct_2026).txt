> They seek justice for the death of Gaby Mendoza, a trans woman found dead in a motel in Ciudad del Carmen.
> The discovery was reported in the early hours of Sunday, October 18, at the establishment located on Avenida Camarón Avenue, near calle Corcovado, in Ciudad del Carmen. According to initial reports, cleaning staff found Gaby lying face down inside a room, unresponsive, so they requested assistance from emergency services.
> 
> Paramedics who arrived at the scene confirmed that he no longer had vital signs. Subsequently, police officers cordoned off the establishment, and personnel from the Campeche State Attorney General's Office carried out the corresponding procedures to clarify the circumstances of his death.
> 
> Gaby Mendoza was a 49-year-old trans woman , known among residents of Ciudad del Carmen for her food business at night.
> 
> According to the civil association JUNTRANS CARMEN, she was a hard-working woman and a beloved colleague among members of the trans community. After her death was announced, family, friends, and acquaintances gathered to say goodbye in the Playón neighborhood, where she also ran her business.
> 
> The organization has asked that her gender identity be respected and that the investigations be carried out without prejudice or expressions that could criminalize her.
> 
> According to preliminary information, Gaby arrived at the motel in a red Chevrolet Spark car, with license plates from the state of Campeche, which remained parked in the space corresponding to the room.
> 
> Initial reports indicate that she was allegedly accompanied by another person, who reportedly left the establishment before her body was found. This circumstance is part of the facts that the authorities will have to clarify.
> 
> It was also reported that the cause of death was mechanical asphyxiation . However, it is up to the Prosecutor's Office to officially confirm the results of the forensic examinations and determine the circumstances of the death.
> 
> Have they already identified the alleged perpetrator of Gaby Mendoza's death?
> Amid the investigations, reports have emerged that a person allegedly involved in the death of Gaby Mendoza has already been identified and remains at large .
> 
> It was also reported that the motel's security camera footage had been handed over to the authorities to help clarify the facts.
> 
> However, none of these allegations have been officially confirmed by the Campeche Prosecutor's Office so far , so it is not possible to confirm that there is an arrest warrant or to establish the responsibility of any person.
> 
> The investigations must determine what happened inside the room, whether there was third-party involvement, and the circumstances that led to Gaby's death.
> 
> Family and the trans community demand justice for Gaby Mendoza
> Following Gaby's identification, members of JUNTRANS CARMEN requested that authorities investigate her death with a gender perspective, locate whoever is responsible, and respect her identity as a trans woman.
> 
> The organization also questioned the treatment given to her identity by some authorities and media outlets after her death and asked to avoid any attempt to justify or minimize what happened because of her gender identity or work activity.
> 
> For now, the case remains under investigation, while family members and organizations await official information on the cause of death, the possible identification of a person involved, and progress in clarifying the facts.
