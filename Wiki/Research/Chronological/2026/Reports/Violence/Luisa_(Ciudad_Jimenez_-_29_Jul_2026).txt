> Luisa LM , a 24-year-old trans woman, died on July 29 at the Jiménez Regional Hospital. She had been found unconscious on calle Hidalgo in the same city in the early morning hours with injuries that resulted in her death. According to the State Attorney General's Office, she died from a traumatic brain injury. The LGBT+ community is demanding justice and a thorough investigation into her death, which they consider a suspected case of transfemicide .
> 
> The events occurred during the early hours of Wednesday, when the 911 emergency system received a report that a person was unconscious on the sidewalk of Hidalgo Street , so paramedics from Civil Protection attended to the case.
> 
> Luisa's body was lying on the public road. She had bruises on different parts of her body and, due to the emergency, was taken to the Jiménez Regional Hospital, where she received first aid; however, she no longer had vital signs, so the State Attorney General's Office was notified .
> 
> Minutes later, agents from the State Investigation Agency arrived at the hospital and opened an investigation into the death to clarify the circumstances. The Prosecutor's Office has stated that the cause of death is still under investigation.
> 
> Personnel from the State Investigation Agency responded to a report from the Jiménez Regional Hospital regarding the death of a person from causes yet to be determined, in events that occurred this Wednesday, July 29 .
> 
> Furthermore, it was reported that he suffered a head injury and a fractured left femur, which resulted in her death. Personnel from the Forensic Services Unit transported the body to a local morgue for the legally required autopsy.
> 
> The Public Prosecutor's Office of the Southern District Attorney's Office was also notified , and began the investigation to clarify the circumstances surrounding his death.
> 
> For its part, the LGBT+ collective Orgullo Jiménez stressed that his death is curious and even suspicious, and demanded that the State Attorney General's Office review the case as a possible hate crime under the category of transfeminicide.
> 
> She also asked the authorities and society to respect the victim's identity and refrain from referring to her by the name that appears on her documents, and noted that the State Attorney General's Office still refers to her by her former name.




> Jiménez - Elements of the State Investigation Agency (AEI) went to the Jiménez Regional Hospital after the report of the death of a 24-year-old transsexual young [woman], identified as Luis Alberto LM, in events recorded this Wednesday, July 29.
> 
> According to initial investigations, the young man was located moments earlier by paramedics from Civil Protection while he was unconscious on calle Hidalgo.
> 
> Suffering from a traumatic brain injury and a fracture in his left femur, he was rushed to the hospital, where he sadly passed away while receiving medical attention.
> 
> To date, the State Attorney General's Office (FGE) has not determined the formal cause of death. Therefore, personnel from the Forensic Services Unit transported the body to the local morgue to perform the corresponding legal autopsy.
> 
> The Public Prosecutor's Office of the Southern District Attorney's Office has already opened an investigation file to clarify the mechanics of the events and determine the circumstances in which the young [woman] suffered the injuries.
