> Transgender woman is killed in rural area of ​​a city in Pará state.
> The crime occurred in the region of kilometer 50; police are seeking to clarify the circumstances and identify those responsible.
> Thursday, June 25, 2026, 00:00
> The transgender woman was found dead in a wooded area in Moju | Disclosure
> 
> Another case of violence was recorded in the interior of Pará and will be investigated by public security authorities. The episode occurred on Tuesday night (23), in a rural area of ​​the municipality of Moju, in northeastern Pará, causing commotion among local residents.
> 
> The victim was identified by the first name Bruna, a transgender woman well-known in the kilometer 50 area. According to initial information obtained at the scene, she was shot and did not survive her injuries .
> 
> After being alerted, teams from the Military Police went to the area to respond to the incident and begin the investigation procedures. The officers carried out initial surveys with the aim of gathering information that could contribute to solving the crime.
> 
> To date, the circumstances surrounding the murder remain unknown. No details have been released about possible suspects or the motive for the murder.
