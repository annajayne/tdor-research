====== Isabele (Tibau do Sul - 9 May 2026) ======
Created Thursday 08 October 2026
@violence


Isabele [Athângelo Miguel Arcanjo]
52
9 May 2026
Tibau do Sul
Rio Grande do Norte
Brazil
murdered


> Victim found this Saturday morning (9); police investigating possible involvement of a criminal faction in the crime
> 
> A trans woman was found dead this Saturday morning (9) in a home located in downtown Tibau do Sul, near the town's main church.
> 
> The victim was officially identified as Athângelo Miguel Arcanjo, 52, known socially as Isabele.
> 
> According to information provided by the Rio Grande do Norte Military Police, the woman had reportedly been kidnapped on Friday night (8), possibly by members of a criminal faction.
> 
> She was found dead inside the property on Saturday morning. Military Police teams cordoned off the area pending the arrival of forensic experts and the Rio Grande do Norte Civil Police, who will be responsible for the investigation.
> 
> Preliminary information indicates that a Molotov cocktail was also found at the scene. It is suspected that the perpetrators attempted to set fire to the house but failed to complete the act.
> 
> The motive for the crime remains unknown and is expected to be clarified during the investigation conducted by the Civil Police.
