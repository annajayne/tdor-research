> The murder of Sabrina , a trans woman , which occurred on the night of Monday, April 6, in a bar in Tlayacapan, shocked the LGBTQ+ community in the state, who condemned the events and demanded justice for this crime .
> 
> Activist Alida Martinez remembered Sabrina as a hard-working woman who owned beauty salons, which provided her with a livelihood to get ahead.
> 
> "We mourn the murder of our comrade, our friend, our sister, of many trans comrades; I come to demand justice for the murder of our comrade, that it not go unpunished like so many others that have been forgotten."
> 
> He expressed confidence that the authorities would clarify the facts , stating that he didn't know what could have happened to cause someone to attack Sabrina in that way. "We don't know if it was a hate crime, a crime of passion; the authorities will conduct the investigation," he said.
> 
> "She was a fighter, she was a woman who had her own beauty salons, she wasn't very well known in activism, she was a very quiet woman" - Alida Martínez, LGBTQ+ rights activist
>
> The activist placed a candle on the altar to victims outside the Government Palace in Cuernavaca, as a sign of mourning for the murder of Sabrina and lamented that the situation of discrimination against members of the LGBTQ+ community is widespread throughout the state of Morelos.
> 
> So far in 2026, other transfeminicides of trans women have been recorded, such as the one that occurred in the area of ​​the Atlacomulco canal, next to the Paso Exprés, in Cuernavaca, where the lifeless body of a trans woman was found.
> 
> Sabrina was murdered inside the El Jacalito bar, located on the Yautepec-Tlayacapan highway, near the Las Vivianas neighborhood. The case has been turned over to the State Attorney General's Office (FGE) for investigation.



> Trans woman murdered in bar in Tlayacapan
> SECURITY /Apr 07, 2026 11:34 AM/ BY: LBahena
> DIARIO DE MORELOS EN:
> 
> Tlayacapan, Morelos.- A member of the LGBT+ community was shot and killed Monday night inside a nightclub in the Las Vivianas neighborhood, prompting a large deployment of security forces in the eastern part of the state.
> 
> Police sources reported that around 9:30 p.m., residents of the Yautepec-Tlayacapan local road alerted authorities after hearing several gunshots coming from the bar called "El Jacalito".
> 
> After a report was made to the 911 emergency number, paramedics from the municipality immediately went to the scene. Upon entering the building, they found a person with bleeding wounds caused by gunshot wounds.
> 
> Sadly, rescuers confirmed that the victim, known to locals as Sabrina, had no vital signs. The gunshot wounds to vital areas caused her almost instantaneous death.
> 
> Witnesses to the attack stated that the alleged perpetrators were two men traveling on a motorcycle. After firing the shots, the gunmen sped off toward the municipal seat of Tlayacapan to evade justice.
> 
> The area was secured by Morelos State Police officers, who cordoned off the perimeter to preserve the ballistic evidence found inside the establishment. The trans woman's body lay in front of customers and employees.
> 
> Forensic experts from the Eastern Regional Prosecutor's Office arrived minutes later to collect the shell casings and carry out the corresponding procedures. The body was transferred to the Forensic Medical Service for the legally required autopsy.
> 
> So far, authorities have not determined whether the attack stemmed from an extortion attempt, a fight, or a hate crime. The specialized femicide unit may take over the case according to the state's established protocols.
