> WHO KILLED VITÓRIA? Trans woman found dead on the side of BR-324 highway was known as “Vicky”, from Cajazeiras; find out the details.
> 20/07/2026 • 14:09
> 
> The trans woman found dead on Sunday morning (19), on the banks of the BR-324, in Simões Filho, was identified as Vitória, known by the nickname “Vicky”. She lived in Cajazeiras 6, in Salvador.
> 
> According to information gathered by Alô Juca, Vicky was found with her hands tied, showing signs of beating and several gunshot wounds. The body was located near kilometer 611 of the highway.
> 
> Initial reports indicate that around 1:00 AM, a car stopped at the location, abandoned the body on the side of the road, and then left the area. It is suspected that the victim was murdered elsewhere and dumped on BR-324.
> 
> During Monday's program (20), new information reached the Alô Juca team. According to reports, Vicky had links with people involved in drug trafficking and was "chipping" members of a rival faction. The information will still be investigated by the Civil Police and, so far, there is no official confirmation about the motivation for the crime.
> 
> Teams from the Military Police, the Department of Technical Police (DPT), and investigators from SILC RMS 01 were at the scene conducting initial investigations. The case will be investigated by the Simões Filho Territorial Police Station.
> 
> Also during the program, host Marcelo Castro reported that he had received accounts that Vicky's family was facing difficulties in paying for the funeral and offered the Alô Juca production team's assistance.
> 
> A transgender woman was found dead on Sunday morning (19), on the side of BR-324, near Rei da Pamonha, in Simões Filho, in the Metropolitan Region of Salvador.
> 
> According to initial information, witnesses reported that a car had stopped at the location around 1 am. Shortly after, the victim's body was abandoned on the side of the highway and the vehicle left the area.
> 
> The victim showed signs of gunshot wounds. Military Police teams isolated the area until the arrival of the Technical Police Department (DPT), which carried out the forensic examination and removed the body. The victim's identity has not yet been officially released.
> 
> The case will be investigated by the Simões Filho Territorial Police Station, which seeks to identify those responsible and clarify the motivation for the crime.
