> Transgender woman stabbed to death for resisting Rs 200 robbery bid in Bengaluru
> The Bengaluru police arrested a 28-year-old man for allegedly stabbing transgender woman Devi to death during a robbery attempt near Majestic bus stand.
> Written by: Atiya Firdos
> Updated: Aug 22, 2026 08:51 PM IST
> 
> The Bengaluru police Saturday arrested a man for allegedly stabbing to death a transgender woman during a robbery attempt near the Majestic bus stand in the city.
> 
> The deceased has been identified as Devi, 27.
> 
> The accused, Gautham, 28, a resident of Ramamurthy Nagar, was traced and arrested by police teams following the incident.
> 
> According to Yatish N, Deputy Commissioner of Police, Bengaluru West Division, the incident occurred between 2.30 am and 3 am under the jurisdiction of the Upparpet police station.
> 
> “The accused approached Devi on a two-wheeler and demanded Rs 200. When she refused and resisted, he allegedly attacked her on the head with a dagger,” the DCP said.
> 
> Gautham then allegedly snatched Devi’s purse and mobile phone and stabbed her multiple times before fleeing the spot on his two-wheeler. Devi sustained severe injuries and collapsed at the scene. She died shortly after, the police said.
> 
> During interrogation, Gautham allegedly told the police that he was under the influence of alcohol and had gone to the area with the intention of committing a robbery.
> 
> Based on a complaint filed by an associate of Devi who was present nearby, the Upparpet police station has registered a murder case.
> 
> The police said they are also investigating whether the accused was previously involved in any other robbery cases reported in the city.
