> The murder of a trans woman inside a room at the Cedro hotel in downtown Cancun is being investigated under the femicide protocol , while authorities intensify the search for the main suspect , who left the place hours before the discovery.
> 
> Authorities are searching for Daniela González, a woman who disappeared in downtown Monterrey; she was last seen at a hotel.
> The body was found in room seven after employees reported to 911 that the person was unresponsive and the door was locked . Police and firefighters forced entry and found the victim covered with blood- stained sheets .
> 
> According to initial reports , the woman had a deep wound to her neck that caused her almost immediate death. The motive for the crime has not yet been determined.
> 
> Initial investigations indicated that the room was rented by two people. However, the man who was with her left in the morning , stating that she would be resting, thus avoiding raising suspicion among the staff.
> 
> The victim 's identity was confirmed through documents found among her belongings , although her body remains in protective custody until her family is notified . The body was transferred to the Forensic Medical Service ( Semefo ).
> 
> Elements of the Specialized Prosecutor's Office analyzed video surveillance cameras from the hotel and the area, as well as C5 records in the vicinity of Tulum Avenue , with the aim of locating the alleged perpetrator .
