> A transgender woman, identified as Paloma, 27, was shot and killed on Friday afternoon on Rua Acaeté, in the Jorge Teixeira neighborhood of Manaus.
> 
> According to preliminary information, the victim was at home when two men broke into the property. During the incident, Paloma was shot three times.
> 
> The Military Police were called and went to the address to respond to the incident. At this time, there is no information about the identity or whereabouts of the suspects.
> 
> The motive for the crime has also not yet been clarified.
> 
> The case will be investigated by the Specialized Homicide and Kidnapping Division (DEHS), which will carry out inquiries to identify the perpetrators and clarify the circumstances of the murder.
