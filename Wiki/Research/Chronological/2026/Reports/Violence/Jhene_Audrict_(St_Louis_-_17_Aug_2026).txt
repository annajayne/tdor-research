> ST. LOUIS, Mo. (First Alert 4) - A mother says she is facing what she calls every parent’s worst nightmare after her 21-year-old daughter, Jhené Audrict, was found dead at the corner of Minnesota Avenue and Bowen Street on Aug. 17.
> 
> “Jhené was found with a gunshot to the back of her head,” said Melissa Crawford, Jhené’s mother.
> 
> Crawford says the story of her daughter should not end there.
> 
> “She is a transgender, 21-year-old woman. I am proud to be her mother. She was very adventurous, a traveler, highly educated,” Crawford said.
> 
> Jhené was a college student who spent a year and a half living in Hawaii. Her mother says she last spoke with her on Aug. 15, when Jhené was headed to her boyfriend’s house.
> 
> “I spoke to her. She told me that she was kind of nervous about going to her boyfriend’s house because they were having issues,” Crawford said.
> 
> The next morning, Crawford received a call from the boyfriend saying Jhené was missing. After a day of searching, Jhené was found.
> 
> “A city worker found Jhené in between this abandoned house and also this abandoned church. She was slumped over,” Crawford said.
> 
> Crawford is questioning what happened in the hours before her daughter’s death.
> 
> “Jhené was legally blind. Unfortunately, when they did find Jhené, Jhené’s glasses were not located on her face. Jhené cannot see without her glasses,” Crawford said.
> 
> Crawford says she does not believe her daughter took her own life. Police say the circumstances surrounding Jhené’s death remain under investigation.
> 
> “The resolution for me is to get justice for Jhené. Also, to advocate for the trans community. Awareness for domestic violence. And also advocating even for mental health,” Crawford said.
> 
> “I will not rest. And I will not let this be swept underneath the rug. Jhené Cordelia Audrict will get justice,” Crawford said.
