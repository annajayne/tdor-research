> Armed attack against member of the Lesbian, Gay, Transgender community
> April 19, 2026
> LGBTQ+ community civil organizations have condemned the incident on social media and are demanding that authorities investigate the crime as a possible hate crime.
> 
> An armed attack left Noé Alejandro López Martínez, 27, dead on Saturday night in the municipality of Cintalapa, after subjects shot at him on José María Morelos street, in the Lindavista Poniente neighborhood. 
> 
> Neighbors reported multiple gunshots and upon going outside found the body lying in the street. Authorities who arrived confirmed that the victim had suffered multiple gunshot wounds.
> 
> The victim was well-known in social and artistic circles, was part of the LGBT+ community, and held the title of Queen of Queens in the MUM Mexico Gay pageant, which she was to relinquish in June. She also worked as a professional makeup artist. 
> 
> Forensic experts carried out the procedures and transferred the body to the Forensic Medical Service (SEMEFO), while the Prosecutor's Office has already opened an investigation to clarify this crime that has caused outrage among family and acquaintances.
> 
> LGBTQ+ community civil organizations have condemned the incident on social media and are demanding that authorities investigate the crime as a possible hate crime.
> 
> Alex's life was cowardly taken in Cintalapa, and he cannot remain just another statistic.
> 
> Saturday night was silenced by gunfire in the Lindavista Poniente neighborhood. There, Noé Alejandro López Martínez, just 27 years old, was brutally and unjustly murdered. A young man full of talent, dreams, and light, he is now missed by his family, his friends, and the entire community that admired him.
> 
> Alex wasn't just a victim. He was art, he was effort, he was inspiration. His name shone in competitions, on stages, and everywhere he left his mark. Today, that light was suddenly extinguished.
> 
> Indignation grows, pain weighs heavy, and the question remains: how long will this go on?
> 
> This cannot go unpunished. Today we not only mourn his passing, today we demand justice.
