> Joel Fidelis dos Santos, 63, was arrested in flagrante delicto for the femicide of firefighter Raelly Braz, 30. The victim, a trans woman, was found dead in her own home in Camaragibe, in Greater Recife, last Tuesday (4). 
> 
> The suspect even gave an interview to TV Tribuna on the day Raelly's body was discovered and sent to the Institute of Legal Medicine (IML). When asked by the reporter if he had reasons to kill her, he denied it. 



> A 29-year-old trans woman was found dead inside her home on Segunda Travessa Pernambuco, in the Bairro dos Estados neighborhood of Camaragibe, in the Recife Metropolitan Area. Raely Braz da Silva's body was discovered this Tuesday (4) by a family member, who had to climb over the property's wall after noticing the gate was locked and the victim was not responding.
> 
> Upon the arrival of emergency teams, the Homicide Task Force forced open the main entrance to the house where Raely had lived for about a year. According to the police report, the home was ransacked, with bloodstains on the floor and the PVC ceiling partially melted.
> 
> Forensic experts from the Institute of Criminalistics (IC) identified wounds caused by a bladed weapon on the victim's neck and under her left arm. According to the Pernambuco Civil Police (PCPE), the perpetrator attempted to set the body on fire after the crime.
> 
> The body was sent to the Institute of Forensic Medicine (IML) for examinations to assist the investigation. Police are gathering forensic reports and witness statements to understand the circumstances surrounding the homicide.
> 
> Raely worked as a civilian firefighter and had been in a relationship with 62-year-old Joel Fidelis dos Santos for about five months. In an interview with reporter Marwyn Barbosa from TV Tribuna, her partner stated that he had gone to the victim's house on the day of the crime; receiving no response when he called out to her, he contacted Raely's aunt to report the lack of contact.
> 
> Joel also stated that he provided financial support to his partner, acknowledged that she used drugs, and claimed to be unaware of any death threats against her, denying any involvement in the homicide.
> 
> After providing a statement, Joel was taken to the Camaragibe Police Station and then to the Institute of Criminalistics (IC) for forensic examinations, subsequently returning to the police station. The Pernambuco Civil Police (PCPE) are continuing their investigation to clarify who committed the crime and the motives behind it, gathering new information that could help identify Raely's killer.




> In Camaragibe, in the Recife Metropolitan Region, a transgender woman identified as Raely Braz da Silva, 29, was stabbed to death inside her home in the Bairro dos Estados neighborhood. Her body was partially burned. Raely worked as a civilian firefighter and was pursuing specializations in the field. The suspect in the crime is Joel Fidelis dos Santos, 62.
