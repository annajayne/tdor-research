> Transgender woman found dead inside her home in Praia da Pipa; police are investigating the case.
> February 27, 2026
> 
> A transgender woman known as Naomi was found dead inside her own home last week in Praia da Pipa, in the municipality of Tibau do Sul, on the southern coast of Rio Grande do Norte. According to police information, she was the victim of stab wounds. The Civil Police of Rio Grande do Norte confirmed that it has opened an investigation to determine the circumstances of the death. In a statement sent to the SAIBA MAIS Agency, the corporation reported that teams were called, carried out the initial investigations at the scene, and began inquiries. “To date, there is no official information about the perpetrator or the motive for the crime. The investigations are ongoing, with the collection of technical evidence and witness statements, with the aim of identifying the person(s) responsible and clarifying the facts,” informed the Social Communication Secretariat of the Civil Police (SECOMS).
> 
> The case caused strong outrage among residents of the region and resonated on social media. Associação Potiguar de Travestis e Transexuais na Ação pela Coerência no Rio Grande do Norte (Attransparência/RN) published a public statement demanding justice. “Justice for the murder of Naomi! Another trans person killed in Rio Grande do Norte. We cannot let this crime go unpunished; the population of Pipa beach in Tibau do Sul cries out for justice,” the organization declared.
> 
> To date, there is no confirmation of suspects nor information about the motive for the crime. The Civil Police reiterated that anonymous tips can be made through the Crime Reporting Hotline 181 and can contribute to the progress of the investigations.
> 
> The reporting team sought out family and friends of Naomi to give statements, but was unable to locate them before the publication of this article. The space remains open for expressions of sympathy from loved ones who wish to pay tribute or contribute information about the victim. The case remains under investigation.
