> Transgender found dead with neck and head injuries
> 
> Bathinda: A 37-year-old transgender has been found dead with injuries to the neck and head inflicted with a sharp-edged weapon.
> 
> Identified as Sarabjit Kaur, the transgender was living alone at Kotbhai village in Gidderbaha sub-division of Muktsar district. 
> 
> Her body was recovered from a cot at her small house on Thursday morning. The reason behind the murder could not be known.
> 
> Gidderbaha DSP Arun Mundan, speaking to TOI, said that the police received information of a body having been recovered from a house at Kotbhai village.
> 
> "When we reached the place, we found the transgender, Sarabjit Kaur, lying on a cot as we detected two injuries to her neck and head that seemed to have been inflicted with a sharp-edged weapon. A case under murder charges has been registered against unidentified persons and further investigations are being made."
> 
> He said that as of now, no exact reason behind the killing has come to the fore.
