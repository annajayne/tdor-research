> Prosecutor's office investigates the murder of Constanza Sánchez, a trans activist found dead in Bogotá
> The body was found with signs of violence in the Santa Fe neighborhood. No arrests have been confirmed.
> According to authorities, the body was found with apparent signs of violence. Authorities are investigating a possible murder. Human rights organizations warned of a serious increase in attacks and murders of transgender people in Colombia at the start of 2026 and stated that Constanza's murder is not an isolated incident.
> 
> 
> The discovery of the body of Constanza Sánchez , recognized for decades as a key figure in the defense of the rights of trans people in downtown Bogotá , shook the Santa Fe neighborhood in the Los Mártires district this Tuesday .
> 
> The community leader, known as “Mother Constanza,” had lived there for years and from that space built a support network that made her a role model for generations of trans women. Authorities confirmed that the body showed signs of violence; however, there is no official version of events at this time, and the incident is still under judicial investigation.
> 
> Prosecutor's office rejects plea agreement and formalizes charges in Jaime Esteban Moreno case
> The news was confirmed by human rights groups and organizations. Among them, Caribe Afirmativo, one of the most visible, called for the case not to be treated as an isolated incident, given the context of repeated episodes of violence against the trans community in the city and the country. The Ombudsman's Office joined these calls , requesting that the Attorney General's Office launch an urgent investigation with a gender and identity-sensitive approach. Ombudsman Iris Marín warned that these events are part of a “systematic and culturally sustained” pattern of violence.
> 
> Constanza was not just an activist: she was a refuge and guide for several generations. For years, she accompanied young trans people displaced or expelled from their homes, providing them with legal guidance, daily support, and a place where they could exist and develop their personalities without fear. Her death now adds to the ten murders of trans people recorded in Colombia so far in 2026 , according to official figures from the Ministry of the Interior , which report an increase in serious incidents of violence motivated by prejudice and discrimination based on gender identity.
> 
> Former Bogotá mayor Claudia López joined the chorus of condolences , lamenting Constanza's death and highlighting her role as a historical figurehead for the trans community. She recalled joint initiatives that, she said, honor Constanza's memory and legacy.
> 
> For her part, the Ombudsman stressed the urgent need for thorough investigations with a differentiated approach. Councilwoman Heidy Sánchez stated that the crime challenges the city and demanded that hatred and violence against the trans community in the nation's capital not be normalized.
> 
> These voices were joined by Representative María Fernanda Carrascal , who demanded urgent action to protect the trans population, clarify the crime and guarantee justice, and asked Mayor Carlos Fernando Galán for concrete protection measures.
> 
> The Ministry of the Interior, under the national government, expressed its solidarity with Constanza's family and condemned the acts of violence in the Santa Fe neighborhood. In a statement, the Ministry categorically rejected any act that threatens the life, dignity, and integrity of transgender people and the LGBTIQ+ community , and reiterated its commitment to guaranteeing their rights and building environments free from discrimination.
