Sona was stabbed by two unidentified assailants in Bhubaneswar, Odisha (India) on 1 Feb 2026.

Her killing has sparked protests.



> Brutal Murder of Transgender Sparks Airport Road Blockade in Bhubaneswar
> Itishree Sethy by Itishree Sethy  February 2, 2026 in Trending Now, Twin City Reading Time: 1 min read
>
> A brutal murder of a transgender person near Bhubaneswar has triggered outrage and protests.
> 
> On Sunday night, a transgender person identified as Anjali alias Sona was stabbed to death near Sifa Gate along National Highway-316 under Pipili police limits. She was rushed to Capital Hospital by fellow community members, but doctors declared her dead. Police have begun an investigation, but the motive remains unclear.
> 
> Angered by the slow progress, transgender activists and community members blocked the Airport Road on Monday, demanding the immediate arrest of the killer and stronger protection for their community. The blockade caused severe traffic disruption.
> 
> Activist Meera Parida condemned the police investigation as lax and warned of intensified protests if the culprit is not arrested quickly. She even cautioned that the community might resort to extreme measures if justice is delayed. Social organisations have also condemned the killing and demanded strict action.
> 
> Pipili police are examining CCTV footage and suspect either old enmity or disputes within the community. They assured that all angles are under investigation and the accused will be caught soon.





> Bhubaneswar, [Feb] 2: A transgender person was allegedly murdered near CIFA on the Puri–Bhubaneswar road on Sunday. The deceased has been identified as Anjali, a native of Salepur, sources said.
> 
> According to preliminary reports, two unidentified miscreants arrived on a motorcycle and attacked Anjali with a knife, leaving her critically injured. Believing her to be dead, the attackers reportedly dumped her on a sand mound near the roadside and fled.
> 
> Later, Anjali reportedly regained consciousness and informed her associates over the phone. She was immediately rushed by ambulance to Capital Hospital, where doctors declared her brought dead.
> 
> Police from Pipili and Capital police stations reached the crime scene and have initiated an investigation. Efforts are underway to identify and trace the accused, while the motive behind the murder is yet to be ascertained.
