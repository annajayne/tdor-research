> Two men were charged on Monday, February 9, with the murder of a 45-year-old transgender woman, beaten to death in a suburb of Nouméa, New Caledonia, the prosecutor's office said in a statement. The suspects, aged 19 and 24, were placed in pretrial detention.
> 
> Two men known to police for theft admitted to violently assaulting a woman in her forties named Joëlla in the Saint-Quentin district of Nouméa. A sparsely populated area including abandoned buildings.
> 
> Extreme violence admitted by the accused.
> According to information released by the prosecutor's office, the two men admitted to kicking the victim in the head about fifteen times and undressing her. The attack reportedly ended when a passerby arrived.
> 
> According to La 1ere, when emergency services arrived, Joëlla had a "massive dent" in her face and could not be revived, stated the Nouméa public prosecutor, Yves Dupas.
> 
> The suspects reportedly claimed to have acted following alleged offers of "sexual favors" from the victim, statements that have not been confirmed at this stage of the investigation, according to La 1ere.
> 
> Outrage in New Caledonia
> The violent death of Joëlla, known locally for playing volleyball, a very popular sport in New Caledonia, has sparked outrage across the archipelago.
> 
> In a press release, the association La Tresse Nouvelle-Calédonie denounced "a major attack on human life," citing systemic violence against transgender people. The association Rainbowlution Nouvelle-Calédonie called for strengthened prevention, awareness, and protection measures for LGBTQIA+ people, as well as for combating hate speech and hate crimes.
