> a trans woman was stabbed to death and the victim's brother arrested the suspect
> 
> A serious act of violence shook Quilmes Oeste after the discovery of a trans person dead in a house on calle Lamadrid at 3800, in an episode that is being investigated as a homicide by the Justice system.
> 
> According to the police report, officers from the Tactical Operations Group (GTO) of the Quilmes 5th Police Station were dispatched to the intersection of calle Mosconi between Liniers and Lamadrid, where a confrontation between neighbors was reported. Upon arrival, the officers found a man restraining another man, 27 years old. During the altercation, the man in custody shouted, "This guy killed my sister."
> 
> Upon receiving this accusation, the police officers immediately arrested the suspect and took him to the station. During the search, they found he was carrying a backpack containing a blister pack of pills, a lighter, a pendant, a cell phone, a Bluetooth speaker box, and an extension cord.
> 
> Simultaneously, officers from the Quilmes Patrol Command were dispatched to a residence located at 3800 Lamadrid Street, where the most tragic discovery was made. In a small house at the back of the property, they found a transgender person lying on the floor with a stab wound to their neck. The SAME (Emergency Medical Assistance System) was urgently requested, and upon arrival, medical personnel confirmed that the victim was already deceased.
> 
> The person was identified. The room was found in disarray, although the bladed weapon believed to have been used in the fatal attack was not recovered.
> 
> After learning of the incident, the Functional Instruction Unit No. 4 of the Quilmes Judicial Department, headed by Karina Gallo, intervened and ordered the presence of the Scientific Police to carry out the corresponding forensic examinations. Once the procedures at the scene were completed, and no procedural irregularities were detected, the magistrate ordered the formal arrest of the suspect for the crime of homicide, also issuing a series of judicial directives and ordering the transfer of the detainee to the courthouse.
> 
> Meanwhile, police personnel continue with investigative tasks to clarify the details of the event, reconstruct the mechanics of what happened and determine the participation of the accused, while the case advances in the judicial sphere under the label of "homicide."
