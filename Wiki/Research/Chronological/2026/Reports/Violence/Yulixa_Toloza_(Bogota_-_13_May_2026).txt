> MEXICO CITY (apro) — The disappearance and death of 52-year-old Yulixa Consuelo Toloza is being investigated by Colombian authorities as a possible homicide related to a cosmetic procedure performed at an unlicensed establishment in southern Bogotá. The victim was reported missing on May 13, 2026 , and her body was found on May 19 on a road in the municipality of Apulo, in the department of Cundinamarca, according to reports from the Attorney General's Office cited by local media.
> 
> On May 13, Yulixa Toloza went to an establishment identified as “Beauty Láser”, located in the Venecia neighborhood, in the town of Tunjuelito, where she was allegedly going to undergo a lipolysis or fat reduction procedure with sedation.
> 
> According to testimonies published in Colombian media, the procedure lasted several hours, and afterward, the woman showed signs of weakness, disorientation, and difficulty standing. Videos recorded by bystanders show the victim in a delicate state as she left the scene.
> 
> Family members indicated that a friend was with her and left the premises to look for personal belongings; upon returning, the establishment was closed and there was no information about Yulixa's whereabouts.
> 
> Cameras captured his departure in an unconscious state
> The investigation included reviewing security camera footage from the area. The recordings show that on the night of May 13, two men took Yulixa from the building, apparently unconscious, and put her in a vehicle.
> 
> This material was key to establishing the line of investigation into a possible irregular transfer of the victim after the cosmetic procedure.
> 
> Local authorities also confirmed that, during an operation at the site, another woman was found recovering, which reinforced suspicions about the operation of medical procedures without sanitary control.
> 
> Body found on Apulo road
> The body of Yulixa Toloza was found on May 19, 2026, beside a road between Apulo and Anapoima, in Cundinamarca. The Prosecutor's Office reported that the body had characteristics consistent with the victim, which was later confirmed through forensic procedures.
> 
> The mayor of Bogotá, Carlos Fernando Galán, publicly announced that the case is being investigated as a homicide and called for the facts to be clarified, as well as sanctions for those responsible.
> 
> Investigation points to clandestine clinic
> Health authorities in Bogotá indicated that the establishment where she was treated operated as a beauty salon and hairdressing center , but did not have permits to perform invasive medical procedures nor the conditions required for patient care.
> 
> The investigation led to the identification of several individuals allegedly involved in the procedure and the victim's transfer. Colombian media reported that some have been arrested, while others are being sought by authorities, including with the support of international mechanisms.
> 
> Risks of unregulated cosmetic procedures
> The case has reignited concerns in Colombia about the proliferation of clandestine clinics offering low-cost procedures without certified medical supervision. Authorities have reiterated that these types of services pose a risk to the health and lives of patients.
> 
> The Prosecutor's Office continues with the proceedings to determine criminal responsibilities, establish the exact cause of death and clarify the route that the victim followed from when he left the establishment until his discovery in Cundinamarca.



> Yulixa Toloza's body found after undergoing cosmetic procedure
> The mayor of Bogotá, Carlos Fernando Galán, confirmed the murder of Yulixa Toloza, who disappeared after undergoing a cosmetic procedure at an illegal establishment, the mayor said.
> Updated: May 19, 2026 at 6:38 PM
> 
> The mayor of Bogotá, Carlos Fernando Galán, confirmed on Tuesday the murder of Yulixa Toloza, the woman who disappeared after undergoing a cosmetic procedure at an illegal establishment in the Colombian capital, a case that has caused shock and captured the attention of the country.
> 
> "I deeply regret the murder of Yulixa Tolosa (...) We have to call this what it is, Yulixa was murdered. It wasn't medical malpractice, it was murder," Galán stated in a video posted on his X account.
> 
> The president explained that after the woman's disappearance on May 13, police investigators obtained information indicating that Yulixa Tolosa had been taken out of Bogotá that same night, so the institution activated a search outside the city.
> 
> "My instruction was clear, the number one priority was to find Yulixa," added Galán, who also assured that he contacted Nubia Toloza, the victim's mother, to express solidarity and explain the actions taken by the authorities.
> 
> For its part, the Colombian Prosecutor's Office reported that, as a result of an investigation coordinated with the Metropolitan Police of Bogotá, a body was found in the municipality of Apulo, in the department of Cundinamarca (central Colombia), "which would have similarities and correspond to that of the woman who was reported missing."
> 
> The investigating body indicated that judicial police units are carrying out the technical inspection of the body before its transfer to the National Institute of Legal Medicine and Forensic Sciences, while investigations continue to clarify the crime and locate those responsible.
> 
> The case of Yulixa Tolosa has generated outrage in Colombia since the news of her disappearance became known after undergoing laser lipolysis at an aesthetic establishment, located in the south of Bogotá, which allegedly operated without health permits or official authorization.
> 
> Family members and friends reported that the woman experienced health complications after the procedure, including respiratory difficulty and disorientation, a situation that was evident in several videos that circulated on social media, where the woman is seen in apparently poor physical condition inside the place where she was operated on.
> 
> In addition, a witness claimed to have seen two men take Toloza unconscious out of the establishment and put her in a vehicle, which was later found by the authorities in the city of Cúcuta, Norte de Santander department, bordering Venezuela.
> 
> Authorities are also investigating those responsible for the Beauty Laser Aesthetic Medicine center, which is accused of operating without the necessary permits to perform highly complex aesthetic procedures.
> 
> In this case, two Venezuelan citizens were arrested and accused of concealing and destroying evidence. However, a judge ordered their release, ruling that the arrests did not meet legal requirements, although the investigation into enforced disappearance and other possible crimes remains open.
