> Transgender woman found dead with signs of violence after going to buy shampoo in the interior of São Paulo state.
> The body of Suellen Jalala Ferreira de Brito, 30 years old, was found on Wednesday (18), in Araras (SP), in an abandoned building with a perforation in the neck and an injury to the face.
> By Fabio Rodrigues , g1 São Carlos and Araraquara
> 
> 19/03/2026 14h42 Updated2 days ago
> 
> A transgender woman was found dead with signs of violence , in Araras (SP), on Wednesday (18). The victim was identified as Suellen Jalala Ferreira de Brito, 30 years old . According to her mother, her daughter left home on Tuesday afternoon (17) to buy shampoo and did not return.
> 
> Suellen's body was found the following morning in one of the rooms of an abandoned building in Parque Tiradentes, the neighborhood where she lived.
> 
> According to the Military Police, the building is frequently used by drug users. The Mobile Emergency Care Service (SAMU) found that the body showed signs of rigor mortis, as well as a puncture wound in the neck area and an injury to the face .
> 
> The Civil Police were also at the scene and requested examinations from the Institute of Criminalistics (IC) and the Institute of Forensic Medicine (IML).
> 
> According to the Public Security Secretariat (SSP), the case was registered as a suspicious death at the Araras Police Station. Investigations are underway to clarify all the circumstances of the case.
> 
> Suellen's body was buried this Thursday morning (19) at the Parque dos Eucaliptos Cemetery, in Araras.
> 
> "Until now we don't know what happened, nor who committed this cruelty against her. I don't know how a human being has the capacity to do such a barbaric thing because she was a very good person and had no malice towards anyone," said the mother, Solange Maria de Lima Brito, 50, in an interview with g1 .
> 
> Solange said that her daughter left around 4 pm on Tuesday, saying she was going to the market near their house to buy hair cream and that she would be right back.
> 
> Since she didn't return, her mother thought Suellen might be at a friend's house and would get in touch. But that didn't happen. The following morning, she learned from a niece that the body had been found in the abandoned building near the market.
> 
> According to her mother, her daughter was a homebody, single, and had no enemies . "She was a wonderful person. On Tuesday, before going to the market, she had taken my 90-year-old father to the hospital for an IV drip because my 76-year-old mother wasn't feeling well. She took very good care of my parents, me, and my sisters," said Solange.
> 
> According to her mother, Suellen had venous thrombosis in her legs (when clots form in the veins and impair circulation), which limited her mobility. After three months of hospitalization, she lost one of her lungs, further aggravating her condition.
> 
> "One of her dreams was to improve the condition of her legs, which were very swollen. She wore compression stockings and suffered a lot because of this problem." Her other dream was to regularize her documentation with her chosen name, something she had recently achieved, her mother said.
> 
> According to Solange, her daughter was single and didn't have a boyfriend. When she went out, it was to go to the movies, something she loved, or to participate in family outings. The mother said that her daughter enjoyed studying and spoke English well.
> 
> According to the mother, her daughter neither drank nor smoked, but about a year ago she started using drugs, such as cocaine, and underwent treatment with family support. "For the past year, nothing else has happened. She was clean and, as far as I know, she wasn't using it," she said.
