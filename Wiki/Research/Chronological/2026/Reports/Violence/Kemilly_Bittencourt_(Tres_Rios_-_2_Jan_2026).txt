> Trans woman brutally murdered in Rio de Janeiro; partner arrested.
> A trans woman was murdered on Friday (2) in Três Rios (RJ). According to the Civil Police, the suspect is her partner, who was arrested.
>
> The crime occurred in the early hours of Friday, in the Santa Terezinha neighborhood. According to the Civil Police, the man attacked her with a "mata-leão" (chokehold) during an argument and then stabbed her in the neck. The victim did not survive and died.
>
> The Civil Police also reported that he left the body at the scene for about 2 hours. Afterwards, the man picked up the body and took it to the Habitat neighborhood. Initially, he tried to throw the car and the body into a river. The vehicle ended up with its rear end sticking out of the water.
>
> “After failing to throw the car into the river, he removed the corpse from the trunk, used an axe to try to dismember the victim to facilitate the transport of the body. He also failed. Thus, he carried the victim for a few meters into a thicket and covered the corpse with vegetation,” said Gustavo Menini, deputy delegate of Três Rios.
>
> The man was found and arrested during the day on Friday. The victim's body was also located in a wooded area. A forensics team was at the scene to gather information that could help in the investigations.
>
> Afterwards, the body was taken to the Forensic Medical Institute (IML) of Petrópolis. The axe and knife used in the crime were seized.
>
> The case was registered at the Três Rios police station, which is responsible for the case. The suspect is at the disposal of the Justice system and will undergo a custody hearing in the coming days.


> #TRÊSRIOS RJ - TRANS WOMAN IS KILLED BY HER PARTNER WHO SURRENDERS TO THE POLICE AND CONFESSES TO THE CRIME.
> A homicide has been reported and is being investigated by authorities. According to preliminary information, a transgender woman, known as Kemilly Bittencourt, was killed after an attack by her partner.
> 
> According to what has been ascertained so far, after the crime, the perpetrator allegedly abandoned the body in the Habitat neighborhood, in an area near the river. Hours later, the man voluntarily presented himself to the police, where he confessed to the crime and indicated the location where the body had been left.
> 
> The Civil Police conducted investigations, confirmed the incident, and initiated legal procedures. The suspect remains at the disposal of the Justice system, and the case continues under investigation to fully clarify the facts and circumstances that led to the crime.
> Source: Rádio Nossa Fm Tres Rios
