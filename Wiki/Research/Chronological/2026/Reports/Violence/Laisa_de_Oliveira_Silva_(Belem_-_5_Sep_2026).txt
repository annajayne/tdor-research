> Two people were shot dead in the early hours of this Saturday (5), on passagem Marcílio Dias in the Jurunas neighborhood of Belém .
> 
> According to information from the Civil Police, the victims were a transgender woman and a man. The suspects, who were hooded, arrived at the location in a vehicle and broke into the residence where the two lived.
> 
> According to the police, the criminals entered the house and shot the victims. After the crime, they fled the scene.
> 
> The case is being investigated by the Jurunas Police Station, which is working to identify the perpetrators and clarify the motive for the double homicide. Information that may help in the investigations can be passed on anonymously through the Crime Reporting Hotline, at number 181 or through the Iara channel, via WhatsApp, at number (91) 3210-0181.
