> The Attorney General's Office is investigating the murder of a person in the Jiquilpan ejido as a transfemicide.
> Vega Mojarro's body was found with signs of violence in the Jiquilpan ejido; LGBT+ groups are asking that the case be investigated with a gender perspective and without revictimizing the victim.
> The murder of a person who apparently belonged to the transgender community, whose body was found with visible signs of violence on a dirt road in the Jiquilpan ejido, generated demands from the LGBT+ community. | Photo: Courtesy
> July 20, 2026
> 
> The State Attorney General's Office (FGE) is investigating the murder of Vega Mojarro, a trans woman whose body was found over the weekend with visible signs of violence on a dirt road in the Jiquilpan ejido, in the Mexicali Valley, as a transfeminicide .
> 
> The president of the Arcoíris Collective, Javier Russell, considered it important that the investigation file be integrated under the transfeminicide protocol , noting that the case should not be treated only as a homicide, but under a line of investigation that recognizes the identity of the victim and avoids her revictimization.
> 
> When a trans person is murdered, it must be understood in context; they didn't just die, they were murdered. We demand that the entire investigation be conducted with a sexual orientation and gender perspective so that their identity is not erased,” declared the representative of the LGBT+ community.
> 
> Russell explained that the group's stance arose after the initial report from the Municipal Police reported the discovery of a deceased woman, and subsequently, the Forensic Medical Service registered the victim as a man when he was still unidentified.
> 
> The president of the Arcoíris Collective stated that, based on the characteristics observed by members of the community , they considered that it was a trans woman, and therefore requested that this possibility be taken into account throughout the investigation.
> 
> The Arcoíris Collective also called on the State Government to strengthen protection policies for people of diverse sexual orientations, warning that trans women continue to be one of the populations most exposed to violence.
> 
> Verónica Ustoa, representative of trans women in Mexicali, stated that there are still obstacles to accessing institutional support and that an effective protection network is needed.
> 
> “There is still a long way to go before we can feel safe and receive support without so many barriers,” said the representative of trans women in Mexicali.
> 
> Trans community demands full access to “Violeta” programs
> The leader of the Rainbow Collective recalled that the reform to the Civil Code regarding gender identity legally recognizes trans women, so he considered that all care programs aimed at women, including the Violet Care schemes and units, must provide them with protection on equal terms.
> 
> In response, Verónica Ustoa pointed out that, although some members of the community have received support, there are still restrictions and a lack of awareness regarding the application of these programs for trans women.
> 
> Russell pointed out that violence against trans people cannot be separated from discrimination and hate speech.
> 
> Hate speech kills people. We need more empathy and for institutions to embrace vulnerable groups to prevent these acts from continuing,” concluded the president of the Rainbow Collective.



> Body found partially buried in Mexicali: Prosecutor's office investigates if it is a trans woman
> Jul 17, 2026 - 4:59pm
> Body found half-buried - 7-17-2026
>
> Tijuana – The body of a person showing signs of violence was found in the Mexicali Valley. The State Attorney General's Office (FGE) is investigating whether the deceased was a transgender woman.
> 
> It is a male body, with evident blows to the head, bruises around the eyes, wearing clothing traditionally used by women, and was found partially buried.
> 
> The discovery was reported to the emergency line yesterday afternoon, Thursday, June 16, on a rural road in the agricultural area of ​​Ejido Jiquilpan, between Rancho Pericos and state highway number 3, also known as Los Pioneros.
> 
> Agents from the Mexicali Municipal Police arrived at the scene and confirmed the presence of the body, which showed no vital signs and had visible signs of violence.
> 
> Given this, the municipal agents requested the intervention of the State Investigation Agency (AEI).
> 
> After processing the scene, the Prosecutor's Office preliminarily reported that it was a male person, around 50 years old, who had several head injuries and bruises on his eyelids.
> 
> Because she was wearing women's clothing, he said he would investigate the incident based on his protocols and with respect for the possible gender identity of the victim, although he clarified that this will be confirmed when the corresponding information is gathered, in addition to clarifying the circumstances of the crime.
> 
> The body was taken to the Forensic Medical Service (Semefo) facilities, where an autopsy was performed. It was later revealed that the cause of death was a traumatic brain injury.
> 
> The body will be identified there, and it can also be legally claimed.





> The body of a trans woman with signs of violence was abandoned in the Mexicali Valley, BC
> Chacho Gómez
> July 18, 2026
>  
>  
> Mexicali, BC, July 18, 2026.  With signs of violence, the  body of a trans woman  was found on a road in the Mexicali Valley, security sources reported.
> 
> The discovery was made during the afternoon of Thursday, July 16, around 5 p.m., in the Jiquilpan ejido, between the state highway and Rancho Pericos.
> 
> Municipal agents confirmed that the victim showed no vital signs and had visible signs of violence, so the intervention of the State Investigation Agency (AEI) was requested.
> 
> The 45-year-old victim sustained head injuries and bruising on both eyelids. Forensic Services processed the scene and ordered the body transferred to the Medical Examiner's Office for autopsy and identification.
> 
> The State Attorney General's Office is continuing its investigations to establish the victim's identity and clarify the circumstances of the crime.
