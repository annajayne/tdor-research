> A 43-year-old military police officer is suspected of killing a trans woman and burying the body in southern Minas Gerais . The body of Maryna Colucci de Jesus, 34, was found this Tuesday (25) in a rural area between Jacuí and Fortaleza de Minas.
> 
> The victim had been missing since Friday night (21), when she left home to wait for a car to arrive. She worked as a child welfare counselor in Jacuí.
> 
> After the Civil Police (PCMG) found the remains, the sergeant from the Military Police (PMMG) identified as the suspect in the crime was taken to a police station. The investigation is ongoing.
> 
> In a statement published on social media, the Municipality of Jacuí lamented the death of the child welfare counselor and expressed solidarity with her family, friends, and colleagues.
> 
> "Maryna dedicated part of her life to protecting and defending the rights of children and adolescents, carrying out a role of great social and human responsibility. Her premature and painful departure leaves a deep sadness in all those who had the opportunity to know her and be around her," he stated.
> 
> Itatiaia has contacted the Military Police, in case the corporation wishes to comment, and is awaiting a response. The space remains open for their comment .




> #BandNews | The body of Maryna Colucci de Jesus, 34, a trans woman murdered by a sergeant of the Military Police, with 20 years of service, was found this Tuesday (25), in the rural area of Jacuí. She had been missing since Friday afternoon (21). The sergeant had killed the victim and buried the body in a difficult-to-access area.
> 
> According to information from the Civil Police, the investigations were carried out with the support of the Military Police, which assisted the Civil Police teams since the disappearance was reported. The body was sent to the Medical-Legal Post (PML) of São Sebastião do Paraíso, to carry out examinations and determine the cause of death.
> 
> According to initial information from the investigation, the policeman had a relationship with the victim and allegedly buried the body after the crime. The case was investigated by the Military Police's own intelligence service, which identified behaviors considered suspicious and followed the military man's steps until his arrest. The sergeant was linked to religious groups and, according to reports, was considered a person "above suspicion" by those close to him.
> 
> The sergeant is in custody and remains at the disposal of the Justice system. Investigations are ongoing to fully clarify the facts and circumstances of the incident.
