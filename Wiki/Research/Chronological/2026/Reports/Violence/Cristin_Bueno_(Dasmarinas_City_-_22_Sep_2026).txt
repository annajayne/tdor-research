> Cristin was shot by a man following an argument and a dispute during a drinking session.


> Cristin Bueno, a 21-year-old transpinay from Dasmariñas City, Cavite, was a beloved member of her family and community. On September 22, 2026, Cristin was shot by a man in Dasmariñas City, Cavite, resulting in her death. She has since been laid to rest, while the suspect remains at large and is being sought by authorities.
> 
> Her death has since become part of the growing documentation of violence against transgender women in the Philippines. The Society of Trans Women of the Philippines (STRAP) has identified Cristin as the sixth transfemicide case it has documented in 2026, following Bella, Betty, Jang, Teddy, and Marijune.
> Cristin’s death comes amid growing concern over violence against transgender women in the Philippines. Details surrounding the shooting and the perpetrator’s motive remain limited, as the case is still under investigation.
> 
> STRAP’s documentation highlights the need for stronger preventive measures, legal protections, investigation procedures, and institutional responses to violence against transgender people.
> 
> These cases have also fueled calls for government action, including House Resolution No. 1369, which addresses violence against transgender people and the need for stronger protections.
> 
> We call for justice and accountability for Cristin Bueno. We urge authorities to conduct a thorough investigation and hold those responsible accountable.
> We encourage everyone to share only verified information, support transgender rights and safety, and help build communities free from violence and discrimination.
> 
> As we mourn Cristin’s passing, may we remember her life with dignity and respect. FEU SAGA stands with her family, friends, and community in calling for justice and reaffirming that every transgender life deserves safety, dignity, and respect.
> 
> #JusticeForCristinBueno
