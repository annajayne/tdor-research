> Violent death: the autopsy confirmed that “Cai” was killed by a blow to the head
> The autopsy on the body of Claudia Gabriela Gómez, known as “Cai,” preliminarily determined that the young trans woman died from a “severe blow to the head that caused a skull fracture,” LaVozDeCataratas was told. The forensic report indicated that the cause of death was severe traumatic brain injury with a fracture “caused by a hard object.”
> Thursday, April 9, 2026
> 
> Iguazu (LaVozDeCataratas) Cai had been found days ago seriously injured and unconscious in a eucalyptus grove in the municipal reserve , and remained hospitalized in critical condition at the SAMIC Hospital until her death was finally confirmed.
> 
> Following his death, the investigating court ordered an autopsy to be performed, with the aim of accurately establishing the causes of death and determining the circumstances in which the injury occurred.
> 
> The preliminary result now confirms that the death occurred as a direct result of the blow to the head , although the investigation continues to clarify whether it was an assault or some other type of incident.
> 
> It is worth remembering that the case generated shock in the community, not only because of the violence of the episode, but also because of the context of vulnerability in which the victim lived.
> 
> So far no one has been arrested , while the Justice system and the Police are pursuing different lines of investigation to determine responsibility.




> “Caí”, the trans woman found seriously injured in Iguazú, has died
> She had been hospitalized in critical condition since April 2nd. The court ordered an autopsy to further the investigation.
> April 8, 2026
> 
> The investigation into the brutal attack suffered by "Caí ," the trans woman found with serious injuries in Puerto Iguazú, took a tragic turn in recent hours. After spending several days hospitalized in critical condition at the SAMIC Hospital , her death was confirmed, and the court ordered an autopsy to try to determine precisely how the injury that caused her death occurred.
> 
> The news was published this Wednesday by La Voz de Cataratas , a media outlet that reported the death of the victim, who had been found unconscious on April 2 in a eucalyptus grove in the municipal reserve, on Avenida Libertad.
> 
> According to information confirmed by PRIMRA EDICIÓN within the framework of the case, the measure was ordered by Judge Martín Brites of the Puerto Iguazú Court of Instruction No. 3 , who has been involved in the case since its inception. The autopsy will be key to determining whether the injuries resulted from a direct attack, a violent fall, or some other circumstance that has not yet been clarified.
> 
> The incident came to light on the morning of April 2nd, when police officers were alerted to the presence of an injured person in the internal corridors of the eucalyptus grove. Upon arriving at the scene, the officers confirmed that it was “Caí,” who was unconscious on the ground and had a visible injury to his face.
> 
> According to the police report filed at the time, the victim was found in an area where she was allegedly living on the streets. Due to the severity of her condition, she was rushed to the local SAMIC Hospital, where she was admitted in critical condition.
> 
> The preliminary report from the police doctor indicated that the injuries were significant and could have been caused by a blunt object, such as a rock, or by a violent fall onto the asphalt. This lack of certainty about the exact origin of the injuries is precisely one of the main focuses of the investigation.
> 
> From the outset, the Scientific Police, the Investigations and Cybercrime Division of Regional Unit V worked at the scene to gather evidence, traces, and potential witness statements. They also made progress in analyzing security camera footage and inquiring to reconstruct the victim's movements before she was found seriously injured.



> A trans woman is fighting for her life after being found with a severe head injury.
> Known as Caí, she was found unconscious in the area known as the Eucaliptal by two women who requested medical assistance.
> Monday, April 6, 2026 | 8:00 PM
> 
> By Puerto Iguazu Correspondent
> 
> Last Thursday morning, two women, who are believed to be from the Mbya Fortín Mbororé community, reportedly found a trans woman known in Iguazú as Caí unconscious and with a severe head injury in the grounds of the El Eucaliptal Municipal Park. According to locals, she used to spend the night there as she is homeless.
> 
> After the discovery, the 107 emergency medical service arrived at the scene and transported the seriously injured woman to the hospital, where she remains hospitalized in intensive care in critical condition. Following the incident, the police began an investigation, first to establish the identity of the transgender woman. They were able to determine that she is Claudia Gabriela Gómez, originally from Paraguay. After her identification, they began searching for her family members, who are believed to reside in Posadas and Paraguay.
> 
> Regarding the attack, police stated they are investigating. However, locals indicated that security cameras in the area captured the attack on "Caí" by a group of men from the Mbya community. The motive for the beating is unknown. No arrests have been reported so far.
