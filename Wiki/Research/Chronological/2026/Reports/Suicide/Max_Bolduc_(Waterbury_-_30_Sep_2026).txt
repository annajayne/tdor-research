> Makaela Rose “Max” Bolduc was born at Copley Hospital on September 1, 2000, and passed away peacefully at his home on September 30, 2026. 
> 
> Max loved life. He fought incredibly hard to live and had a deep desire to be here. His family wants him remembered for the love he shared, the people he cared for, and the gentle spirit that made him who he was. 
> 
> Max loved his family deeply. He was a loving son, brother, grandson, nephew, and cousin, and his family meant the world to him. He also shared a deep and loving relationship with his girlfriend, Casandra Patton, who held a very special place in his heart. The love they shared brought him great happiness, and Casandra was an important part of his life. 
> 
> Max had a special heart for caring for others. He worked for five years at Maple Ridge Memory Care as a licensed nursing assistant and medical technician. He loved his job and especially loved caring for residents living with dementia. His compassion and gentle nature made caring for others a natural part of who he was. 
> 
> Max also had a great love for animals. His beloved dogs, Mitzi and Willow, held a special place in his heart and brought him comfort, happiness and companionship. 
> 
> He attended Harwood Union High School and graduated in 2018. 
> 
> Max leaves behind his parents, Rachel and Michael Bolduc; his brother, Lennon Bolduc; his grandmother, Sharon Byrd; his aunt, Shelby Miller; his uncle, Patrick Audette; his cousin, John Audette; his beloved girlfriend, Casandra Patton; his cherished dog, Mitzi; and many friends, coworkers, and others whose lives he touched. 
> 
> Rest peacefully, Max. You fought so hard to live, loved so deeply, and gave so much of yourself to others. You were deeply loved, and you will never be forgotten. 
> 
> In lieu of flowers, please consider making a donation to North Country Animal League, 16 Mountain View Meadow Road, Morrisville, VT 05661. Direct any donation questions to donations@ncal.com. 
> 
> A celebration of Max’s life will be held at Hunger Mountain Christian Assembly, 4940 Waterbury-Stowe Road, Waterbury Center, on Oct. 17 at 1 p.m. Light refreshments will follow the service.
