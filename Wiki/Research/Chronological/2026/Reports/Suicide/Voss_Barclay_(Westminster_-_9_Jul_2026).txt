> MISSING PERSON LAST SEEN SATURDAY WITH DOG
> September 7, 2026
> 
> THORNTON, Colo. — Voss, a transgender man, has reportedly been missing since Saturday morning, and his girlfriend is asking the public for help finding him.
> 
> According to his girlfriend, Kirsten DeVon, Voss was last heard from around 11 a.m. Saturday, when he said he was going to the store to get supplies for a planned camping trip.
> 
> DeVon said Thornton Police told her Voss’s vehicle was captured by a Flock camera in Westminster at approximately 11:42 a.m. Saturday.
> Voss is believed to be traveling in a white Toyota Tacoma with Colorado license plate BSCY97. The truck has two main doors with smaller rear doors that open after the front doors are opened. A blue-and-white “Blue Leaf School” sticker is on the back.
> 
> Voss also has his dog, Porter, with him. Porter is described as a roughly 40-pound chocolate Lab.
> 
> DeVon said Voss has been struggling with his mental health for several months and recently left a crisis center in Aurora. Before that, she said, he had been at a crisis center in Littleton off Santa Fe.
> 
> A missing-person report has been filed under case number 2026011826. DeVon also said there is a nationwide Attempt to Locate notice for Voss.
> 
> Anyone who has seen Voss, Porter or the white Toyota Tacoma, or who has information about their whereabouts, should contact Thornton Police and reference case number 2026011826.
> 
> Information in this report comes from a public missing-person appeal posted by Voss’s girlfriend. Updates will be provided as additional verified information becomes available.
