On Monday morning, we received the sad news that our friend and comrade M. had taken her own life.
M. loved listening to music, for example Disastar, whose concert she attended last October. No matter where she was, she had her Blahaj with her. Due to work, she relied on her car—an unbelievable piece of junk that felt like it would fall apart at any moment.
She was vegan, out of love for animals

She didn't cook much, but one dish she often cooked and loved was Korean fried tofu.
M. was a quiet and reserved person who nevertheless made a huge impact on our lives. She was the kind of friend you could call at any time and she would come right away.
She leaves behind a lot of grief and anger. Anger, not at her, but at all the crap. At the system that she could never have changed on her own.
Despite everything, she made the world a better place
