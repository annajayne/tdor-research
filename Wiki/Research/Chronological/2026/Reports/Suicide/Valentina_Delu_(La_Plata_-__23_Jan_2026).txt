> A young woman was found dead in the Teatro Argentino in La Plata
> Security personnel at the Teatro Argentino found a 26-year-old woman dead in a basement
> January 23, 2026 - 2:31 PM
> 
> In the last few hours, police officers conducting their routine security patrol inside the Teatro Argentino in La Plata spotted a body in the building's central courtyard . This area is difficult to access from inside the building, leading to speculation that the body entered from the outside.
> 
> The young woman's body was found in a basement, showing no vital signs. Upon discovery, authorities were notified, paramedics confirmed her death, and the scene was secured for forensic investigation.
> 
> According to initial reports, the woman climbed to the upper floor of the theater via the exterior and from there jumped to the basement , ending her life.
> 
> She was carrying a backpack with personal belongings and documents , so she was quickly identified as 26-year-old Valentina Delú. She is a transgender woman from La Plata. The case is now in the hands of the UFI N°7 (Specialized Prosecutor's Office No. 7) and the Court of Guarantees No. 3 , who are analyzing security camera footage from the area to gather more information.
