>  A transgender woman known as Tutu was found dead on Thursday morning, the 21st, in Itapororoca, in the interior of Paraíba. Her body was under a bridge over the Mamanguape River, in an area connecting the municipality to Cuité de Mamanguape.
> 
> According to information based on data from the Civil Police, the main suspicion is that Tutu was murdered by being beaten with a stick.
> 
> The police reported that the victim lived in the region.
> 
> The Legal Medicine and Dentistry Center of Guarabira was called to carry out the forensic examination.
> 
> Investigations are ongoing to identify and locate the suspects.
