> Thea Lourdes Masica of Plymouth, Minnesota passed away on March 3, 2026 at the age of 24. She was born on June 5, 2001 in Minneapolis, Minnesota. She graduated from Wayzata High School in 2019.
> 
> Thea was preceded in death by her Great Grandmother (Grandma Sweetheart), her Grandfather Charles Donner, Her Grandmother Charlotte Masica, and her Grandfather Michael D. Masica.
> 
> She is survived by Michael and Laura Masica (father and mother), Ian and Sarah Masica (brother/sister-in-law), Duncan Masica (brother), and Sandra Donner (grandmother)
> 
> A Memorial will be held to celebrate her life at Unity Minneapolis, 4000 Golden Valley Road, Golden Valley, MN. If you are unable to attend in person, please attend virtually using this link: https://online.brushfire.com/unityminneapolis/memorial-service/yg3wq4rx3w
> 
> In honor of Thea’s own expressive dress style, we would ask those attending to feel free to wear their favorite flannel, band t-shirt, beanie or whatever demonstrates your own self-expression. She would love to see an audience full of individual creativity.
> 
> Thea is a beautiful, creative and compassionate soul, who will be forever in our memory. Her smile could light up a room and her giggle was contagious. Thea is very empathetic and cared deeply for her family, friends and community. Her superpower is the ability to connect people together through her relationships. She created and maintained safe, supportive and loving spaces that touched the hearts and souls of so many.
> 
> There are no words that describe the heartbreak we all feel. It is hard to imagine a life without her and the energy she brings to this world. We feel so blessed to have the support of friends, family and community during this difficult time.
> 
> Anyone that knew Thea, knew that she cared deeply for the well-being of others; especially those in the LGBTQ+ community. To honor that, we have created this fundraising page for the Trevor Project. Our goal is to raise $25,000 in her honor.
> 
> We request that in lieu of gifts or flowers, please donate to this valuable organization that supports the mental health of the LGBTQ+ community. Any donation amount would be greatly appreciated. https://give.thetrevorproject.org/fundraiser/7060365
