> Transgender woman dies by suicide after marital dispute in Siddipet
> 
> Siddipet: Differences with her husband drove a transgender to suicide in Siddipet town. The deceased Sunanda (28) was found hanging from the ceiling at her residence at Indiramma Colony in the town on Thursday. According to her friend Sunitha, Sunanda married Ajay some three years ago. However, the duo developed differences after the man started moving with another woman. As the differences turned serious, Sunanda started living separately. Depressed over the strained relationship and vexed with life, Sunanda took the extreme step. Siddipet one-town police registered a case and the probe was on.
