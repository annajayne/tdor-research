> Following the suicide of Arya, a trans woman and student at TOBB University of Economics and Technology, in her dormitory, the "What Happened to Arya Initiative," established by LGBTQ+ student communities at the university, described the events following her suicide on Arya's birthday with the words, " We know that Arya's suicide was a result of transphobic policies. Therefore, we demand that an effective investigation be conducted and that it not be forgotten ."
> 
> Arya, a transgender woman and student in the English Language and Literature Department at TOBB Economics and Technology University (TOBB ETÜ), died on April 13, 2026, in the TOBB ETÜ dormitories in Ankara. Following allegations that Arya was driven to suicide by verbal and physical violence, DEM Party Istanbul Member of Parliament Özgül Saki brought the matter to the attention of the Parliament.




> Arya, a transgender student at TOBB ETÜ , died in her student dormitory. DEM Party Istanbul MP Özgül Saki brought the allegations surrounding Arya's death to the attention of parliament, demanding an investigation. Saki stated, " There is information suggesting that Arya suffered verbal abuse from dormitory administrators and physical and verbal abuse from other students. We know that this suicide is a result of the hatred and systematic exclusion organized by the AKP government for years, the anti-LGBTI+ regulations disseminated through 'Family Year' rhetoric, and the targeting spread from political platforms. Transgender suicides are political."
> 
> The suicide of Arya, a transgender student in the English Language and Literature Department at TOBB Economics and Technology University (TOBB ETÜ), in the TOBB ETÜ dormitories in Ankara on April 13th, came to public attention after other students launched a social media campaign with the slogan "Be Arya's voice," followed by a condolence message released by the university's rectorate.
> 
> DEM Party Istanbul MP ??Özgül Saki brought the issue to the parliamentary agenda. In her speech in parliament, Saki drew attention to the fact that trans+ suicides cannot be evaluated separately from the government's anti-LGBTQ+ policies, and stated the following:
> 
> "We have received information that Arya, a student at TOBB University of Economics and Technology, committed suicide in her dormitory in Ankara on April 13th. There is information suggesting that Arya suffered verbal abuse from dormitory administrators and physical and verbal abuse from other students. Students state that LGBT+ individuals do not feel safe on campus, and that the sexual orientation of LGBT+ students is shared without their consent. Friends and relatives of the trans student Arya are demanding an investigation into the bullying, exclusion, and allegations against the dormitory administration. We know that this suicide is a result of the hatred and systematic exclusion organized by the AKP government for years, the anti-LGBT+ regulations disseminated through family year rhetoric, and the targeting disseminated from political platforms. Trans suicides are political."
