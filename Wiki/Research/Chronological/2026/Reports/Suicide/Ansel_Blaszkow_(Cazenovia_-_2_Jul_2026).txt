> CHITTENANGO, N.Y. — The New York State Police found the body of a person who went missing in the Chittenango Falls area in May.
> 
> The state police said the body of Anna J. Blaszkow, 24, was found on July 2 around 11:31 a.m. in a heavily wooded area just outside of Chittenango Falls State Park on Carrys Hill Road in Cazenovia, N.Y.
> 
> Police noted that Blaszkow was also known as "Ansel."
> 
> The police credited Trooper Connors and K9 "Todz" for finding Blaszkow. They were conducting a preliminary inspection of the area in preparation for an additional search that was scheduled with the Chittenango Police Department.
> 
> As of Monday morning, the state police said there is no indication of foul play. The Onondaga County Medical Examiner's Office will conduct an autopsy to determine how Blaszkow died.
> 
> Blaszkow was first reported as missing by the state police on May 16.
> 
> At the time, Blaszkow was last seen by family on May 11. The family was concerned about Blaszkow after they found a letter expressing self-harm.
> 
> Investigators believed that Blaszkow used an "unknown vehicle" from their residence in the Village of Chittenango to Chittenango Falls State Park on May 12.
> 
> The New York State Police Bureau of Criminal Investigation in Oneida, N.Y., has taken the lead in the investigation. The Chittenango Police Department is assisting.
> 
> The investigation into the matter remains ongoing.
