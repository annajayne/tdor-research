There was no newspaper article about her or anything else I could enclose. Please write back to me, if you need anything else in order     to add her to the Remembering our Dead List 2026.



> With great sadness I must announce, that our beautiful and brave daughter Nina died by siucide at
> the 21th of March 2026 at the age of 29.
> 
> After growing more and more silent during male puberty, Nina came out as a trans woman at the age
> of 21 and began transitioning. Although she was supported by family and friends, living in a
> transphobic world, always being questioned and never being fully accepted by society, caused her a
> lot of stress. Unbearable stress that caused depression and made it impossible for her at the end to
> even go to work. She loved her work, she loved her friends, she loved her life. But at the end, there
> was no life energy left to live her life. We only realised after her death how much pain there was
> behind her beautiful smile.
> 
> Nina was gentle and kind to everyone, she was very thoughtful. It was important to her, never to
> hurt the feelings of anyone. She hold a university degree in computer science / data science, she
> was highly intelligent, very interested in politics and history, she loved to travel and loved meeting
> friends. She was into all sorts of games, especially table top games and role playing games. She was
> working voluntarily for a queer film festival.
> 
> Nina was a wonderful person, she is deeply missed by her whole family and friends.
> 
> Fly to the rainbow, Nina! We will always remember you.
