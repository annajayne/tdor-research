> A 14-year-old girl has taken her own life in the province of Ragusa.

> According to reports by activists and #trans groups on social media, the victim is a young trans girl. 
> 
> The news, initially reported by the local press as the suicide of "a boy of just 14," sparked a strong reaction among the trans and LGBTQIA+ community, who denounced misgendering and the silence surrounding the young woman's identity. 
> 
> The Instagram account themystictsunade_ relaunched the story with a different perspective. 
> 
> The slides published read: "A young 14-year-old trans sister committed suicide in Ragusa." And again: "Her name will not be in the newspapers. It will not be on her grave. Once again, the same story. The same silence. The same repression, the same censorship, even in death." 
> 
> The language used is political and blunt. The author denounces "double standards even in death" and speaks of a "deafening silence," claiming that the story was reported without acknowledging the young woman's identity.
