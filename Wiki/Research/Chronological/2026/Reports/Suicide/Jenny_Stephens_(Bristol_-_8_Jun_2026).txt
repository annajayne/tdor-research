> JENNY

> IF YOU KNOW A GIRL NAMED MUD

> SHE'S STILL WORTH YOUR LOVE
> 
> We will love and miss you forever. You are everything we sing for and we will never forget you.
> 
> We met you on our first tour, right at the start of our journey and we were bowled over by your love and enthusiasm. In a strange city we had never been to you came bounding over to us, full of joy and got us to sign a condom for your collection. Baffled and flattered, we were full of love and excitement that our music was reaching exactly the kind of girls that we had hoped would love [BRICK]. And that was just the beginning.
> 
> 
> You were behind our first sold out show in Bristol, it was also the first show you had ever put on. We were more than a little nervous after you declared to us that you had no idea what you were doing "but after a certain point it just runs itself, right?". Well it was a blinding success and we were so proud of you for pulling off such a brilliant night. You made us feel so welcome and even organised a delicious sigil cake for Joy's birthday! We were struck by how caring you were and how comfortable you made us feel on the road. But we also learned then that you were troubled.
> 
> There was a sadness in you and you told us that you were starting to feel really isolated.
> 
> We left feeling a little bit worried and promised to keep in touch.
> 
> 
> Over the next few months we came to understand that trouble was brewing. People who were once friends were turning their backs on you and shunning you. People were spreading whispers and rumours and you no longer felt you could join in with the punk shows you loved so much.
> 
> We were so glad to see you when we next came to Bristol. You helped us lug all our gear around and were outraged when the hardcore kids just crossed their arms as we screamed our hearts out at them. It was hard saying goodbye that night. You looked tired and run down and we could see that you were hurting. We were playing again in Bristol soon and it broke our hearts when you said you didn't think you could come because you felt you had no friends left to come with. We said you were always welcome to hang with us and we would love to see you. We sort of made you promise to come and you did.
> 
> 
> We were so glad when you turned up at the next show. You were looking loads better than we had seen you last and we had such a great time together just yapping and watching you tear up the pit like a mad little coyote. We left that night with hope that you would be ok after all and resolved to always make an effort to catch up whenever we passed through Bristol.
> 
> We will never get to see you again. We will never get to see you grow and thrive. We are all heartbroken at the loss of beautiful you. We are all furious at the cruelty of the world that pushed you to the edges and then let you slip off.
> 
> 
> 
> It's time to stop the whispers and the rumours. It's time to hold your friends close and truly love them. It's time to give all women who are hurting a chance to unravel and then carefully help wind them back together.
> 
> We mean it every night when we scream "IF YOU KNOW A GIRL NAMED MUD, SHE'S STILL WORTH YOUR LOVE"!
> Scream it with us. If you are hurting, if you feel alone come and speak with us.
> 
>
