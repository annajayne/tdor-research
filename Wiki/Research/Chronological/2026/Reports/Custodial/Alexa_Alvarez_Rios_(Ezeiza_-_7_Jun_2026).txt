> Ezeiza prison: reports that a trans woman detained committed suicide because she was not given assistance
> The student center at Ezeiza prison denounces the suicide of Alexa Álvarez Ríos, a 29-year-old trans woman. The student group asserts that prison officials are responsible for denying her access to mental health care. The Federal Court of Lomas de Zamora is investigating the case.
> 16/06/2026
> See the comments from Ezeiza Penitentiary: reports that a trans woman detained committed suicide because she was not given assistance
> 
> 
> From the Ezeiza University Center, located within Federal Penitentiary Complex 1, it has been reported that a fellow inmate, Alexa Álvarez Ríos, 29, a trans woman, committed suicide because prison authorities denied her access to mental health care. The Federal Court of Lomas de Zamora is investigating.  
> 
> "Alexa was unwell and everyone knew it; it could have been prevented if she had received help from mental health professionals," the CUE told Tiempo . They added, "She was very depressed; we know she had a very hard and painful life ."
> 
> Alexa's body was found lifeless in the early hours of June 7th inside a cell in Pavilion 5, designated for the LGBTQ+ community. The woman was going through a very difficult time and hanged herself , her fellow inmates say; "she never saw a psychologist."
> 
> "We have been denouncing for some time that detainees are dying due to a lack of medical attention and mental health professionals. We are talking about deaths that occur in state custody and that could be prevented ," they emphasized. 
> 
> The students denounced: " We consider it a slow-motion genocide, something very ugly that expresses a great contempt for human life ." 
> 
> In this context, Tiempo contacted Ornella Infante , general secretary of the Argentine LGBT Federation and activist of the Evita Movement: " Since the arrival of the government of Javier Milei, the restrictive measures have intensified ," she denounced. 
> 
> He also pointed out that protocols developed jointly with the National Penitentiary Ombudsman's Office (PPN), social organizations, and the former National Institute Against Discrimination, Xenophobia, and Racism (INADI) had been repealed. He explained that this action destroyed what they had worked so hard to build.
> 
> Ornella stated: “Today, LGBT-oriented protocols have ended; today, transfers—for example—are impulsive, and harassment is constant and intense . There are no gender policies being implemented in the prison. To prevent another case like Alexa’s, we must continue training the prison service.” 
> 
> "Recently, there were rapes of women detained in the Magdalena prison, including waterboarding, practices that were used during dark periods in Argentine history . The prison guards involved have been removed from their posts, but the justice system isn't helping ," the general secretary pointed out.
> 
> The secretary general continued to denounce that the Federal Penitentiary Service hinders the entry of social organizations and universities, which are the ones that provide some protection to people deprived of their liberty. 
> 
> Infante considered the worsening of torture practices immeasurable . He concluded by pointing out a significant setback in healthcare and education, which are crucial for the social reintegration of detainees.
