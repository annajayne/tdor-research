> Travesti found dead inside cell in Viana prison.
> The victim was being held in a unit designated for the LGBTQIA+ population. Two inmates confessed to the crime after an argument, according to Sejus.
> 
> A travesti was found dead inside a cell at Penitenciária de Segurança Média 2 (PSME II), in Complexo Penitenciário de Viana, early Sunday morning (27). The victim was identified by the social name Gabrielle and was known as Bananinha.
> 
> According to information from the Department of Justice (Sejus), two inmates confessed to committing the homicide after an argument. Two other inmates who were in the same cell at the time of the crime were also taken to give statements to the police authority.
> 
> The Sejus (Secretary of Justice) reported that the Civil Police and the Scientific Police were called in and that the case will be investigated. PSME II is designated for the custody of the LGBTQIA+ population and is located in the Rodrigo Figueiredo da Rosa Penitentiary Complex in Viana . Sejus identifies the unit as a Medium Security Penitentiary II.
> 
> According to a source interviewed for this report, the victim was decapitated. However, information regarding the circumstances and the nature of the injuries will need to be clarified by the police investigation and forensic examination.
> 
> Gabrielle had been in prison since June 8, 2024. According to information provided to the reporter, she was facing charges of robbery and drug trafficking.
> 
> Among the two inmates who confessed to the homicide, one has been imprisoned since August 2, 2017, and is charged with rape of a vulnerable person, conspiracy to traffic drugs, and property damage. The other has been in the prison system since April 23 of this year, for the crimes of drug trafficking and conspiracy to traffic drugs.
> 
> The other two inmates questioned by the police also have criminal records. One of them has been incarcerated since May 31, 2022, for drug trafficking and receiving stolen goods. The other has been in custody since August 3, 2023, for drug trafficking and robbery.
> 
> According to Sejus, eight violent deaths were recorded in prisons in Espírito Santo in 2026, including the case that occurred on Sunday.
> 
> The investigation should determine the circumstances of the homicide, the participation of each of the four inmates, and the motivation behind the argument that preceded the crime.



> A trans woman was found dead inside a cell at Medium-Security Penitentiary 2 (PSME2), part of the Viana Penitentiary Complex. The incident was recorded early last Sunday (27) and gained public attention this Tuesday (29). The facility is designated for the custody of LGBTQIA+ individuals. According to the Department of Justice (Sejus), the victim—identified by the initials L.R.O. — was found dead in her cell.
> 
> Two inmates, identified as A.M.S. and A.J.A., confessed to participating in the crime following an argument. Information released regarding the case indicates that the victim was decapitated. However, no details have been disclosed so far regarding the motive for the attack. Two other inmates, E.M.C.M. and U.S.P., who were also in the cell at the time of the crime, were taken to the police station to give statements. The Civil Police and the Forensic Police were called to the scene.
> 
> The case remains under investigation. Regarding those involved: The victim had been incarcerated since June 8, 2024. According to Sejus, she faced charges for robbery, arson, and drug trafficking. One of the inmates who confessed to the crime, A.M.S., has been detained since August 2, 2017; he faces charges for the rape of a vulnerable person, criminal association for drug trafficking, and property damage. A.J.A. has been imprisoned since April 23, 2026, for drug trafficking and criminal association for drug trafficking.
> 
> Meanwhile, E.M.C.M., who was also in the cell, has been incarcerated since May 31, 2022, for drug trafficking and receiving stolen goods. U.S.P. has been imprisoned since August 3, 2023, for drug trafficking and robbery.
