> VIDEO WITH UPDATES: TRANS WOMAN DIES AFTER FALLING ILL AT RESIDENCIAL TUPÃ, IN ALFENAS
> Military police and forensic experts from the Civil Police are at the scene; the cause of death is still under investigation.
> August 9, 2026
>  
> In Alfenas, on the afternoon of this Sunday (09/08), a trans woman, identified as Daniele Colt, died after falling ill in Residencial Tupã, in the Jardim São Paulo/Alvorada neighborhood area.
> 
> According to preliminary information, Daniele began to feel unwell, and someone who lives with her called for help. SAMU (Mobile Emergency Care Service) and Fire Department teams arrived at the address and found the victim in cardiorespiratory arrest.
> 
> Paramedics performed resuscitation procedures, but Daniele did not survive and was pronounced dead at the scene.
> 
> Given the situation, the Military Police were called in, cordoned off the area, and requested the presence of the Civil Police forensics team, which is currently monitoring the incident.
> 
> According to reports from people close to her, Daniele was undergoing treatment for a wound that had worsened. It was also reported that, the previous night, an unknown woman had given her a brigadeiro (a Brazilian chocolate truffle) and, subsequently, she began to feel unwell. Initially, the possibility of poisoning was ruled out after a medical evaluation at the Santa Casa hospital.
> 
> Authorities initially suspect the death may have been due to natural causes, possibly a heart attack. However, the cause of death has not yet been officially confirmed.
> 
> The body will be sent to the Forensic Medical Institute, where examinations will be carried out that may assist in the investigation and clarify the circumstances of the death.
> 
> The incident is still ongoing and new information may be released after the forensic procedures are completed.
