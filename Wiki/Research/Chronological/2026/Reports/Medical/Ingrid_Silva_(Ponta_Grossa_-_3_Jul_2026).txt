> Transgender woman found dead inside residence in Ponta Grossa.
> A transgender woman was found dead inside a residence in Ponta Grossa. The incident occurred in the Contorno neighborhood, and the cause of death will be investigated.
> 03/07/2026 · 17:24Updated 17:352 min read
> 
> A transsexual woman was found dead on Friday afternoon (3) inside a residence on Rua Salvador de Mendonça, in the Contorno region, in Ponta Grossa.
> 
> According to initial information gathered by BNT Online, the victim was found lying on the floor of the house, already without vital signs. The initial assessment did not identify any injuries or apparent signs of violence, and the main hypothesis is that the death occurred due to natural causes. Confirmation, however, will depend on the examinations carried out by the Scientific Police.
> 
> According to the report, the victim had a history of tuberculosis and also had a diagnosis related to classification B24. It is estimated that she had been dead for more than 24 hours before being found.
> 
> According to information gathered at the scene, only a woman and a man lived in the residence. According to his statement to the teams that responded to the incident, the two met approximately a year ago at a bar, and since then, she had been living in the property.
> 
> According to the resident, he realized that the woman had already been dead since Thursday (2). However, it was only this Friday that he was able to ask for help. A niece of the man sought assistance from the Scientific Police, who contacted the other agencies responsible for handling the incident.
> 
> SAMU teams were at the address to provide support and remove the body, which was taken to the morgue at the UPA Santana, where it will remain at the disposal of the Scientific Police for forensic procedures.
> 
> The victim's identity had not been officially released at the time of publication. The case will be investigated, and the cause of death will be confirmed after the completion of the forensic reports.
> 
> BNT Online is following the case and will bring you new information as soon as it is released by the authorities.
