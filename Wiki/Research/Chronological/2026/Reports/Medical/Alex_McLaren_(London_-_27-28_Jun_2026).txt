====== Alex McLaren (London - 27-28 Jun 2026) ======

Alex McLaren ("Alexandraa Live")

London (TBC)
United Kingdom
overdose


> Hello. We're writing to enquire as to the list of the dead for that is read out on Trans Day of Remembrance.
> How do we find out if our friend and comrade Alex McLaren is on the list?
>
> She died of drug overdose in June this year
>
> She went by Alexandraa Live as a stage name and has a wife and two kids still grieving her.



> By now most of you will be aware that our good friend Alexandraa passed away this weekend.
> 
> We only had the opportunity to get to know Alex for a year—but that year is one we will cherish for the rest of our lives. Launching Defund Transphobes, and trying to build a community of activists to advocate for our rights, was one of the most intense and empowering experiences of our lives. Alex was with us from almost the very beginning—and was always eager to know when the next event was.
> 
> She showed up to a topless protest in Manchester day, got involved, made an incredible speech on a megaphone without any preparation, and then asked what we would be doing next. She was a friend and a comrade in arms against a world that felt like it was closing in on us from all directions. 
> 
> She spent her whole life feeling she wasn't good enough or anxious that she wasn't doing the right thing—but she was one of the very best Def Trans activists we had. We feel so incredibly privileged to have known Alex, to have worked with her, to have broken bread with her, to have laughed and cried with her, and to have listened to her beautiful music.
> 
> There will always be an Alex shaped hole in DefTrans but if there is anything we have learned from working with the trans community it is that we are made up of exceptional individuals and more of us are discovering ourselves each and every year. 
> 
> This is an incredibly dark and difficult time for us and our community but we must believe it will not always be so. We must honour the memory of our lost siblings by continuing to move forward and by reaching outwards instead of shrinking inwards.
> 
> We will be holding a memorial for Alex on Sunday the 19th of July.
> 
> We will be meeting in Soho Gardens at 4:30pm and we will be doing a mass chalking mural before going and spending some time together as a community for the whole evening. There will be some accommodation available. DM us for details.
> 
> Feel free to bring flowers and please bring drinking water.
