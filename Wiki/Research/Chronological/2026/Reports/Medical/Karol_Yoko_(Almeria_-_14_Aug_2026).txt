> Transgender woman dies of heart attack in Spain; family launches campaign to bring her body back to Brazil.
> The family of Karol Yoko, a 29-year-old woman from Acre, is trying to raise R$ 79,000 to repatriate her body from Spain to Brazil. She died last August 14th in the city of Almería, in the south of the European country. The transgender woman, originally from Senador Guiomard and with a history in Rio Branco, had been living in Europe for five years seeking better job opportunities.
> 
> The family had lost contact with Karol on August 11th and even reported her disappearance to the Federal Police. Confirmation of her death only came on Tuesday, August 25th, when relatives were informed that the young woman had suffered a sudden heart attack in her hotel room. In previous conversations with her sister, the victim had reported experiencing severe headaches and chest pains.
> 
> Lacking the financial means to cover the costs required for the release and international transport of the body, the family started a fundraising campaign. So far, they have only managed to raise R$ 1,800 of the total needed for the burial to take place in Acre.
