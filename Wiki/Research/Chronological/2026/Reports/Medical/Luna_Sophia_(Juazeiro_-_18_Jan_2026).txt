> A trans woman was found agonizing in her home on Sunday afternoon (18) in Juazeiro, where military police were present. It was around 3 pm and Luna Sophia Gabriel Silva dos Santos, 28 years old, was quickly taken to the Cariri Regional Hospital (HRC), but arrived lifeless. She lived on Travessa São Damião (Santa Tereza) and died on her birthday.
> 
> Due to the suspicious circumstances of the death, the body was collected by the coroner's van to be autopsied at the Forensic Science Institute (Pefoce) in Juazeiro. There are suspicions that it was a case of drug overdose, which will be investigated in the post-mortem examination. Luna Sophia's body is being viewed at her residence and will be buried this Monday afternoon.
