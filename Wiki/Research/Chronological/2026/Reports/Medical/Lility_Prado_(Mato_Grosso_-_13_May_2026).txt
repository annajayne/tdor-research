> It is with deep sorrow that associação de Trans e travestis de Mato Grosso – Conscientização e Direitos Humanos bids farewell to Lility Prado, one of the great partners of the social movement and the articulations in defense of the LGBTQIAPN+ population. 
> 
> Lility Prado, being the first president of the trans and transvestite association of Mato Grosso, was part of the construction of the struggle and resistance of the trans movement in Mato Grosso, leaving her mark through courage, solidarity, and commitment to human rights. 
> 
> In this moment of grief, we stand in solidarity with family, friends, and the entire community that shares this irreparable loss. Her trajectory will remain alive in the memory and history of our movement. 
> 
> Rest in peace, Lility Prado. Your struggle will never be forgotten.




> Former president of the Association of Transvestites of Mato Grosso, Lilith Prado, a reference in the fight for the rights of the LGBTQIA+ population, especially trans and transvestite people in the state, died this Wednesday (13).
> 
> Lilith was hospitalized for treatment of an undisclosed health problem, but she did not survive. Her death was confirmed by the Livre Mente group, which published a note of condolence on social media.
> 
> "Lilith Prado was part of building the struggle and resistance of the trans movement in Mato Grosso, leaving her mark through courage, solidarity, and commitment to human rights," says an excerpt from the statement.
> 
> The organization also highlighted the activist's importance to the social movement in Mato Grosso. "In this moment of grief, we stand in solidarity with her family, friends, and the entire community that shares in this irreparable loss. Her life will remain alive in the memory and history of our movement," the group stated.
> 
> Throughout her career, Lilith denounced numerous episodes of violence against transgender people, especially sex workers operating in the Baixada Cuiabana region. She herself was a victim of aggression throughout her life, including being beaten with an iron bar in the Zero Km area of ​​Várzea Grande.
> 
> In 2011, Lilith also gained national prominence by becoming one of the first transvestites in the country to contribute to the INSS (Brazilian Social Security Institute) as a sex worker, an activity recognized by the Ministry of Labor.
> 
> The activist's death generated expressions of grief among members of social movements and human rights defenders in Mato Grosso.

> Former president of the Association of Trans and Transvestites of Mato Grosso – Awareness and Human Rights (ASTTRAMT), Lilith Prado, died this Wednesday (13), at the age of 48. In a statement, ASTTRAMT highlighted that Lilith was the first president of the association and "was part of the construction of the struggle and resistance of the trans movement in Mato Grosso, leaving her mark through courage, solidarity and commitment to human rights". She was praised for being one of the great partners of the social movement and the articulations in defense of the LGBTQIAPN+ population. The association expressed its condolences to Lilith's family and friends.
