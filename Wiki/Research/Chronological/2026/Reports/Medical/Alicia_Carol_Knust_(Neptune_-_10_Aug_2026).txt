> Alicia C Knust
> August 29, 1981 - August 10, 2026
> 
> Alicia Carol Knust, 44 years old, passed away Monday, August 10, 2026, surrounded by loved ones at Jersey Shore Medical Center in Neptune, NJ.  She was born in New Brunswick, NJ on August 29, 1981.  She attended elementary school in Piscataway, NJ.  She moved to Wall Twp., NJ where she attended junior and high school, graduating in 1999. She obtained a Bachelor of Fine Arts degree from the School of Visual Arts in Manhattan. 
> 
> Alicia was a passionate, free spirit who enjoyed music, art, fan conventions, gardening and her pet cats and dogs.
> 
> Alicia is pre-deceased by her nana Carol Knust, grandfather William Knust, Aunts Shirley and Ginny Knust, Ruth Tauriello, and loved ones Pop-Pop Vic LaRosa and Charlie Faller. Alicia is survived by her mother Linda Knust, father Nicholas Agostino and partner Jan Murray, daughters Annika and Zoe Traxler, half-sister Nicole Grebosz, aunts Judy and Valerie Knust, Janice Scheinler-Moore, Marissa Kaminski, uncle Bill Knust, cousins Allison and Dana Vanore, Krista Szoboszlay, Dawn, Melissa, and Marc Tauriello, Ryan and Emily Knust, Holly Harriss, Nathaniel Dudley, partner Don DeVries, and many nieces, nephews and loved ones including Angel Delgatto and Steven DiGuiseppe.
