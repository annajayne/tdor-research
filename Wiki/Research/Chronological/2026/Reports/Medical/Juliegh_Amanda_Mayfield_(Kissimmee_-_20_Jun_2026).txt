> Juleigh Amanda Mayfield Obituary
> Juleigh Amanda Mayfield, 51, passed away on June 20, 2026, surrounded by the love of her parents.
> 
> Born James Bradford Mayfield on June 18, 1975, in Alabama, Juleigh lived a life marked by courage, humor, faith, beauty, and purpose. She often spoke with love and gratitude about the years she lived as Brad, recognizing that those experiences helped shape the woman she would later become. Brad was quirky, outgoing, funny, and larger than life, and Juleigh carried those qualities with her throughout her extraordinary journey.
> 
> Juleigh was born intersex, with a 47XXY chromosome variation, and became a passionate advocate for the intersex community. She used her voice, intellect, and unmistakable presence to educate others, challenge assumptions, and encourage greater compassion and understanding. She believed that people should never be hidden, silenced, or made to feel ashamed for being different.
> 
> Juleigh was a storyteller, teacher, advocate, and fighter. She had the rare ability to turn pain into purpose, silence into conversation, and hardship into an opportunity for others to learn. Her advocacy reached countless people, including her devoted online community, whom she affectionately called her "little cocktails."
> 
> She was also known for her humor, dramatic flair, love of beauty, and the way she could command a room. For Juleigh, presentation was never simply about appearance. It was about dignity, confidence, and being seen. She loved makeup, fashion, baking, Disney, sharks, Steel Magnolias, and anything that allowed her creativity and personality to shine.
> 
> At the center of Juleigh's life was her deep faith in God. Her Christian values guided her compassion, forgiveness, and belief that every person was created with purpose and worthy of love. During the final months of her life, the hymn "It Is Well with My Soul" seemed to find her again and again. When it played unexpectedly, she would smile and say, "Okay, God. I get it. I get it."
> 
> Even during her final days, Juleigh continued to touch the lives of the doctors, nurses, caregivers, friends, and family members who surrounded her. Her courage, honesty, humor, and faith left a lasting impression on everyone who had the privilege of knowing her.
> 
> Juleigh is survived by her mother, Bootsie Mayfield; her father, Bob Mayfield; her brothers, Craig Mayfield and Alex Mayfield; her sister, Courtney Jackson; and her beloved nieces and nephews. She also leaves behind a large circle of friends, chosen family, advocates, and followers whose lives were changed by her presence.
> 
> Juleigh's life was not ordinary. It was bold, complicated, beautiful, painful, joyful, and entirely her own. She hoped to be remembered for her fierce advocacy, her unmistakable spirit, her faith, her laughter, and her refusal to allow the world to look away from what deserved to be seen. She taught those around her that if you have a voice, you should use it; if you have a story, you should tell it; and if no one gives you a seat at the table, you should pull up your own chair.
> 
> Juleigh Amanda Mayfield was seen, heard, and deeply loved. She was, and will always be- Just Juleigh. 
>
