> A well-known trans woman was found dead inside a bar in Veracruz.
> The trans woman, who was highly regarded in the region, was found dead inside the La Vicenta bar in Misantla, Veracruz.
> The victim was formally identified as José Guadalupe Soto Landa, 38 years old, alias La Rabiosa.
> By Sergio Aldazaba, July 26, 2026 · 6:47 PM
> 
> An intense mobilization by police corporations and emergency services was registered this Sunday inside an establishment in the Benito Juárez neighborhood in the city of Misantla, after the lifeless body of a well-known trans woman was found.
> 
> The discovery took place in the bar called “La Vicenta”, where people who entered the premises found the victim lying down, formally identified as José Guadalupe Soto Landa, approximately 38 years old, who was affectionately nicknamed “La Rabiosa”.
> 
> It was revealed that the deceased was originally from the town of La Vigueta, worked as a waitress in the aforementioned establishment , and occasionally worked as an assistant to a well-known dressmaker in the municipal capital, so she was quite well-regarded in the area.
> 
> After the alert was issued to rescue corporations, first responders from the National Rescue Squad arrived at the scene to provide initial care, but unfortunately they could only confirm that he no longer had vital signs, and the Municipal Police proceeded to cordon off the scene.
> 
> Minutes later, members of the Ministerial Police and experts from the State Attorney General's Office arrived to remove the body and transfer it to the facilities of the Forensic Medical Service, where the required autopsy will be performed to determine the precise causes of death.
> 
> The incident caused shock among residents of the municipality of Misantla and members of the LGBTIQ+ community, who expressed their dismay on social media, while authorities continue their investigations to clarify how the events occurred.
