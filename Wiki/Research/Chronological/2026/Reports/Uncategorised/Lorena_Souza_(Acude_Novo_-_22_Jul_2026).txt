> Transgender woman found dead in a pond in the rural area of Garanhuns.
> Lorena's body was recovered from the water by firefighters and taken to the IML (Forensic Medical Institute) in Caruaru; the Civil Police will investigate the case.
> Published on July 23, 2026 at 6:50 AM
> 
> The Civil Police will investigate the circumstances of the death of a trans woman found on Wednesday afternoon (22) in Açude Novo, in the rural area of Garanhuns, in the Agreste region of Pernambuco.
> 
> The victim was identified by local residents as Lorena. The body was inside the reservoir and was found wearing only underwear.
> 
> The victim's remaining clothing was not found near the reservoir, according to information gathered at the scene by the Portal Agreste Violento.
> 
> Fire department teams were called to remove the body from the water. The area also underwent initial procedures related to the incident.
> 
> After the rescue, Lorena's body was sent to the Institute of Legal Medicine in Caruaru, where it will undergo examination.
> 
> The forensic examination will determine the cause of death. The investigation will also seek to clarify how the victim arrived at the reservoir and what circumstances preceded the death.
