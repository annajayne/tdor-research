> Michelle Spikes was a trans woman from Georgia. She died at 44 yo. She was a community member in Washington DC and worked for HIPS [https://www.instagram.com/p/DXNL4GqEwCS/](https://www.instagram.com/p/DXNL4GqEwCS/) and did harm reduction. She took part in an online International Transgender Day of Visibility event in 2023 [https://bcph.org/visiblity/](https://bcph.org/visiblity/) 
> I haven't found the cause of death.


> HIPS mourns the passing of Michelle Spikes, a community member, fierce and caring advocate, and former HIPS staff member. Michelle served across many aspects of HIPS' work, including overnight shifts with our mobile services team and with our clinical services team. Michelle’s bright, beautiful smile will be missed. May she rest in power. She is already greatly missed by her friends and colleagues.
