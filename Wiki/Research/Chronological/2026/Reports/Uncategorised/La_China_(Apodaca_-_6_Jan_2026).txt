> Monterrey, Nuevo León – The Nuevo León Attorney General's Office has launched an investigation to determine the cause of death of a transgender woman whose body was found in an informal settlement in the municipality of Apodaca. The body was located inside a makeshift structure next to the Topo Chico stream, around 9:00 a.m. this Tuesday, under the Periférico highway overpass.
> 
> Although initial examinations revealed no obvious signs of violence, authorities have indicated that the forensic expert report will officially confirm the cause of death. Preliminary findings suggest it may have been due to natural causes, given that the victim had reported persistent discomfort, primarily related to gastric problems, according to a neighbor.
> 
> The deceased, unofficially identified as "La China," 31 years old, reportedly had no close relatives in the area. A neighbor reported that the woman had been in poor health for the past few weeks, suffering from what appeared to be stomach infections.
> 
> State investigators and municipal police officers arrived at the scene, cordoned off the area, and collected witness statements. The body was taken to the Forensic Medical Service for examination. The investigation remains open to rule out any foul play and fully clarify the circumstances of the death.



> Macabre discovery near the Topo Chico stream; trans woman found dead in Nuevo León
> A transgender woman was found dead inside a cardboard and wood shack next to the Topo Chico stream in Nuevo León; authorities are investigating the cause of death.
> Written in NATIONAL on 6/1/2026 · 11:10 PM
> 
> A trans woman was found dead inside a makeshift room made of wood and cardboard, next to the Topo Chico stream, in the municipality of Apodaca, Nuevo León, which generated a police mobilization in the area.
> 
> The discovery was reported under the Periférico highway bridge, behind the Triana housing development and approximately 600 meters from the old Agua Fría road, on an irregular property.
> 
> The deceased person has not been officially identified; however, a local resident indicated that she was known as 'La China', approximately 31 years old, and that she allegedly had no relatives in the area.
> 
> The witness reported that, for three or four weeks, the woman had been experiencing various health problems, mainly related to gastric issues, and had therefore been bedridden on several occasions.
> 
> He added that the day before he had seen her in delicate condition inside her makeshift home and that, upon returning on Tuesday morning, he found her without vital signs.
> 
> Ministerial agents, in coordination with municipal police, cordoned off the area and carried out the necessary legal procedures. The official cause of death will be determined by the forensic expert report.
