> Authorities are conducting investigations to clarify the death of a transgender woman known as "La Chapola," whose body was found in recent hours in a rural area of eastern Antioquia.
> 
> The incident occurred between the municipalities of La Ceja and Rionegro, specifically near the La Pereira stream, in an area known as San Sebastián, on the afternoon of Monday, June 8. Judicial units arrived at the scene to conduct a forensic examination of the body and begin the corresponding investigation.
> 
> The victim, identified as Paulina Blandón Villada, was a well-known figure in various social circles in the municipality of La Ceja, which has generated numerous reactions since the news broke. Through social media, friends, acquaintances, and members of the LGBTQ+ community have shared messages of farewell and expressions of condolence, as well as concerns about what they believe may be a case of transphobia.
> 
> Preliminary reports from the municipal administration indicate that no external signs of violence were found during the initial inspection. However, authorities clarified that the forensic medical report will definitively establish the cause of death, and therefore the case remains under investigation.
> 
> While forensic procedures are underway, the incident continues to have an impact on the community, where the victim was known for her close ties to different social groups and processes in the area.



> Authorities are investigating the death of Paulina Blandón Villada, a trans woman known in eastern Antioquia as 'La Chapola' . The discovery of her body has shocked family, friends, and members of the LGBTQ+ community.
> 
> Authorities found the body in the La Pereira ravine, in the San Sebastián sector of La Ceja , on the road leading to the municipality of Rionegro.
> 
> Following the discovery, the La Ceja Mayor's Office activated the corresponding protocols to clarify what happened. The municipal Secretary of Government, Geovanny Henao , reported that the body showed no signs of violence at the time of the inspection.
> 
> "The body showed no signs of violence ," the official said, adding that authorities are conducting investigations to determine the circumstances of the incident.
> 
> Authorities have not yet determined the cause of death. The National Institute of Legal Medicine will conduct forensic analyses to establish what happened.
> 
> The mayor of La Ceja, María Ilbed Santa, publicly lamented her departure and recalled that she had recently become involved as a collaborator in the restaurants of the School Feeding Program, PAE.
> 
> The news has caused sadness among residents of La Ceja and other municipalities in eastern Antioquia , where Paulina Blandón was widely known. Through social media, many people expressed their condolences and requested that the authorities clarify the case.
