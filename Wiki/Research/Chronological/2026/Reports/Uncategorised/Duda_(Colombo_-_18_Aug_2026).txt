> Transgender woman found dead in a stream in Colombo; cause of death will be investigated.
> The case will be investigated to determine the cause of death and identify whether it was a case of violence or natural causes.
> Published on August 18, 2026 at 6:11 PM.
> 
> 
> The body of a trans woman , identified as Duda, 36, was found in a stream in the Rio Verde neighborhood, in Colombo, in the Metropolitan Region of Curitiba (RMC), on the afternoon of this Tuesday (18).
> 
> According to information gathered at the scene, the body was found on the side of Rua Sudão. The witness who found the body contacted the Paraná Military Police (PMPR) to report the incident. When the team arrived, they cordoned off the area to carry out the necessary procedures.
> 
> Investigator Fábio Rodrigo, from the Colombo Police Station, explained that the woman's body did not have visible signs of injury , but it will undergo examinations to identify if there are any injuries that were not noticeable.
> 
> "Apparently, according to the forensic examination at the scene, there are no stab wounds or gunshot wounds. The body was sent to the IML (Institute of Legal Medicine) for further examination to see if there are any other types of injuries that apparently did not appear at this time." said Fábio.
> 
> According to investigator Fábio, the body was found in a difficult-to-access location . Removing the body required cutting a fence and using ropes . He also confirmed that the woman lived in the area .
> 
> "The location was difficult to access; the body was inside the stream. We had to retrieve it using ropes, along with the Military Police and the Forensic Police. The personnel had to cut through the fence to enter the area and access the body." he explained.
> 
> The case will be investigated to determine the cause of death and identify whether it was a case of violence or natural causes.First published on Banda B 



> Police investigate death of transgender woman found in stream in Colombo.
> The body was found in the Rio Verde neighborhood; initial forensic examination found no apparent signs of aggression, and the cause of death will be determined by the IML (Institute of Legal Medicine).
> 18/08/2026 • 19:03 • Updated on 18/08/2026 • 19:13
> 
> 
> The Civil Police are investigating the death of a trans woman found inside a stream in the Rio Verde neighborhood, in Colombo, in the Metropolitan Region of Curitiba, this Tuesday (18).
> 
> The body was found on the banks of Rua Sudão, between the culverts of the stream. The victim was reportedly known in the area as Duda, but the identity had not yet been officially confirmed at the time of the last update.
> 
> According to the Military Police, employees of a real estate agency were at the location to inspect a plot of land when they found the body and alerted the police.
> 
> The area was cordoned off to allow for the work of the Civil Police and the Scientific Police.
> 
> After the body was removed, the Scientific Police carried out the initial investigations at the scene.
> 
> According to the information provided during the initial assessment , no apparent signs of aggression were found .
> 
> The absence of visible injuries, however, does not determine the cause of death.
> 
> According to investigator Fábio Rodrigo, from the Colombo Police Station, the Civil Police will await the supplementary examinations carried out by the Institute of Forensic Medicine (IML).
> 
> The autopsy report should help clarify the cause of death and the circumstances in which the victim ended up in the stream .
> 
> Residents reported that Duda was known in the neighborhood and had been seen walking around the area on Tuesday morning.
