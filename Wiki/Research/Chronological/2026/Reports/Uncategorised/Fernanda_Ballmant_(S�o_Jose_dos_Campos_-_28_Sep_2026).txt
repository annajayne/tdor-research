> A body was found in a difficult-to-access area in Jardim Santa Maria, in the east of São José dos Campos , on Monday morning (28). The circumstances of the incident are still being investigated by the authorities.
> 
> Due to the terrain conditions, the Fire Department was mobilized to remove the body. The Military Police and the Civil Police were also called and are monitoring the situation.
> 
> The victim's identity and the circumstances of the death will be investigated by the Scientific Police, who will conduct the forensic examination at the scene.
> 
> To date, there is no official confirmation regarding the identity of the person found or the cause of death.
