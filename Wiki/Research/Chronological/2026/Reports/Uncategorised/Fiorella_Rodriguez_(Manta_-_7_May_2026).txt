> Transgender woman found dead in Manta
> Fiorella Rodríguez, a transgender woman and well-known stylist in Manta, was found dead . The discovery was made on the morning of Thursday, May 7, 2026, inside a home located in the Yes Housing housing development in the city of Manta, Manabí province. The body was found at approximately 11:00 am by neighbors and acquaintances who had not seen her on her usual workday.
> 
> People close to her went to check on her and, receiving no response, entered the property where they found her body without vital signs. National Police personnel arrived at the scene to collect evidence and remove the body, which was taken to the Manta Forensic Center for an autopsy. As of press time, it is unknown whether the death was due to natural or violent causes.
> 
> The alert was triggered by concerns from neighbors and friends regarding Rodríguez's absence from her hair salon located on Avenida Cuatro de Noviembre, in the Picapiedra neighborhood. The woman owned the salon and was a well-known figure in that part of the city. Police officers conducted a forensic inspection of the premises, collecting evidence for the preliminary investigation.
> 
> The body was sent to the forensic center where experts will determine the exact causes of death.
> 
> Police have launched an investigation to determine whether the death was from natural causes or the result of violence. No official statements have been released regarding possible motives, nor have any further details been disclosed about the condition in which the body was found, respecting investigative confidentiality and the presumption of innocence.
> 
> Specialized experts are conducting the corresponding investigations at the residence and will analyze evidence to clarify the circumstances. The autopsy results are expected to provide clarity in the coming hours or days.
> 
> So far, no arrests have been reported, nor have any individuals been directly linked to the case as suspects. Authorities are holding further information.
> 
> Violence in the district
> Manta and the province of Manabí have registered high levels of violence in recent years. In the first months of 2026, the Manta-Montecristi-Jaramijó district continued to report dozens of violent deaths per month. To date, there have been 159 violent deaths.
> 
> According to data from the National Institute of Statistics and Censuses (INEC), more than 53.5% of LGBTI+ people in Ecuador have suffered discrimination or violence, with psychological violence being the most reported (89.8%), followed by violence motivated by prejudice.
> 
> Human rights organizations have documented cases of transfemicide and specific violence against trans women, although many deaths are initially investigated within the general context of citizen insecurity.
