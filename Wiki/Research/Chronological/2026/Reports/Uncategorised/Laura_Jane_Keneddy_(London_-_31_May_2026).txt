> For Laura
> 
> Some sisters die as they have lived alone
> Whether because a loved one turned to hate
> Or that they found their own real life quite late
> Or hated how they sounded on the phone
> Silent let friendships wither. They were sad
> Self medicated with pills drugs or men
> The same old harms go round and round again
> Repeating spirals. Let's at least be glad
> They knew themselves had many perfect days
> And many nights safe sheltered warm well-fed
> Whatever torments rumbled in their head.
> So let us mourn but also let us praise
> It was as brave to live as sad to die.
> Cheers for the one and for the other cry.
