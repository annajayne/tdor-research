On Tuesday night (05), a trans woman, identified only as Agatha, was found dead in her residence, located on Travessa Antônio Martins, in the Santo Antônio neighborhood, in Mossoró.

After the forensic examination was completed, the victim's body was removed to the Institute of Legal Medicine (IML) of the Scientific Police of Rio Grande do Norte for further examination.

The cause of death is still unknown.

The case will be monitored by public security forces.
