> This past weekend we had a Celebration of Life for @ariellesourcelove coming together soothed our tender hearts and it was incredible to see and hear how deeply and profoundly she touched so many lives - from those of us who knew her almost a decade to those who just met: Arielle was a light that uplifted us all. We will miss you our dear friend. Thank you to @queenofviolets13 for steering the ship and all those that contributed to putting together this gathering: it was beautiful.
