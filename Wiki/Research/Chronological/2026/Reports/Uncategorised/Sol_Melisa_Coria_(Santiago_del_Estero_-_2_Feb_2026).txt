> Shock in Las Termas: a well-known hairdresser from the Usina neighborhood was found dead
> The deceased is Sol Melisa Coria, a 43-year-old transgender woman. She was found at her home by her brother. An autopsy has been ordered to determine the cause of death.
> 
> Deep shock has gripped the city of Las Termas de Río Hondo following the discovery of the body of Sol Melisa Coria, a 43-year-old transgender woman, a well-known hairdresser, and a resident of the Usina neighborhood. The tragic news broke on the evening of Tuesday, February 3rd, and has profoundly impacted the community.
> 
> The incident occurred around 8:40 p.m. at a residence located on calle Islas Malvinas 392 , within the jurisdiction of Community Police Station No. 40. According to official information, it was her brother, Carlos Andrés Coria, 61, who alerted the authorities after finding her without vital signs inside the home.
> 
> According to his testimony, upon returning home he noticed a strong odor coming from inside . When he received no answer after knocking on the door and looking through a window, he decided to force entry, finding his sister lying on the bed. He also stated that the last time he saw her alive was on Sunday.
> 
> Police officers and forensic investigators worked at the scene, carrying out the necessary procedures and preserving the evidence. At the direction of the prosecuting attorney, Dr. Rafael Zanni , witnesses were allowed inside the room and an autopsy was ordered to definitively establish the cause of death.
> 
> Sol Melisa Coria was well-known and highly regarded in Las Termas for her career as a hairdresser , and her death has generated numerous expressions of grief and sorrow among neighbors, clients, and friends. The investigation is ongoing while awaiting the results of the forensic examination.




> The decomposing body of a young trans hairdresser was found
> She was found by her brother when he went to visit her and was not attended to, but he noticed a strong nauseating smell so he forced his way in.
> 04/02/202606:52 Police
> 
> A 61-year-old man, a resident of Las Termas de Río Hondo , found a macabre scene when he went to visit his sister and found her lifeless, lying in one of the rooms of her house in the Usina neighborhood.
> 
> The serious incident, which caused shock in the "Spa City", came to light around 8:30 p.m. when Carlos showed up at the victim's house —located on Islas Malvinas street— to visit her since he had not seen her in the last 48 hours.
> 
> According to police sources, when Carlos arrived at his sister's house —a well-known 43-year-old trans hairdresser— he knocked on the doors for several minutes, but received no response.
> 
> As he was about to leave, he smelled a strong, nauseating odor coming from the building, so he went to a window and, finding no trace of his sister, decided to force his way in.
> 
> Once inside, he found the victim lying on the bed, without vital signs. Carlos immediately notified the police, also informing them that the last time he saw her alive was on Sunday.
> 
> According to investigators, the body was in a state of decomposition, indicating that it had been without vital signs for more than 24 hours.
> 
> Police personnel worked at the scene alongside forensic experts, who carried out the necessary procedures and preserved the scene.
> 
> By order of the intervening prosecutor, Dr. Rafael Zanni, the entry with witnesses and the performance of the corresponding autopsy were ordered, with the objective of reliably establishing the cause of death.
