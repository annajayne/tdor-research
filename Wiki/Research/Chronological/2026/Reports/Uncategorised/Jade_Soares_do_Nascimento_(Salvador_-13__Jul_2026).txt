> Jade Soares, founder of the NGO Afrodite, dies at age 45.
> The activist received tributes from organizations and leaders connected to the defense of the LGBTQIAPN+ population.
> 
> By Editorial Staff
> 13/07/2026 15h03
>    
> Jade Soares, founder of the NGO Afrodite, dies at age 45.
> Jade Soares worked in defense of the rights of the LGBTQIAPN+ population in Alagoas. - Photo: Reproduction
> Organizations dedicated to defending LGBTQIAPN+ rights in Alagoas expressed their sorrow at the passing of activist Jade Soares do Nascimento, 45. Founder of the NGO Afrodite in Coqueiro Seco and a member of the State Council for Human Rights, she was honored by organizations that highlighted her work in promoting citizenship and human rights.
> 
> Jade Soares was found dead inside her home in the Santos Dumont neighborhood of Maceió. Her body has been released for burial.
> 
> The news caused a stir among members of the LGBTQIAPN+ movement and civil society organizations, who highlighted the activist's contribution to defending rights and strengthening spaces for representation in Alagoas.
> 
> In a statement of condolence, representatives of the movement highlighted Jade's dedication to social causes and her work with the LGBTQIAPN+ community.
> 
> "Today we received the sad news of the loss of our friend, partner, and founder of the NGO AFRODITE LGBTQIAPN+ in Coqueiro Seco. She was a friend who contributed greatly to the LGBTQIAPN+ movement. Today our movement is saddened by the news of her passing. We are in mourning for her death. Thank you and gratitude for all the teachings and learning. She will always be remembered in our hearts. She always fought and worked for the rights of the LGBTQIAPN+ movement. May God comfort the hearts of her friends and family. My sincere condolences. Go with God and rest in peace."
> 
> The Gay Group of Maceió (GGM) also published a statement of solidarity with the family, friends, and people close to the activist. In the statement, the organization highlighted the importance of Jade's trajectory and reinforced the need to expand public policies aimed at the LGBTQIA+ population.
> 
> "The Gay Group of Maceió (GGM) expresses its deepest sorrow for the passing of Jade Soares."
> 
> In this moment of immense sorrow, we express our solidarity with his family, his friends, and all those who are grieving this loss today.
> 
> Jade's passing deeply saddens us and reinforces the urgency of looking, with responsibility and sensitivity, at the reality experienced by the LGBTQIA+ population, especially trans and transvestite people, who daily face violence, discrimination, social exclusion, and countless barriers to accessing rights, protection, and comprehensive care.
> 
> As an organization that has been working for decades to defend the human rights and citizenship of the LGBTQIA+ population, we reaffirm our commitment to promoting life, acceptance, qualified listening, and strengthening public policies that guarantee dignity, respect, and access to health, including mental health.
> 
> No one should face their pain in silence. Collective care is also an act of resistance, and building support networks is a responsibility of the whole society.
> 
> Recognized for her social activism, Jade Soares leaves a legacy of mobilization, representation, and defense of human rights, being remembered by colleagues and organizations as one of the active voices in the fight for inclusion and citizenship in Alagoas.
