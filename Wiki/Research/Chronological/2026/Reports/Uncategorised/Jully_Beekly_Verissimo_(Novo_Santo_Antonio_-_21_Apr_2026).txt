> Transgender woman found unconscious and dies in hospital in Mato Grosso.
> Jully Beekly was 33 years old; the Civil Police in Novo Santo Antônio were notified and are investigating the case.
> 
> Jully Beekly Veríssimo was found unconscious and bleeding from her face.
> 
> A trans woman identified as Jully Beekly Veríssimo, 33, died this Tuesday (21) after being found unconscious and with injuries by her sister, in the municipality of Novo Santo Antônio (1,118 km from Cuiabá).
> 
> According to the Civil Police, Jully was taken by her sister to a health unit on Monday (20). She was unconscious, with injuries and bleeding in the facial area.
> 
> Despite receiving medical attention, the victim succumbed to their injuries and died on Tuesday. 
> 
> Given the circumstances, the Civil Police were called in and are investigating the case.
> 
> On social media, friends and family shared messages of affection and paid tribute.
> 
>  "Today our hearts are heavy. We lost our dear friend Jully, a person full of light, affection and love, who touched the lives of everyone around her," said a friend.
> 
> Considered a leading figure in the June festival dance groups in Mato Grosso, Jully was a dancer and active member of groups connected to the state's traditional festivities, which also paid tribute to her.
> 
>  "Her passing represents an irreparable loss for the entire June festival movement, especially for those who knew her and were aware of her character. Always so cheerful, smiling, and courageous," declared the Abalantes do Sertão profile.




> TRANS WOMAN DIES AFTER BEING FOUND UNCONSCIOUS IN MT
> Trans woman Jully Beekly Verissimo, 33, died after being found unconscious inside her home in the municipality of Novo Santo Antônio.
> 
> The case was registered by the Mato Grosso Military Police on Monday afternoon (20).
> 
> According to information, Jully was found by her own sister, fallen, unconscious, with injuries to her face and bleeding.
> 
> The victim was unable to report what happened, and so far there is no confirmation whether the injuries were caused by assault, a fall, or another situation.
> 
> She was rescued, transferred to the Regional Hospital of Água Boa, but did not survive.
> 
> The victim's cell phone was handed over to the Mato Grosso Civil Police, which is investigating the case.
> 
> The incident was initially registered as bodily injury, but should be reclassified as the investigations progress.
> 
> Jully was well known and active in June festival groups in the region. In 2024, she was part of the Junina Barnabé group.
> 
> In 2025, she was queen of the Bomjarraiá festival.
> 
> In a statement, Junina Barnabé lamented her death:
> 
> “Jhully touched everyone with her contagious joy and love for dance. Her presence illuminated our rehearsals and performances.”
> 
> Friends and family are paying tribute on social media.
> 
> The case is still under investigation.
