> A trans woman identified as Ellen Nascimento de Lima was found dead in a mangrove area near Complexo Joana Bezerra in Recife on Tuesday morning (18). According to witnesses, she was still alive when found in the water and was calling for help.
> 
> Reports indicate that people arriving at the site for work heard groans and spotted the victim in the mangrove area beneath the Joana Bezerra viaduct, near the Coque community. High tide made it difficult to reach her.
> 
> The victim was pulled from the water with the help of bystanders. Military police officers passing by were also alerted and called the Fire Department. However, by the time the rescue team arrived, Ellen had already died. Resuscitation efforts were attempted, but she did not survive.
> 
> The cause of death is currently being treated as drowning. There is no official information yet on how Ellen ended up in the water. According to acquaintances, she did not know how to swim and frequented the area.
> 
> Witnesses also reported that the location is used as a gathering spot for drug use, particularly in the late afternoon and at night. However, there is no confirmation that this played a role in her death.
> 
> Residents stated that Ellen was well-known in the community and described as a quiet, well-liked person. Reports also indicate that her brother had been murdered last month inside a home on Ilha Joana Bezerra.
> 
> The case is expected to be investigated by the Civil Police, who will look into the circumstances of the death and whether others were involved. The official cause of death has yet to be determined by the responsible authorities.
